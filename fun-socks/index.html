How to play

The (very short) rulebook

🧦 Match Pairs

  • Tap a sock to flip it, tap another to find its twin. Match all the pairs to clear the drawer.
  • Bigger drawers = more pairs = more glory. Stars: finish in fewer flips for more stars (3 is the dream).
  • Timer mode: beat the clock. Chill mode: no clock, just vibes.
  • Cozy gives you more time, Wild gives you less (and bragging rights).

🧺 Sort the Laundry

  • A sock pops up — tap the bin with the matching pattern before it piles up.
  • Timer mode: sort as many as you can before the clock runs out. Wrong bin costs you seconds!
  • Chill mode: a gentle pile of 20 socks, no clock. Mistakes are just… learning.

✦ The Golden Sock

  • Rumour has it a rare golden sock hides in some drawers. Match its pair for a big bonus. That's all we're saying.

🎓 Practice mode

  • Practice is just for fun — scores there never touch your stars or bests.
