How to play

1
Play Money

Play money only — no real money, no prizes.

2
New Card

Tap New card to start.

3
Scratch Cover

Drag finger or mouse over the shiny cover to scratch it off.

4
Match Three

Match 3 of a symbol anywhere to win its virtual coins.

5
Auto Reveal

Scratch enough and the rest reveals itself!

6
Card Cost

Easy 25, Normal 50, Hard 100 coins.

Rules

  • Play money only — no real money, no prizes. Coins have no cash value.
  • Each card costs coins (see Card cost). Match 3 symbols to win their prize.
  • Prizes: ★ 25 · ♥ 50 · ♦ 100 · ♣ 250 · ● 500 · ▲ 1000 coins.
  • Only one prize per card (the matching triple).
  • Best = your highest coin balance. Top up free any time.
  • Practice mode never saves to your best.
