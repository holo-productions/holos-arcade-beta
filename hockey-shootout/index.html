Rules

Hockey Shootout — how a match works

The Match

A shootout is best of 5 rounds. In every round you take one shot, then the Rival takes one shot. Whoever has more goals after 5 rounds wins. If one side can't be caught, the match ends early. If it's tied after 5, you go to sudden death: the first round where one side scores and the other doesn't decides it.

How to Shoot

  • Pick a zone: tap one of the 6 zones in the net — top left, top middle, top right, bottom left, bottom middle, or bottom right.
  • Time it: press Shoot! and the timing needle starts sweeping. Press Stop & Shoot to fire. Land in the green for a perfect shot, yellow for a wobbly one, red and you'll likely miss the net entirely.
  • Beat the goalie: the goalie dives to a zone. If he covers your zone, you're saved. On Normal and Hard he can also smother shots in neighbouring zones — and he studies your habits, so don't pick the same corner every time.

Practice Round

Flip the Mode switch above the rink to Practice any time. Practice is untimed and unscored: take as many shots as you like, the Rival never shoots back, and nothing counts toward your bests. Hints stay on — you'll get a suggested corner each shot, the timing needle runs at its slowest, and the goalie guesses at random instead of reading your habits. Switch back to Match when you're ready to play for real. Your mode choice is remembered for next time.

Difficulty

  • Easy: slow timing needle, wide green zone, goalie guesses at random and never covers neighbours. Rival scores less often. Great for learning.
  • Normal: medium needle, goalie sometimes reads your favourite corner and can cover a neighbouring zone on a wobbly shot.
  • Hard: fast needle, narrow green zone, goalie reads your tendencies often, covers neighbours, and the Rival is clinical.

Scoring & Bests

  • ● Green dot = goal   ● Yellow dot = saved   ● Red dot = missed the net
  • Your bests — most goals in a match, wins, games played, and win streak — are saved on this device and shown above the rink.
  • Your difficulty choice is remembered for next time.

Good Sportsmanship

All teams, players, and the goalie in this game are generic and original — no real leagues or players. It's just you, a puck, and a very determined cardboard-cutout of a goalie. (He says hi.)
