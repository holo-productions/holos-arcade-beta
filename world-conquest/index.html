📜 Rules — World Conquest

Goal

Own all 24 territories. Last commander standing wins.

Your turn — 3 phases

  1. Reinforce: You get troops = max(3, ⌊territories ÷ 3⌋) + continent bonuses (Verdania +3, Solmara +2, Kragoria +4, Lunaris +2 if you hold every territory in it). Tap your territories to place them.
  2. Attack: Tap one of your territories with 2+ troops, then tap a neighbouring enemy territory. Attacker rolls up to 3 dice (must leave 1 troop behind), defender up to 2. Highest dice are paired off — higher die wins, ties go to the defender. Loser of each pair loses 1 troop. Capture a territory at 0 troops and you move troops in.
  3. Fortify: Once per turn, move troops between two of your neighbouring territories (leaving at least 1 behind), then your turn ends.

Difficulty

Easy: rivals attack cautiously and pick softer targets. Normal: balanced. Hard: rivals attack more often, hunt your weakest borders, and fortify smartly.

Scoring

In Standard mode, a win saves your fastest win (fewest turns) on this device. Practice mode never saves a score and shows hints.
