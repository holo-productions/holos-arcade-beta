    How to play Ludo

    You (red) race Blaze, Nova and Tex around the board. First to get all 4 tokens home wins.

    1. Roll a 6 to move a token out of your yard onto your start square.
    2. Tap Roll dice, then tap a glowing token to move it that many squares.
    3. Land on a rival to send their token back to its yard — unless it's on a safe square (starts and ★ stars).
    4. Race up your home column (your colour, pointing at the centre). You need the exact roll to land in the middle.
    5. Bonus rolls: rolling a 6, capturing, or getting a token home earns another roll. Three 6s in a row ends your turn!

    Ludo — full rules

    The complete rulebook for this version.

    Goal

    Be the first player to move all 4 of your tokens from your yard, all the way around the board, up your home column and into the centre home triangle.

    Setup

    • 4 players: you play red; Blaze (green), Nova (yellow) and Tex (blue) are AI opponents. Red goes first, then play moves clockwise.
    • Every token starts in its coloured yard in the corner.

    Leaving the yard

    • A token can only leave the yard when you roll a 6. It is placed on your coloured start square.
    • If you have no tokens on the board and don't roll a 6, your turn passes.

    Moving

    • On your turn, roll the dice and move one token forward that many squares, clockwise around the track.
    • After a full lap, your tokens turn into your home column (the 5 squares of your colour leading to the centre).
    • Reaching the centre needs an exact roll. If the roll is too big for a token, that token simply can't move this turn.

    Capturing & safe squares

    • Land exactly on a square holding a rival token and it is captured — sent back to its yard.
    • Safe squares can't be captured on: the 4 coloured start squares and the 4 squares marked ★.
    • Two or more tokens of the same colour on one square form a block: they can't be captured, and rivals can't land on that square.
    • Your own tokens may share squares freely.

    Bonus rolls & three sixes

    • Roll a 6 → roll again.
    • Capture a rival → roll again.
    • Get a token into the centre → roll again.
    • But roll three 6s in a row and your turn ends immediately — the third 6 doesn't move anything.

    Winning

    • The first player to get all 4 tokens into the centre wins the game.
    • Your records (games, wins, captures, and your fastest win measured in dice rolls) are kept as your personal bests.

    Practice rounds

    • Use the Mode control at the top: Counted game (default) or 🧪 Practice.
    • A practice round uses the exact same rules and AI — it's a full warm-up game, not a demo.
    • Nothing from a practice round is added to your records: games, wins, captures and best-win rolls are all paused while Practice is on.
    • Switching between Counted and Practice starts a fresh round in the new mode.
    • Your last mode is remembered on this device, so if you left Practice on, the yellow banner at the top will remind you.

    AI opponents & difficulty

    • Easy: the AI plays loosely and misses danger.
    • Normal (default): the AI captures, uses safe squares and mostly watches its back.
    • Hard: the AI plays sharp — captures, blocks, safe squares and dodging danger whenever it can.
    • Set difficulty with the Difficulty control at the top of the board. Changing it applies from the AI's next decision — no need to restart.
    • Your difficulty choice is remembered on this device for your next game.
