Rules

Upper section

Ones to Sixes: score the sum of dice showing that face. If your upper total reaches 63 or more, you earn a 35 point bonus.

Lower section

Three of a Kind: at least 3 dice the same. Score the sum of all dice, else 0.

Four of a Kind: at least 4 dice the same. Score the sum of all dice, else 0.

Full House: 3 of one face and 2 of another. Score 25, else 0.

Small Straight: 4 dice in a row (for example 1-2-3-4). Score 30, else 0.

Large Straight: 5 dice in a row (1-2-3-4-5 or 2-3-4-5-6). Score 40, else 0.

Yahtzee: all 5 dice the same. Score 50, else 0.

Chance: score the sum of all dice, always counts.

Scoring 0 in a category is allowed and sometimes smart. Each category is used once. After 13 rounds each, highest grand total wins. Match wins are saved on this device.
