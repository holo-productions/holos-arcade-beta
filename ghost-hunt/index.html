👻 Meet the ghosts

  • Regular ghost — 10 points. Friendly, a bit wiggly.

⚡ Combos

Catch ghosts quickly one after another to build a combo. Every catch in a row raises your multiplier up to ×5 — points pile up fast. Miss your timing and the combo resets. In Practice mode there are no timers and no pressure: ghosts glow with hints so you can learn the ropes.

How to play

Rules 📜

The goal

Ghosts hide in the dark haunted house. Your flashlight is the only light — a ghost can only be caught while your beam is shining on it. Catch as many as you can before time runs out!

How to play

  • Move your mouse, or drag your finger, to aim the flashlight.
  • Tap / click a ghost while it's lit up to catch it.
  • Ghosts in the dark can't be caught — tapping in the dark does nothing (no penalties, promise).

Scoring

  • Regular ghost: 10 pts · Sleepy ghost: 15 pts · Shy ghost: 20 pts · Golden ghost: 50 pts
  • Quick back-to-back catches build a combo multiplier (×2, ×3, ×4, up to ×5) applied to every catch.
  • The combo resets if you go about 2.5 seconds without a catch.

No clock, no score saved, slower ghosts, and hint glows show you where ghosts are hiding. Perfect for learning — or for little hunters.

All ghosts here are friendly and love being caught — it's the highlight of their afterlife. Fun-spooky only, never scary-gory. 👻
