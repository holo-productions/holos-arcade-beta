📖 How to Play — Rules

🎯 Goal

Hit the coin goal before the café day timer runs out. Don't lose all 3 hearts.

☕ The loop

  • Guests arrive with an order (shown above their head).
  • Tap a menu station to brew, bake, or make that item. Each one takes a few seconds.
  • When it's done, it pops onto the Serving Counter automatically.
  • When the counter has everything in a guest's order, their SERVE button lights up. Tap it!

💰 Coins & tips

  • Sandwich $8 • Muffin $6 • Latte $5 • Coffee $4
  • Serve with patience bar above 70% = +50% tip. Above 40% = +25% tip.
  • Extra items stay on the counter for the next guest — brew and bake ahead during quiet moments!

💔 Patience

The bar under each guest drains over time. If it empties, they leave and you lose a heart. Lose 3 hearts and the café closes early.

⚙️ Difficulty

Easy / Normal / Hard changes day length, goal, how many guests you seat at once, order size, and patience speed. Your choice is saved for next time.

🎓 Practice Round

Tap Start Practice Round on the start screen for a no-pressure round: no timer, no coin goal, and no hearts to lose. Orders are just 1 item, guests are extra patient, and everything brews and bakes a bit faster. If a guest does wander off, it's no big deal — a new one takes a seat. End practice any time with End Practice. Practice coins are just for fun and never count toward your best scores.
