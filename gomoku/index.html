Rules

  • Board size is 13 by 13 squares.
  • Black always plays first in round 1. After that, the starting player swaps each round.
  • Players take turns placing one stone on an empty square.
  • First player to get 5 stones in a row wins the round. Rows can be horizontal, vertical, or diagonal.
  • A line of 6 or more also counts as a win.
  • If the board fills up with no winner, the round is a draw.
  • Scores are saved on this device across rounds. Restart Scores clears them back to zero.
  • In vs Computer mode, you are Black and the Computer is White.
