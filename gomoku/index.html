How to play

  1. Pick a mode at the top: 2 Players to share one phone, or vs Computer to play solo.
  2. Player 1 uses black stones. Player 2 or the Computer uses white stones.
  3. On your turn, tap any empty square to place your stone.
  4. In 2 Players mode, hand the phone over when the pass screen appears, then tap I am ready.
  5. Try to make a line of 5 of your stones in a row. Lines can go across, down, or diagonal.
  6. Block your opponent when they have 4 in a row. That stops them from winning next turn.
  7. When someone gets 5, those stones glow pink and the score updates. Tap New Round to play again.

New to the game? Start near the middle of the board. It gives you more directions to build a line.

Rules

  • Board size is 13 by 13 squares.
  • Black always plays first in round 1. After that, the starting player swaps each round.
  • Players take turns placing one stone on an empty square.
  • First player to get 5 stones in a row wins the round. Rows can be horizontal, vertical, or diagonal.
  • A line of 6 or more also counts as a win.
  • If the board fills up with no winner, the round is a draw.
  • Scores are saved on this device across rounds. Restart Scores clears them back to zero.
  • In vs Computer mode, you are Black and the Computer is White.
