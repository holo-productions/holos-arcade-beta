How to play

Thirty seconds and you'll be slithering like a pro.

1
Start game

Press Start game. After 3·2·1 your snake heads right — it never stops.

2
Steer

Arrow keys or WASD, swipe on the board, or use the arrow pad.

3
Eat apples

Eat red apples to score and grow one segment longer each time.

4
Golden apple

Appears after every 5th apple, gone in about 7 seconds. Grab it for bonus points.

5
Don’t crash

Avoid walls in Solid walls mode and never bite your own body.

6
Practice round

A touch slower, crashes won’t end it, and the score never touches your bests.

Handy keys

  • Space or P — pause / resume
  • R — restart
  • Esc — close a pop-up (the game auto-pauses while one is open)

Snake — full rules

Everything that scores, speeds up, or ends your run.

The board & your snake

  • The board is a 20 × 20 grid. Your snake starts 4 segments long, moving right, and moves continuously — there is no stopping or reversing.
  • You cannot turn 180°. A turn directly back into your own neck is ignored.
  • Up to two quick turns in a row are remembered, so fast zig-zags work if you time them.

Scoring

Red apple102030
Golden apple50100150
  • Every apple — red or golden — grows your snake by exactly 1 segment.
  • Changing difficulty mid-run is allowed, but the score multiplier for each apple uses the difficulty in effect when you eat it. Your best score is shared across all difficulties and edge modes.

Golden apples (special rule)

  • After every 5th red apple you eat, a golden apple appears on a random empty square.
  • It stays for about 7 seconds, then disappears. Only one can be on the board at a time.
  • Eating it does not count toward the next golden apple — only red apples advance that counter.

Speed

  • The snake speeds up a little after every 5 apples eaten: roughly 4% faster per speed level, up to a top speed for each difficulty.
  • Starting pace: Chill is the gentlest, Classic is the traditional arcade pace, Turbo starts fast and gets frantic.

Edges

  • Solid walls: touching any wall ends the run.
  • Wrap-around: you pass through a wall and come out the opposite side. Only your own body can end the run.

Practice rounds

  • Pick Practice round before you start. Practice runs a touch slower than the speed you picked.
  • Walls wrap you around to the other side and biting yourself doesn't end it — you just slither on through. It's for getting the hang of steering, not for sweating.
  • Practice scores, lengths, and apples don't count: no best score, no career totals. The end screen shows what you did, marked as practice.
  • Tap End practice (in the yellow banner or the pause screen) to wrap up early. Switching back to For real mid-run scraps that run without saving it.

How a run ends & winning

  • A run ends when your head hits a wall (Solid walls mode) or any part of your own body.
  • There is no final level: Snake is a high-score game. You "win" a run by beating your saved best score.
  • Perfect game: if your snake ever fills all 400 squares, the run ends as a win, whatever the score.
  • Pausing, opening How to play or Rules, or switching tabs pauses the clock — a golden apple's 7 seconds only tick down while you play.
