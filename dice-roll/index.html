How to play Dice Roll

Fill all 13 boxes on your scorecard, one per turn. Beat Nova's total to win.

Your turn, step by step

1
Roll dice

Tap Roll dice. You get up to 3 rolls per turn.

2
Hold dice

Tap a die to hold it gold. Tap again to release.

3
Score a box

Tap a score box to lock it in for the game.

4
Chase bonus

Score 63+ up top for a +35 bonus.

The boxes

  • Ones – Sixes (upper section): add up only the dice showing that number. Score 63+ across the upper section for a +35 bonus.
  • Three / Four of a Kind: add up all five dice if you have 3 / 4 the same.
  • Full House (25): three of one number + a pair of another.
  • Small Straight (30): any run of four, like 2-3-4-5. Large Straight (40): a run of five.
  • Five of a Kind (50): all five dice the same — the big one!
  • Chance: add up all five dice, no combo needed. A good home for a roll that fits nowhere else.

Tips

  • Stuck with a bad roll? Score a 0 in a box you were unlikely to fill anyway — Five of a Kind is the usual sacrifice.
  • Chase the upper bonus early: three 6s (18) beats chasing a straight most turns.
  • Pick your match: set Difficulty above the table — Easy while you learn, Normal for the classic game, Hard when you want a real test. You can change it mid-game; it applies from Nova's next turn.
  • Practice round: tap Start practice above the table for a full game with a coach hint under the dice on every turn — what to hold, and your best box right now. No timer, and practice games never change your Best score, Games, Wins, or Five of a Kind records.
  • Nova plays a solid, greedy game — she'll grab straights and full houses when she sees them.

Dice Roll — Full rules

Goal & win condition

The game lasts 13 rounds. Each round, you and Nova each take one turn and must fill exactly one score box. When all 13 boxes on both cards are filled, the higher total wins. Equal totals are a tie. There is no elimination and no way to lose early — every turn must end by scoring, even if that score is 0.

Turn rules

  • Each turn allows up to 3 rolls of five dice. The first roll rolls all five.
  • Between rolls you may hold any number of dice and re-roll the rest. Held dice can be released before the next roll.
  • You may score after the 1st, 2nd, or 3rd roll. Scoring ends the turn immediately.
  • Each box can be used once only. A box scored at 0 is spent, just like any other score.

Scoring table

  • Ones / Twos / Threes / Fours / Fives / Sixes: sum of the dice showing that face only.
  • Upper bonus: if your six upper boxes total 63 or more, add +35. (63 is exactly three of each face.)
  • Three of a Kind: at least three dice the same → sum of all five dice, otherwise 0.
  • Four of a Kind: at least four dice the same → sum of all five dice, otherwise 0.
  • Full House: exactly three of one face plus a pair of another → 25. Five of a Kind also scores 25 here.
  • Small Straight: any run of four sequential dice (1-2-3-4, 2-3-4-5, or 3-4-5-6) → 30. Duplicate dice don't break a run.
  • Large Straight: a run of five (1-2-3-4-5 or 2-3-4-5-6) → 40.
  • Five of a Kind: all five dice the same → 50.
  • Chance: sum of all five dice, no combination required.

Special rules in this version

  • No joker / bonus Five of a Kind rules: rolling a second Five of a Kind scores normally in any open box (50 in Five of a Kind only if that box is still open; otherwise it counts as its dice in other boxes, e.g. 25 as a Full House or the face total in the upper section). There is no +100 bonus.
  • Forced scoring: if no box fits your roll, you must still choose a box and take 0 — you cannot pass.
  • Nova (AI): plays by the same one-box-per-turn rules and picks her box when her rolls run out. How sharply she plays depends on the difficulty setting.
  • Difficulty (set above the table; change it any time — it applies from Nova's next turn; your records are shared across all three):
    • Easy: Nova takes only 2 rolls per turn, holds only toward her most common number, and sometimes scores a box that isn't her best.
    • Normal: Nova takes all 3 rolls, holds toward pairs and straights, and picks a strong box with light strategy.
    • Hard: Nova takes all 3 rolls but banks a premium hand early (a straight, full house, four of a kind, or four of a face) instead of risking it; she chases the +35 upper bonus, protects Chance and Five of a Kind, holds her two highest dice when nothing is paired, and sacrifices her least valuable box when forced to take 0.
  • Practice round (toggle above the table): a full 13-round game against Nova with coach hints and no timer. Practice results are not saved — they never change your records below.
  • Records: best score, games, wins, and Five of a Kind count are kept on this device only.
