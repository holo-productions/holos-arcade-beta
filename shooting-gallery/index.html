📖 Rules - Shooting Gallery

🎯 Goal

Score as many points as you can before the timer runs out by tapping or clicking moving targets on three shelves. When time is up, your final score is compared with your best for that difficulty.

🕹️ How to play

  • Tap or click directly on a target to pop it. The crosshair follows your mouse or finger.
  • Targets roll left to right and right to left on three shelves at different speeds. If a target rolls off the shelf, it is gone with no penalty.
  • There is a short pause between shots, so steady taps beat frantic mashing.
  • Use Pause any time. Use End round to finish early and see your summary.

How to play

A quick tour, then step right up:

1
Pick Difficulty

Easy: more time, slower targets, no miss cost. Hard: fast with a steep miss cost.

2
Watch Shelves

Targets roll on three shelves both ways; the top is quick, the bottom is calmer.

3
Tap to Pop

Tap a target: ducks 10, balloons 15, stars 25, bullseyes 50, golden duck 100.

4
Mind Misses

Misses cost 5 on Normal and 10 on Hard; Easy and Practice are free.

5
Pro Tip

Pick one shelf, settle your rhythm, save fast taps for bullseyes and golden ducks.
