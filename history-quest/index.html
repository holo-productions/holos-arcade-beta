Rules

  • Pick a grade band, then pick a quest.
  • One try per question in the quests — after you answer, you see the correct answer and a fact.
  • Practice Rounds are different: try as many times as you need, and no stars are earned — they're just for warming up.
  • Timeline rounds: tap all 3 events in order before checking.
  • 1 star per correct answer + 1 bonus star for finishing.
  • No timers, no losing, no lives. Play at your own pace.
  • Be kind to fellow time-travellers — this is a solo learning game with no chat.

About the history: Questions cover world and Canadian history. Dates use the years events are most commonly taught in Canadian classrooms. Some topics — like residential schools — are included in the older bands because learning this history matters; they are written respectfully and factually.
