How to play

Every round, the letters of a real word get shuffled into tiles. Your job: put them back in the right order.

New — Practice round: Pick Practice on the setup screen for an untimed warm-up. No clock, hints are free, wrong guesses cost nothing, and scores don’t save — just learn at your own pace and end whenever you like.

PLANET

These six tiles spell PLANET — once you spot it, build it:

1
Build your answer

Tap tiles in order, or type the letters on your keyboard.

2
Fix a mistake

Press Backspace or tap Clear to pull letters back.

3
Shuffle tiles

Re-jumble the leftovers — a new arrangement can make the word jump out.

4
Check it

Fill every slot, then hit Check answer. Right scores; wrong costs 5 points.

5
Stuck?

Hint shows the clue, then the first and last letters. Skip moves on with no points.

6
Beat the clock

Your difficulty sets the clock — per word in Classic, one clock in Blitz.

That's it — no account, no downloads. Your best scores save on this device automatically.

Rules

The full rulebook for Word Scramble — scoring, hints, timers, and how a game is won.

The basics

  • Each round shows the letters of one word, shuffled. The scramble is never the word itself and never a different valid spelling of it.
  • Build the word by tapping tiles or typing. Every letter must be used exactly once — the answer always uses all the tiles.
  • Words come from the category and difficulty you picked. Easy = 4–5 letters, Normal = 6–7, Hard = 8+, Mixed = any length on Normal timing.
  • Guesses aren't case-sensitive, and you can re-arrange freely until you press Check answer.

Difficulty — the arcade standard

Every game in the arcade offers Easy / Normal / Hard. In Word Scramble, difficulty changes four things at once: word length, the clock, what hints and mistakes cost, and your score multiplier.

  • Mixed lengths plays any word length (4+) on Normal timing, costs, and scoring.
  • On Hard, the ×1.5 multiplier applies to the whole solve — base points + speed bonus + streak bonus — rounded to the nearest point.
  • Difficulty is picked before you start and can't change mid-game. Your best scores don't separate by difficulty, so a Hard run is the way to chase a bigger best.

Modes & win conditions

  • Classic: 10 words, timed per word by difficulty (60 / 45 / 35 seconds). The game ends after the 10th word (solved, skipped, or timed out). There is no way to "lose" — your final score sets your grade: 900+ Word Master · 700+ Wordsmith · 500+ Scrambler · 300+ Warming Up · under 300 Keep Practising.
  • Blitz: One clock for the whole game — 75 / 60 / 50 seconds by difficulty. Each solve adds +2 seconds. The game ends when the clock hits zero; your grade uses the same scale.
  • Practice: Untimed and endless — no clock at all. Hints are free, wrong guesses cost 0, skips cost 0, and the score is not saved to your bests. Words use the category + difficulty you picked (so Practice on Easy is shorter words, Hard is longer). End practice any time with End practice; you’ll see how many you solved and your first-try rate.
  • A word that times out counts as skipped: no points, and your streak resets. (Practice words never time out.)

Correct word10 × word length

  • Scores never drop below 0.
  • Hints are per-word: each new word resets you to no hints used. You can't take Hint 2 before Hint 1.
  • Revealed letters (Hint 2) are shown under the answer slots — they aren't placed for you; you still have to build the word.
  • In Blitz there is no speed bonus (the clock is the bonus — solves add time instead).

Best scores

  • Your best Classic score, best Blitz score, and best streak are saved on this device and shown on the setup screen.
  • A best only updates when you finish a game (ending early still counts, with the score you had).
