📜 Empire Builder — Rules

Everything a ruler needs to know.

Goal

  • The campaign lasts 30 seasons (turns). Each year has Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter.
  • Win by being the first kingdom to reach the prosperity target (Easy 600 · Normal 750 · Hard 900), or by capturing both rival capitals.
  • If turn 30 ends with nobody at the target, the highest prosperity wins — ties go to you.
  • Lose if a rival reaches the target first, or if your capital falls to raiders or a rival army.

Each season

  • Press End Season: your provinces and buildings produce resources, your people and troops eat food, a random event may occur, and both rival kingdoms take their turn.
  • Your capital's base land & labour also adds +2 gold and +2 wood every season. Food eaten each season = 1 per troop + 1 per 8 population — the food figure in the resource bar is net of that upkeep, and the line under the resource bar shows the full breakdown.
  • Farms produce less in Winter. If food runs out, people leave and troops desert — keep a surplus.
  • You may build, train, claim and attack as many times as you can afford before ending the season.

The map

  • 12 provinces. Neutral provinces show a garrison: your army must be at least that large to claim one, and claiming costs gold and wood.
  • Every province you own adds its income each season and +50 prosperity.
  • Attacking a rival province: your army fights their defending force. Win and the province is yours; lose and you lose troops. Rival capitals are heavily defended — bring a big army and expect losses either way.

Events

  • Harvests, storms, traders, raiders, festivals and more arrive most seasons. Each offers simple choices — there is rarely a free lunch.
  • Raiders test your defences: troops + walls. If your capital's defence is overwhelmed, the capital falls and the campaign ends.
  • On Hard, bad events hit harder and rivals grow faster. On Easy, the land is kinder and rivals slower.

Prosperity (score)

  • Prosperity = a share of your treasury and stores, +20 per building, +50 per province, +3 per troop, +15 per Temple.
  • In Practice mode an advisor offers hints and your score is never saved. Your best real score is kept on this device.
