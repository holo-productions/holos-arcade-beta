Rules

The game

  • A first-person carnival shooting range. You have 60 seconds to pop as many targets as you can.
  • All targets are cartoon carnival fun — ducks, balloons, stars and bullseyes. No gore, no blood, no real weapons.

Points

  • Duck — 10 points. Balloon — 20 points. Star — 30 points. Bullseye — 50 points.
  • Bullseyes are rare and vanish fast. Stars twinkle in place, balloons float up, ducks drift side to side.
  • A shot only counts if the crosshair is on a target — the nearest target in the middle of your view is the one you hit.

Looking & shooting

  • Click the range to lock the mouse and look around (ESC unlocks and pauses). Without mouse lock, drag on the range to look.
  • Arrow keys turn and tilt the view; Space fires; P pauses.
  • On touch, drag to look and tap the big FIRE button to shoot. A controller works too: stick / D-pad to look, A to fire, Start to pause.

Difficulty

  • Easy: targets pop up slowly and linger.
  • Medium: a fair pace of spawns and movement.
  • Hard: fast spawns, quick targets, short windows.
  • You can change difficulty between rounds — it takes effect on the next round. Your choice is remembered for next time.

Practice round

  • Tap 🎯 Practice round to switch to a practice session at any time.
  • No score is kept and the clock doesn't run — pop targets for as long as you like.
  • Practice never counts toward your bests. Tap the button again to return to a 60-second round.

Bests

  • Best score, rounds played, best accuracy and total hits are stored on this device only.
