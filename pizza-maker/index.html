    📜 Pizza Maker rules

    The job

    Each ticket is a customer's order: a sauce, a number of cheese handfuls, and toppings with exact counts. Build the pizza to match, bake it, and serve it before the customer's patience (the green bar) runs out. If patience hits zero, that's a strike — three strikes ends your shift. In Practice mode there are no strikes.
