    Rules & How Scoring Works

    🎯 Goal

    Answer 10 vocabulary questions. Each question has 4 choices — tap the best one. You get feedback and a short explanation right away.

    ⭐ Stars

    Exam Quiz mode: 9–10 correct = 3 stars, 7–8 = 2 stars, 5–6 = 1 star. Stars are saved on this device only and add up per grade band. Practice mode does not award stars — it's for learning with hints.

    🌱 Practice vs Exam

    Practice is untimed with a Hint button on every question. Exam Quiz has no hints, so your stars show what you know on your own — just like test day, but friendly.

    🔒 Safe by design

    No chat, no accounts, no personal information, no ads, and no external links. Progress (stars) stays in this browser on this device.

    💬 Encouragement

    Missing a question is part of learning. Every answer — right or not quite yet — comes with an explanation and a word to review at the end.
