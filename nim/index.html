Rules

  • Starting piles are 3, 5, and 7 tokens.
  • Players take turns. On each turn, pick exactly one pile and remove 1 or more tokens from it.
  • You cannot pass, and you cannot take from two piles in one turn.
  • Normal play: the player who takes the last token wins the round.
  • Misere play (when ticked): the player who takes the last token loses the round, so the other player wins.
  • Changing the Misere tick or the mode starts a fresh round.
  • Scores are saved on this device across rounds. Restart Scores clears them back to zero.
  • Easy Computer sometimes makes a random move. Hard Computer always plays the best move it can find.
