Flip cards, find pairs, trust your brain.

  2. 2

Memory Match — Rules

The full rulebook: scoring, turns, and win conditions.

The basics

All cards start face down in a shuffled grid. On a turn, a player flips exactly two cards. If the pictures match, the pair is collected and stays face up. If they don't match, both cards flip back face down after a short pause — every player can see them while they're up, so remembering positions is the whole game.

Solo mode

  • There are no turns and no opponent — just you against the board.
  • Scoring: the clock starts on your first flip. Your score is your finishing time and your total flips (every single card flip counts, so a pair attempt costs 2).
  • Win condition: find every pair to clear the board.
  • Records: your best time and fewest flips are saved separately for each board size (Small 6 pairs, Classic 8 pairs, Big 15 pairs). Card theme is just for looks and never affects scoring.

Practice round 🎯

  • Practice is a warm-up — it doesn't count. No best time, no best flips, and no win/loss record is saved.
  • Every practice round starts with a peek: all cards are shown face up for a few seconds so you can memorize where they are, then they flip face down and you play as normal.
  • Tap 👀 Peek at cards anytime during practice to see all unmatched cards again for 3 seconds. There's no limit and no penalty — peek as often as you like.
  • Misses stay face up a little longer in practice (1.4 seconds) so you have more time to take them in.
  • Board size, theme, and difficulty all work in practice, so you can rehearse the exact setup you want to try for real in Solo or Vs AI.

Vs AI mode

  • You and Nova alternate turns. A match scores that pair for the player who found it and earns the same player another turn — a good memory can collect several pairs in a row. A miss passes the turn to the other player.
  • Scoring: each collected pair is worth 1 point. Pairs you find are outlined in green; Nova's are outlined in orange.
  • Win condition: when the board is empty, the player with the most pairs wins. Equal pairs is a tie.
  • Your win / loss / tie record against Nova is saved.

Difficulty — Easy / Normal / Hard

  • Every game uses the same three settings: Easy, Normal, and Hard. You can change difficulty at any time — it applies from that moment on and never reshuffles the board.
  • Solo: difficulty sets how long mismatched cards stay face up before flipping back — Easy gives you 1.6 seconds to memorize them, Normal 0.9 seconds, Hard just 0.5 seconds.
  • Vs AI: the same flip-back pace applies to both players, and difficulty also sets Nova's memory (next section).
  • Difficulty never changes scoring — Solo records are still kept per board size, and a Vs AI pair is still worth 1 point.

Nova's memory

  • Nova can only use cards she has actually seen flipped — she never peeks at face-down cards.
  • Easy — Forgetful: Nova forgets most of what she's seen almost immediately.
  • Normal — Sharp: Nova remembers most seen cards, but still loses track of some.
  • Hard — Photographic: Nova remembers every card she has ever seen, perfectly.

Special rules

  • You can't flip a card that's already face up or already matched, and the board locks briefly while a pair is checked.
  • Switching board size or card theme starts a fresh shuffled game. Switching Solo ↔ Vs AI also starts fresh.
  • There's no flip limit and no penalty for misses in either mode — in Solo they simply cost you time and flips.
