📖 Rules - Snowboard Slalom

🎯 Goal

Steer your snowboarder down the course and pass between every pair of gate poles. Reach the finish line with as many clean gates and as fast a time as you can.

🕹️ How to ride

  • Hold Steer left or Steer right, drag on the course, or use the left and right arrow keys.
  • Each gate is a pair of poles. Pass between the poles to clear it clean.
  • Speed rises the farther you travel, so gates arrive quicker near the finish.
  • Missing a gate costs points and briefly slows you down.
  • The race ends at the finish line, or any time with End round.
