How to play Gin Rummy

Goal: meld your cards into sets and runs, keep deadwood (unmatched cards) low, and be first to 100 points.

New to the game? Switch to Practice round above the table. Practice hands don't count toward your match score or bests — play one hand at a time, try knocking, and learn at your own pace. Switch back to Match play whenever you're ready.

On your turn

  • Draw from the stock or take the top discard.
  • Tap one of your cards to select it, then Discard it — or Knock with it if your remaining deadwood is 10 or less.

Scoring

  • Knock: score the difference in deadwood — unless the defender ties or beats you (after laying off on your melds), which is an undercut: they score the difference + 10.
  • Gin (zero deadwood): their whole deadwood + 25.
  • Big Gin (all 11 cards melded): their whole deadwood + 31.

For scoring details, laying off, undercuts, and Big Gin, tap Rules in the header — that's the full rulebook.

Gin Rummy — full rules

The goal

Each hand, gather your 10 cards into melds — sets of 3–4 cards of the same rank (7♠ 7♥ 7♦) or runs of 3+ cards in the same suit (4♣ 5♣ 6♣). Cards left over are deadwood, and they count against you: Ace = 1, 2–10 = face value, J/Q/K = 10. Win hands to score points; first to 100 wins the match.

Your turn

  • Draw the top card of the stock pile, or take the face-up top card of the discard pile.
  • Discard one card face-up onto the discard pile. You can't discard the card you just took from the discard pile on that same turn.
  • The first card of the hand (the upcard) is offered to the non-dealer first, then the dealer. If both pass, the non-dealer draws normally.
  • The dealer alternates each hand. If the stock runs down to two cards with no knock, the hand is a draw — nobody scores and the same dealer deals again.

Ending a hand

  • Knock — if, after your discard, your deadwood totals 10 or less, you may knock instead of discarding normally. The hand ends and both hands are scored.
  • Gin — knock with zero deadwood. You score the opponent's full deadwood plus a 25-point bonus.
  • Big Gin — if all 11 cards (before discarding) form melds, declare Big Gin: opponent's deadwood plus a 31-point bonus, no discard needed.
  • Laying off — after a regular knock (not gin), the defender may lay off deadwood cards that extend the knocker's melds, lowering their deadwood before scoring. There is no laying off against gin or Big Gin.
  • Undercut — if the defender's deadwood (after laying off) is equal to or lower than the knocker's, the defender steals the hand: they score the difference plus a 10-point bonus.
  • Otherwise the knocker scores the difference between the two deadwood totals.

Win condition

Hands keep dealing until one player reaches 100 points at the end of a hand — that player wins the match. If both cross 100 on the same hand, the higher total wins.

Play money only: this game is scored in points. There is no real-money wagering, no prizes, and no side betting — just points and bragging rights.

Quick tips

  • Watch the gold dots on your cards — those are deadwood in Melds sort. Aim to shrink them.
  • High deadwood (queens, kings) is expensive — ditch it early unless it's one card from a meld.
  • Watch what Nova picks up from the discard pile. On Hard, she is watching you back.
