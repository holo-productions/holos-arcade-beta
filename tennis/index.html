⚡ Quick Tips

Stay near the middle after every shot — most balls come back cross-court.

  • Hit with the edge of your racket to send the ball wide.
  • On Hard, the Bot reads your position — mix up your angles.
  • Long rallies wear the Bot down: its errors creep up after 12+ shots on Easy & Normal.

Controls recap

  • ← → / A D — move
  • Space — serve / start rally
  • P — pause  •  Mouse / touch drag — move directly
  • On phones, hold the ◀ ▶ buttons under the court if dragging feels awkward.

Know your opponent

  • Easy: slower Bot, bigger mistakes — learn the angles here.
  • Normal: a fair club-level rally. Good default.
  • Hard: fast feet, tiny error margin, quicker ball. Bring your wide shots.

Your difficulty choice is remembered for next time.

Rules

Scoring

  • Points: 0, 15, 30, 40, then Game — you must win by 2 points.
  • Deuce: at 40–40, the next point wins Advantage (AD). Win again for the game; lose it and you're back to Deuce.
  • Games & sets: first to 6 games wins the set, by 2 games (e.g. 6–4). At 6–6, a tiebreak decides it 7–6.
  • Tiebreak: first to 7 points, win by 2. Points count 1, 2, 3… in the tiebreak.
  • Match: best of 3 sets — first to 2 sets wins.

Serving

  • Serve alternates every game. The server is marked with the green dot and tennis-ball chip.
  • When it's your serve, position yourself, then press Serve / Space. Rally Bot serves automatically after a short pause.

Winning a point

  • Your opponent misses the ball past their baseline — point to you.
  • You miss it past yours — point to Rally Bot.
  • Side walls are the doubles sidelines: balls bounce in play off them in this arcade version, so rallies keep flowing.

Practice rounds

  • Practice mode (Mode toggle at the top, or 🎯 Practice under the court) has no points, games, sets or timer — rallies just keep coming so you can learn the controls.
  • The Bot plays gentler in practice and coaching hints rotate under the court. Practice stats are not saved to Bests.
  • Switch back to 🏆 Match at any time to start a fresh scored match.

Difficulty & records

  • Easy / Normal / Hard changes Bot speed, ball pace, and Bot error rate. Your pick is saved on this device.
  • Bests (matches won, longest rally, sets won, winners) are saved on this device and shown on the game screen.
