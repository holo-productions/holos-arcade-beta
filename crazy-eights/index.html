How to play Crazy Eights

Goal: Be the first to play all your cards each round. You score points for the cards left in everyone else's hands. First to 200 points wins the match.

On your turn

  • Play a card that matches the top card's suit or rank — e.g. on a 7 you can play any heart or any 7.
  • 8s are wild. Play an 8 on anything, then choose the new suit everyone must follow.
  • No matching card? Tap the draw pile to draw one card. If it can be played, you may play it — otherwise your turn passes.
  • Down to one card? No penalty here — just enjoy the suspense.

Want a warm-up first?

Tap Practice round for one relaxed round with coaching tips on your turn. Nothing counts — no match points and no change to your records — so it's the perfect place to try out saving an 8 or picking a suit.

That's everything you need to start. For the full scoring table, blocked rounds, and difficulty details, see the Rules.

Crazy Eights — full rules

Objective & winning the match

  • Win rounds by being the first player to empty your hand.
  • The round winner scores points equal to the value of every card left in the other players' hands (see scoring below).
  • The first player to reach 200 points wins the match. Only the round winner gains points, so only they can cross 200 in a given round.

Setup

  • Standard 52-card deck. Every player is dealt 5 cards.
  • One card is flipped face-up to start the discard pile. The starting card is never an 8 — if one comes up, it's shuffled back into the deck and a new card is flipped.
  • The starting player rotates each round: round 1 starts with you, round 2 with the next player, and so on.
  • You play against 2 or 3 AI opponents (your choice in the menu above the table).

Taking a turn

  • Play exactly one card per turn. It must match the top card of the discard pile by suit or by rank — or follow the active suit chosen after an 8.
  • If you have no playable card, tap the draw pile to draw one card.
  • If the drawn card can be played, you may play it immediately — or pass your turn and keep it. If it can't be played, your turn passes automatically.
  • You may not draw while you already hold a playable card.
  • When the draw pile runs out, the discard pile (except its top card) is shuffled and becomes the new draw pile.

Special rules — the 8s

  • 8s are wild: an 8 can be played on any card, regardless of suit or rank.
  • Whoever plays an 8 chooses the new active suit. The next player must follow that suit, match the 8's rank (play another 8), or play an 8 of their own.
  • An 8 left in your hand at round end is the most expensive card in the game — worth 50 points to the round winner. Don't get caught holding one.
  • There is no penalty or call-out for holding one card — no "last card" rule in this version.

Blocked rounds

  • If the draw pile is empty and every player passes in a row, the round is blocked.
  • The player holding the lowest-value hand (by the scoring table below) wins the blocked round and scores the value of all cards left in the other players' hands.

Difficulty

  • Pick Easy, Normal, or Hard in the menu above the table. The line under the menu always shows what the current level does.
  • Changing difficulty (like changing the number of opponents) starts a fresh match, so a whole match is always played at one level.
  • Easy — the AI plays a random legal card, sometimes wastes its 8s early, picks a random suit after an 8, and occasionally fails to spot a playable card it just drew.
  • Normal — the AI sheds its highest-value non-8 cards first, saves 8s for when it has no other play, and names the suit it holds the most of.
  • Hard — everything Normal does, plus it remembers which suits players get stuck on and steers the active suit toward them.

Practice round

  • Tap Practice round above the table to play a single practice round instead of a full match to 200 points.
  • Practice uses the same cards, turns, and difficulty you have selected, plus extra coaching tips on your turn suggesting a strong card to play and why.
  • Practice points are shown for that round only. They do not count toward a match, and practice rounds do not change your matches, rounds won, best round, or streak records below the table.
  • Tap Restart practice for another practice round, or Exit to real match in the practice banner to start a scored match.

Play money only

  • Points in this game are play money. There is no real-money wagering, no prizes, no buying or cashing out points, and no side bets — just bragging rights.

Your records

  • Your matches played, matches won, rounds won, best round score, and win streaks are kept in this browser only, under the table. They reset if you clear your browser data.
