Connect Four — full rules

Objective

Be the first player to connect four of your discs in an unbroken line — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally — on the 7-column × 6-row grid.

Setup

  • Red always plays the first colour slot; Yellow plays second. In Vs Computer you are Red unless “First move” says otherwise.
  • “First move” decides who drops first: You/Red, Computer/Yellow, or Alternate (the starter swaps every game).
  • Each player has an unlimited supply of discs — you never run out.

Playing a turn

  • On your turn, choose one of the 7 columns. Your disc drops to the lowest empty slot in that column — discs always stack from the bottom.
  • A column with all 6 slots filled is closed; you cannot play there.
  • Turns strictly alternate. There is no passing and no timer.

Winning & draws

  • The game ends the instant a player completes a line of four — even if that drop also fills the last slot on the board.
  • Lines of five or more contain a line of four and also win.
  • If all 42 slots fill with no line of four, the game is a draw.
  • The winning four discs are highlighted on the board.

Scoring & bests

  • The score chips count wins and draws for this session and reset when you switch mode.
  • Bests (saved on this device) track games against the computer only: best win streak, fastest win measured in your own moves, total games, and win rate. Draws break a win streak.
  • 2-player pass-and-play games don't affect your bests.
  • Practice rounds don't count at all — no score chips, no bests. They're just for learning.
  • “Reset bests” clears the saved record; it can't be undone.

Special rules in this version

  • Undo: in Vs Computer, Undo takes back the last full round (the computer's reply and your move), so it's always your turn again. In 2 Players it takes back one move. Undoing a finished game also removes it from the score and bests.
  • Difficulty: Easy looks 2 moves ahead and sometimes plays a friendly random move; Medium looks 5 ahead; Hard looks 7 ahead and picks the strongest line it can find.
  • Practice mode: a third mode against the computer. Every turn it highlights a suggested column above the board and explains the pick — a win, a block, or the strongest build it can find. Nothing in a practice round is scored.
  • Rematch starts a fresh board with the same mode, difficulty, and first-move setting.
  • No chat, no accounts, no real-money play — just the board.
