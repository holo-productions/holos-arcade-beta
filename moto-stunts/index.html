How to play

You're a stunt rider on an endless dirt course full of ramps and gaps. Your job: go far, fly big, land clean.

1
Build speed

Hold Gas — you need pace to clear the gaps.

2
Launch ramps

Rolling up a ramp launches you into the air.

3
Lean in air

Lean back and forward to rotate the bike.

4
Land clean

Match your wheels to the landing slope.

5
Flip bonus

Full 360° in one jump pays — if you land it.

6
Feather taps

Small lean taps beat holding the keys down.

7
Start on Easy

Try Easy first — kinder gaps, softer landings.

Rules

How scoring works

Distance travelled1 pt / metre
Clean landing50 pts × combo
Perfect landing (near-exact angle)100 pts × combo
Backflip or frontflip (landed)150 pts each
Big air (2+ seconds airborne)25 pts / second
Rough landingNo bonus, combo resets

Combos

Each clean landing in a row raises your combo multiplier, up to ×5. A rough landing or a long slow crawl resets it to ×1. Flips only pay out if you land the jump — a flip into a wipeout scores nothing.

Wipeouts

  • Landing with your bike tilted too far from the slope angle.
  • Falling into a gap.
  • Landing upside down. (Classic.)

Difficulty

  • Easy — narrower gaps, slower top speed, generous landing angles.
  • Normal — the intended stunt-show experience.
  • Hard — wider gaps, faster bike, landings must be precise.

Your difficulty choice and best score for each difficulty are saved on this device. Stages get a little meaner every 500 m: gaps widen as you go.

Practice mode

Practice is for experimenting — wild flips, dumb ideas, the works. Practice scores are never saved to your bests, and the run is marked with a badge so there's no confusion.

Fair play

Just you, a bike, and gravity. No real-money gambling, no prizes — only bragging rights.
