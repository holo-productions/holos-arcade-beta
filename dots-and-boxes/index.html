How to play

  1. Tap a line between two dots to claim that edge.
  2. Take turns with the other player. Pass the phone after your turn in 2 Players mode.
  3. If your line completes a box, that box is yours and you get another turn.
  4. Your boxes fill with your colour and your initial.
  5. When all 9 boxes are claimed, the player with the most boxes wins the round.

Rules

  • The board has 4 dots across and 4 dots down, making 9 boxes.
  • Each turn you claim one empty edge.
  • Completing a box gives you an extra turn right away.
  • You cannot claim an edge that is already taken.
  • Most boxes at the end wins. Equal boxes is a draw.
  • Restart clears the board and round scores. New Round keeps round scores.
