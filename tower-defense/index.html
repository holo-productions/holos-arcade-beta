How to play — Tutorial

This tutorial is optional — close it any time and play straight away. Open it again whenever you like with the How to play button. For scoring, special rules and win conditions, see the separate Rules.

Goal: stop enemies walking the tan road from reaching the right-hand base. If your lives hit 0, the game ends.

Difficulty — pick first

  • Easy: 300 starting gold, 30 lives, enemies have 30% less health and move a little slower. Score ×0.7. Best for learning.
  • Normal: 200 starting gold, 20 lives, standard enemy health and speed. Score ×1. The way the game was balanced.
  • Hard: 150 starting gold, 15 lives, enemies have 40% more health and move faster. Score ×1.5. For experienced defenders.
  • Changing difficulty restarts the game. Your best wave & score are saved separately for each difficulty.

Practice Round — warm up first

  • Flip the Mode switch at the top to Practice Round any time — it restarts the game in practice.
  • Unlimited gold: build, upgrade and experiment with any tower combo, no saving up.
  • No lives lost: enemies that reach the base just wave and walk off — the game never ends in practice.
  • Nothing counts: practice score and waves are shown while you play but are never saved to your Best stats.
  • Difficulty still applies in practice, so you can rehearse the exact Hard-mode waves before playing them for real.
  • Switch back to Real Game when you're ready to make it count.

Handy controls

  • Pause freezes the action. Speed toggles 1× / 2× / 3×.
  • Starting the next wave early (while one is still running) is allowed — waves will overlap, so be careful!
  • Hovering / tapping a placed tower shows its range ring.

Win & lose conditions

  • Win: clear Wave 20 with at least 1 life left. After that, Endless mode continues with ever-tougher waves.
  • Lose: your lives reach 0. Each enemy that reaches the base costs 1 life; Tanks cost 2 lives.

Scoring & gold

  • Score (before difficulty multiplier): +10 per kill (+25 Speeder, +50 Tank, +30 Swarm, +500 Boss), plus +100 × wave number when a wave is cleared. Then multiplied by your difficulty: Easy ×0.7, Normal ×1, Hard ×1.5.
  • Gold: bounty per kill (6–20 by type, 150 for a Boss) + wave-clear bonus of 25 + 2 × wave number — same on every difficulty.
  • Best wave and best score are saved on this device, separately per difficulty, and shown in the 🏆 Best stat.

Mode & fair play

  • Mode: single-player vs. the waves — there is no opponent player and no wagering. Gold and score are play points only, with no cash value.
  • 🧪 Practice Round: an optional warm-up mode chosen with the Mode switch at the top. Gold is unlimited, leaked enemies cost no lives (so a practice game can't be lost), and practice score/waves are never saved to your bests. Difficulty settings still apply. Switching mode restarts the game.
  • Boss lives: a Boss that reaches the base costs 5 lives (see Enemies table above).
  • Endless: after Wave 20 the win is banked; Endless waves keep scaling in health and count toward your best wave and best score.
