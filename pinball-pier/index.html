How to play

1
Launch Ball

Tap LAUNCH to send the ball up.

2
Use Flippers

Tap the flipper buttons or ← → keys to keep the ball alive.

3
Hit Bumpers

Bounce off the bumpers to score points.

4
Chase Score

You get 3 balls per game — chase the high score!

Rules

  • Bumpers score 50, flipper saves score 10.
  • 3 balls per game.
  • Hard: faster ball. Easy: slower, gentler table.
  • Practice mode never saves to your best.
