📖 Rules - I Spy Hunt

🎯 Goal

Find every item and character on the find list hiding in the busy scene. Tap or click each one when you spot it. Find them all before the clock runs out for the best score!

🕹️ How a round works

  • Pick a difficulty and a scene, then press Start the hunt. The crowd and every hiding spot are placed fresh each round.
  • Tap or click directly on a hidden item in the picture. A gold ring marks it found and it gets ticked off the find list.
  • Tapping the wrong spot costs 10 points, so look before you tap.
  • Use the + zoom button to make the scene bigger, then scroll or swipe around to search up close.
  • The round ends when everything is found, when the countdown hits zero, or when you press End round.

⭐ Scoring

ActionPoints
Each item found+100
Wrong tap-10
Hint used (real rounds)-50
Time bonus (Normal and Hard, only when you find everything)+2 per second left

Your best score for each difficulty is saved on this device. Scores never go below zero.

💡 Hints

You get 3 hints per round. A hint lights up the quarter of the scene where one unfound item is hiding. In a real round each hint costs 50 points. In Practice, hints are free and unlimited.

How to play

A 20 second tour, then the hunt is yours:

1
Pick Difficulty

Easy has no countdown and fewer items; Hard packs the crowd with just 2 minutes.

2
Pick a Scene

Choose Market Day, Beach Picnic, or Surprise Me; your picks are remembered.

3
Read Find List

The list under the picture shows who and what is hiding; the crowd shuffles every round.

4
Tap Finds

Tap an item in the picture for 100 points; wrong taps cost 10 points.

5
Zoom In

Press + to zoom in and scroll around the scene for a closer look.

6
Use Hints

A hint lights up the quarter hiding something; 3 per round, each costs 50 points.

7
Pro Tip

Search left to right in rows, and watch for the little rubber duck.
