Picking a difficulty

Easy is kind — snacks rarely stick (1-in-6) and you get 4 shakes. Normal is the classic machine: 1-in-4 stuck, 3 shakes. Hard is a rusty old machine: 1-in-3 stuck, only 2 shakes, and a shorter 75-second round.

Rules

The round

  • One 90-second round (75 seconds on Hard). Sell as many snacks as you can before time runs out.
  • You start each round with 20 coins in your purse.

Buying snacks

  • Space or the INSERT COIN button moves one coin from your purse into credit.
  • Tap a snack, press 1–9, or move with the arrow keys and press Enter to buy it.
  • Each snack costs 1–3 coins. If you can't afford it, nothing dispenses — no penalty, just feed more coins.
  • A sold snack drops into the tray. The machine restocks itself, so every snack is always available.

Stuck snacks

  • Sometimes a snack jams on the coil instead of dropping. Easy: 1-in-6, Normal: 1-in-4, Hard: 1-in-3.
  • Press S or tap SHAKE to shake the machine. Each shake has a chance to free it. Easy: 4 shakes, Normal: 3, Hard: 2 per stuck snack.
  • Free it and the sale counts. Run out of shakes and the snack is lost — no points, but your coins are refunded.
  • While a snack is stuck you can't buy another one. Shake first!

Score

  • Each sale scores price × 10 points (a 3-coin snack is worth 30).
  • Speed bonus: sell another snack within 4 seconds of the last sale and earn +5.
  • When time runs out, the round ends — score is sales plus speed bonuses only.
  • Your best score, best sales in a round, and total snacks freed are saved on this device.

Difficulty

  • Easy: 90 seconds, stuck 1-in-6, 4 shakes.
  • Normal: 90 seconds, stuck 1-in-4, 3 shakes.
  • Hard: 75 seconds, stuck 1-in-3, 2 shakes.
  • You can change difficulty between rounds — it takes effect on the next round. Your choice is remembered for next time.

Practice round

  • Tap Practice round to switch to a practice session at any time.
  • No timer, no score is kept — just feed coins and learn the machine.
  • Practice never saves anything. Tap the button again to return to a timed round.
