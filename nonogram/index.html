Rules

The full rules — scoring, tools, and exactly what counts as a win.

Objective & win condition

Reveal the hidden picture by filling squares so that every row and every column exactly matches its clues. You win the moment every square that belongs in the picture is filled. Squares left blank or marked ✕ are treated the same — only fills are judged. There is no move limit and no way to lose; the clock and your score are the challenge.

Difficulty

Pick a difficulty above the board — it sets the grid size for both the picture library and random boards, and your choice is saved on this device:

  • Easy · 5×5 — quick warm-ups: one picture plus random boards.
  • Normal · 10×10 — the classic size: four pictures plus random boards.
  • Hard · 15×15 — the big grid: one picture plus random boards.

Switching difficulty loads the first picture at that size right away. Bigger grids score more (see Scoring).

Clues

  • Numbers list the lengths of consecutive filled runs in that line, in order.
  • Separate runs are always divided by at least one blank square.
  • A clue of 0 means no squares in that line are filled.
  • When a line's fills exactly satisfy its clue, the clue greys out.

  • Hint: −50 points each, and fills one correct square.
  • Check: −25 points each use. It outlines wrong fills in red for a few seconds; wrong fills it flags stay until you fix them — Check never changes the board for you.
  • Speed bonus on a win: +2 points per second under par (par: 2 min for 5×5, 6 min for 10×10, 12 min for 15×15).
  • Score never drops below 0. The “clean-solve bonus” needs zero hints and zero checks.

Practice rounds

Flip the Round switch above the board to Practice for a no-pressure round on any library puzzle or random board: the clock never starts, no score is kept, and Hint and Check are free and unlimited. Practice solves don't save a best time, and switching rounds restarts the current puzzle with a clean board. Your round choice is saved on this device.

Special rules

  • Random puzzles are generated fresh and always have at least one valid solution — the one that was generated. They score as their size, and their picture has no name to reveal.
  • Show picture name just tells you what you're drawing. It costs nothing and doesn't affect your score.
  • Restart clears the board, the clock, and your score for that attempt. Your best time for the puzzle is saved on this device.
  • Marks (✕) are private notes. They don't score, don't block a win, and get ignored by Check.
