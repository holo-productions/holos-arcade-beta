🎓 Tutorial — How to Play

No forced walkthrough — dip in whenever you like, then jump straight into a race.

0. Try a Practice Round first

Press 🟢 Practice Round for solo, untimed laps with no score and no rivals. A glowing racing line, braking hints and live tips are on — nothing is saved to your bests, so experiment freely. Exit any time.

1. Start the race

Pick a difficulty, then press Start Race. Watch the 3 · 2 · 1 · GO countdown — moving early just wastes revs, there's no penalty.

2. Steer & speed

Hold accelerate to build speed. Ease off before tight corners — karts understeer at full speed, so a short lift (or tap of brake) keeps you on the racing line.

3. Stay off the grass

Grass cuts your top speed roughly in half and makes steering sluggish. The red-and-white kerbs are safe; the green is not.

4. Use the gold pads

Three boost pads sit on the track. Rolling over one fills your boost meter. Spend it with Space on a straight for an easy overtake.

5. Watch your position

Live Standings update as you race. Your lap only counts when you cross the start/finish line going the right way — no shortcuts.

6. Stuck or spun?

Press R to pop back onto the track at the last point you passed, facing the right way. It costs a couple of seconds, but beats reversing out of a fence.

📖 Rules

  • Practice Round: Solo, untimed free driving with hints on — a glowing racing line, braking markers and live tips. No rivals, no countdown, no laps limit, and no score: practice never changes your best lap, best race, wins or races played.
  • The race: 3 laps of the circuit against 5 AI karts. First kart to finish 3 laps wins; everyone else is placed by distance covered when the winner finishes.
  • Difficulty: Easy — rivals are slower and make more mistakes. Normal — a fair fight. Hard — rivals are faster, hold tighter lines, and get a small top-speed edge. Your kart never changes; only the rivals do.
  • Lap counting: A lap counts only when you cross the start/finish line in the correct direction after passing the mid-track checkpoint. Cutting across the infield does not count.
  • Off-track: On grass your speed is heavily limited. Kerbs count as track.
  • Boost: Gold pads charge your meter (max 100%). Boost can be used any time the meter is above 25% and drains while active.
  • Contact: Karts bump and push each other — light contact is part of racing. There's no damage and no elimination.
  • Bests: Your best lap and best full-race time are saved separately for each difficulty on this device, along with races played and won.
  • Fair play: All karts, names, and colours are original and generic — no real teams, drivers, or brands.
