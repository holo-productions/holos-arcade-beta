How to play

No forced walkthrough. Dip in whenever you like, then jump straight into a race.

1
Practice First

Practice Round: solo untimed laps, no score, hints on, nothing saved.

2
Pick a Track

Choose Kart Park or Neon Loop, pick difficulty, press Start Race, watch 3-2-1-GO.

3
Steer and Speed

Hold accelerate; ease off or brake before tight corners to hold the line.

4
Stay Off Grass

Grass halves top speed and dulls steering; kerbs are safe, the infield is not.

5
Grab Item Boxes

Drive over pink boxes for a random item; hold one item at a time.

6
Use Your Item

Press Space or Item: Turbo boosts, Bubble shields, Peel drops, Shell homes ahead.

7
Boost Pads

Three gold pads charge your boost; spend it with Shift on a straight.

8
Watch Position

Standings run 1st to 8th; laps count only past the checkpoint the right way.

9
Stuck? Reset

Press R to pop back on track; spins from peels and shells last about 1 second.

Rules

  • Practice Round: Solo, untimed free driving with hints on, including a glowing racing line, braking markers and live tips. No rivals, no countdown, no lap limit, and no score. Practice never changes your best lap, best race, wins or races played.
  • The race: 3 laps against 7 AI karts, 8 racers total. First kart to finish 3 laps wins. Everyone else is placed by distance covered when the race ends. The results screen shows all placements and times.
  • Tracks: Kart Park is wide and flowing. Neon Loop is tighter with more hairpins. Best lap and best race times are saved separately for each track and each difficulty.
  • Difficulty: Easy: rivals are slower, make more mistakes, and get weaker items. Normal: a fair fight. Hard: rivals are faster, hold tighter lines, use items more often, and catch up faster if they fall behind. Your kart never changes. Only the rivals and item luck change. Difficulty is saved for next time.
  • Rubber banding: To keep races close, rivals far behind get a small speed help and rivals far ahead ease off a little. The effect is stronger on Hard and gentler on Easy. It never teleports anyone. Driving clean still wins.
  • Items: Item boxes give one random item. Racers at the back are a bit more likely to get a Turbo Star or Seeker Shell. The leader is a bit more likely to get a Bubble Guard or Slippery Peel. Turbo Star boosts for about 2 seconds. Bubble Guard blocks one hit for up to 8 seconds. Slippery Peel stays on track for 20 seconds and spins anyone who touches it. Seeker Shell homes to the nearest rival ahead and spins them unless they are shielded. You can hold one item at a time.
  • Lap counting: A lap counts only when you cross the start and finish line in the correct direction after passing the mid track checkpoint. Cutting across the infield does not count.
  • Off track: On grass your speed is heavily limited. Kerbs count as track.
  • Boost pads: Gold pads charge your meter up to 100 percent. Boost can be used any time the meter is above 25 percent and drains while active. Turbo Star is separate and does not use the meter.
  • Contact: Karts bump and push each other. Light contact is part of racing. There is no damage and no elimination. Spins only slow you briefly.
  • End Race: Press End Race any time to stop the current race and see results based on progress so far. In Practice, End Race exits practice. Results from an early end still count as a race played if you were racing, but best race time only saves if you finished 3 laps.
  • Bests: Your best lap and best full race time are saved separately for each track and each difficulty on this device, along with races played and won. Practice never saves bests.
  • Fair play: All karts, names, items, art and colours are original and generic. No real teams, drivers, brands or copied characters.
