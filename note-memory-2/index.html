How to play

  1. Pick a difficulty and tap Start game.
  2. Watch & listen — the pads light up and play a short melody, one note at a time. You can't tap during playback.
  3. Your turn — tap the same pads in the same order. Each pad is a musical note (C, D, E, F, G, A, B, C) with its own colour and pitch. Keys 1–8 work too.
  4. Get the whole tune right and the next round adds more notes. One wrong note ends the game.
  5. Your score is the longest tune you repeated correctly. Beat your best!
  6. Just warming up? Tap 🎓 Practice round (or pick Practice under Mode). Nothing is scored, a wrong note never ends the game — the tune simply plays again so you can retry it — and you can tap 🔁 Hear it again any time on your turn to replay the tune. Quit whenever you're ready for the real thing.

Tip: hum the melody back to yourself as it plays — your ears remember tunes your eyes forget.

Rules

The game

  • Note Memory is a sequence-memory game. Each round, the game plays a melody on 8 note pads (C4–C5). Repeat the exact sequence, in order, by tapping the pads.
  • The sequence grows every round (see Difficulty). A single wrong note ends the game immediately — there are no retries within a round.
  • Pads are locked while the melody plays back; taps during playback are ignored.
  • Keyboard: keys 1–8 play the pads left to right, top row first.

Scoring

  • Your score is the length of the longest sequence you repeated fully correctly in that game.
  • Example: complete the 5-note tune, then miss a note in the 6-note tune — your score is 5.
  • Best scores are saved on this device, separately for each difficulty.

Practice rounds

  • Practice rounds are unscored: nothing is saved, and your best score is never affected.
  • A wrong note does not end a practice round. The game names the note that should have come next, replays the whole tune, and you try the same round again.
  • On your turn in practice, tap 🔁 Hear it again to replay the current tune as many times as you like; your progress on that tune resets.
  • Tunes grow at the same rate as the chosen difficulty. End a practice round any time with Quit to start.
  • Mode (Scored / Practice) can only be changed between games, never mid-game. Your choice is remembered on this device.

Difficulty

  • 😌 Easy — slow playback (700 ms per note), starts at 2 notes, grows by 1 note per round.
  • 🎯 Normal — medium playback (500 ms per note), starts at 3 notes, grows by 1 note per round.
  • 🔥 Hard — fast playback (350 ms per note), starts at 4 notes, grows by 2 notes per round.
  • Difficulty can only be changed between games, never mid-game. Your choice is remembered on this device.

Sound

  • Every tone is synthesized live with WebAudio — no songs, recordings, or samples are used. Use the Sound button to mute.
