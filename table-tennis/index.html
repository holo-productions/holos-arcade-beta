Easy is for learning the controls. Normal is a fair rally. Hard reads your angles and smashes anything high — beat it and your win counts double toward bragging rights (not points, just pride).

Rules

The game

  • Table tennis, side view of the table. You play the left side (blue paddle); the computer or Player 2 plays the right side (red paddle).
  • First player to 11 points wins the match — and you must win by 2. At 10–10, play continues until someone leads by 2.
  • Serve swaps every two points — and every point once the score reaches 10–10.

Play

  • Your paddle moves up and down beside your end of the table. Step toward the net (D or →) to smash; step back (A or ←) to defend.
  • Where the ball meets your paddle sets its angle: meet a low ball just below it to lift it over the net, meet a high ball from above to drive it down. A moving paddle adds its own speed to the shot.
  • Every shot must clear the net and land on the other side of the table. Into the net = point to the other side.
  • If the ball bounces twice on your side, or flies past your end of the table, the point goes to the other side.
  • After a point, play re-serves from the player whose turn it is to serve.

Modes

  • vs Computer: you (left) against the computer (right). You can use WS or the arrow keys.
  • 2 Players (pass-and-play, same device): Player 1 uses WS to move and AD to step in/out; Player 2 uses ↑↓ to move and ←→ to step in/out. Touch: drag the left half for Player 1, the right half for Player 2.
  • 2-player games don't count toward your bests — they're for bragging rights only.

Difficulty

  • Easy: the computer moves slower, reacts late, and often leaves the ball.
  • Normal: the computer tracks the ball, defends its end, and takes its chances.
  • Hard: the computer moves at full speed, reads the bounce, and smashes high balls.
  • You can change difficulty mid-match — it takes effect right away. Your choice is remembered for next time.

Practice round

  • Tap 🏋 Practice round to switch to a practice session at any time.
  • No score is kept, the clock doesn't run, and there is no winner — points just re-serve so you can keep rallying.
  • A coaching hint shows above the table and changes after every point.
  • Practice points never count toward your bests. Tap the button again to return to a first-to-11 match.

Bests

  • Wins, matches played, fastest win, and best win streak (vs-computer matches only) are stored on this device only.
