How to play

Key Tiles is a falling-tile music game. Tiles drop down four lanes — your taps play the tune!

  1. Pick a difficulty and a melody, then tap ▶ Play.
  2. Watch the tiles fall. When a tile reaches the gold line near the bottom, tap its lane.
  3. A good tap plays the next note of the melody and scores points. Closer to the line = better score.
  4. If a tile falls past the bottom, or you tap a lane with no tile on the line, you lose a life.
  5. Tiles fall faster the longer you survive. Keep your combo going for bonus points!

Controls

  • Touch / mouse: tap or click the lane under the tile.
  • Keyboard: D · F · J · K or 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 (one key per lane, left to right).
  • P or Esc pauses. The button mutes the music.

Practice round

  • Tap Practice round on the start screen (or pick Practice round under Round type, then Play).
  • In practice you can't lose: misses and wrong taps don't cost a life and the round never ends on its own.
  • The fall speed stays steady — no speed-up — so you can learn a melody and get your timing down.
  • You still see your score, combo, Perfect/Good counts and misses while you play, but practice scores are not saved as bests.
  • Tap ✓ Finish practice under the board whenever you're done to see your practice summary.

Quick tips

  • Tap when the tile's middle crosses the gold line for a Perfect.
  • Don't tap empty lanes — in a scored game a wrong tap costs a life, just like a missed tile. In practice it just resets your combo.
  • New here? Start with a Practice round on Easy: slower tiles, wider timing, no pressure.

📜 Rules

Objective

Tap falling tiles as they cross the gold hit line to play an original melody note by note. Survive as long as you can and score as many points as possible. The game ends when you run out of lives.

How play works

  • Tiles fall one at a time down four lanes. Each tile belongs to one lane.
  • Tap a tile's lane while the tile is in the hit zone around the gold line to play it. The tile disappears and the next note of your chosen melody sounds.
  • Only one tile can be played per tap. Tapping a lane whose tile is still high above the hit zone counts as a wrong tap.
  • Tiles speed up gradually as your hit count grows. The melody loops until the game ends.

Scoring

  • Perfect (tile centred on the gold line): 100 points.
  • Good (tile in the hit zone but off-centre): 60 points.
  • Combo bonus: every hit adds bonus points equal to your current combo streak, up to +50. Example: a Perfect on a ×20 combo scores 100 + 20 = 120.
  • Your combo grows by 1 with every successful tap and resets to 0 on any miss or wrong tap.
  • Best score and best combo are saved per difficulty on this device.

Misses & lives

  • A tile that falls past the bottom unplayed is a miss: you lose 1 life and your combo resets.
  • Tapping a lane with no tile in the hit zone is a wrong tap: you lose 1 life and your combo resets.
  • When lives reach 0, the game ends and your final score is shown.

Practice round

  • A practice round uses the difficulty and melody you picked, but with relaxed rules: unlimited lives, no speed-up (fall speed stays at that difficulty's starting speed), and no game over.
  • Misses and wrong taps still reset your combo and count as misses in your summary, so you can see what to work on — they just don't end the round.
  • Points and combos are shown live for feedback only. Practice scores and combos are never saved as best scores and don't count as a played scored game.
  • End a practice round any time with ✓ Finish practice, Pause → Quit to start, or Restart. Finishing shows a summary: score, Perfect, Good, misses, best combo and accuracy.

Difficulty

  • Easy: slowest fall speed, widest hit zone, 5 lives, gentle speed-up.
  • Normal: steady fall speed, standard hit zone, 3 lives.
  • Hard: fastest fall speed, tightest hit zone, 2 lives, quickest speed-up.
  • Difficulty, melody and round type are chosen before a game starts and are remembered for next time. Scoring values are the same on every difficulty.

Sound

  • All music is synthesized live with WebAudio — original melodies only, no recordings or samples.
  • Use the 🔊 button to mute or unmute. Your choice is remembered.
