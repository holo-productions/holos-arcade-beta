Rules — Instrument Match

Objective

Identify each synthesized instrument sound. Finish 10 rounds with the highest score you can. There is no opponent — you are playing against your own best.

Practice round

Practice is an optional, endless warm-up. Tap 🎧 Practice round on the setup card to open it. At the top you'll get a sound explorer — tap any of the 9 instruments to hear its labelled game sound as many times as you like. Below that, practice questions keep coming at your chosen difficulty until you tap Finish practice. No points are scored, no streak bonus is paid, and nothing from practice is saved to your best score, best streak, or games played. When you finish, you can jump straight into a scored game.

Difficulty — the arcade standard

Every game in the arcade uses the same three settings. Pick one before you start; it applies to the whole game and is saved on this device.

SettingChoicesWhat you get
Easy3Very different sounds only (e.g. Bell vs Kick vs Guitar) — good for warming up or learning.
Normal4A mix of distinct and trickier pairs. The balanced default.
Hard5Similar sounds grouped together (Piano / Bell / Strings, Snare / Hi-Hat, Bass / Kick) — for a real challenge.

Special rules

  • Each round's instrument is chosen at random; the same instrument can appear more than once in a game, but never twice in a row.
  • Melodic instruments play the same 3-note phrase shape at a random starting pitch each round, so you must judge by tone (timbre), not by pitch.
  • All sounds are synthesized live in your browser with WebAudio oscillators and noise — no recordings or samples are used.
  • Best score, best streak, and games played are saved on this device only (localStorage), separately per difficulty.
