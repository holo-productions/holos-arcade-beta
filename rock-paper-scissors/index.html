How to play

1. Pick a mode: 2 Players on one phone, or vs Computer.

2. Choose Best of 3, 5 or 7. First to win the majority wins the match.

3. Player 1 taps a choice in secret. The pick stays hidden.

4. In 2 Player mode, pass the phone to Player 2. Player 2 taps Ready, then picks.

5. Both picks reveal with an animation. The winner of the round scores a point.

6. Draws replay with no point. Tap Next Round to keep going.

7. Turn on Lizard-Spock for 5 choices and extra matchups.

8. In vs Computer mode, you pick and the computer picks at random right away.

Rules

Classic (3 choices)

Rock beats Scissors. Scissors beats Paper. Paper beats Rock. Same pick is a draw.

Lizard-Spock (5 choices)

Rock beats Scissors and Lizard.

Paper beats Rock and Spock.

Scissors beats Paper and Lizard.

Lizard beats Spock and Paper.

Spock beats Scissors and Rock.

Match

Best of 3 means first to 2. Best of 5 means first to 3. Best of 7 means first to 4. Draws do not count toward the target. Match wins are saved on this device.
