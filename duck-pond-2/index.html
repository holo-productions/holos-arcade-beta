How to Play

Duck Pond is the classic carnival game: every duck hides a number underneath. Find the matching pairs and win tickets!

1
Watch the peek

Numbers show briefly — memorize where each one is.

2
Pick two ducks

Tap one duck, then a second. Two ducks = one pick.

3
Match numbers

Same number = match. Bigger numbers win more tickets!

4
No match?

Different numbers flip back. Remember and pick again.

5
Clear the pond

Match every pair to clear the pond and level up.

6
Practice mode

Unlimited picks, no timer, Peek any time for a hint.

Rules

  • Goal: Match every pair of ducks with the same number hidden underneath to clear the pond and advance to the next level.
  • A pick = two ducks. Tap one duck, then a second. If the numbers match, the pair stays flipped and you earn tickets. If they don't match, both flip back.
  • Tickets: Each matched pair pays tickets equal to its number (for example, a pair of 7s pays 70 tickets). Matching pairs back-to-back builds a streak bonus. Clearing a pond pays a level bonus, plus a time bonus on timed difficulties.
  • Peek: Numbers are shown briefly at the start of each pond. In Practice you can peek again any time. In Play for Tickets, peek is only at the start (Easy gives you the longest look).
  • Levels: Each cleared pond levels you up — more pairs, tighter picks and time, and bigger prize numbers. The game ends when you run out of picks or time with pairs still hidden.
  • Hard shuffles: On Hard, after a missed pick two hidden ducks swap places. Watch carefully!
  • Practice mode: Unlimited picks, no timer, peek any time. Practice scores are never saved to your bests.
  • Bests (best tickets, highest level, ponds cleared) are saved per difficulty in this browser only (localStorage). Clearing browser data removes them.
