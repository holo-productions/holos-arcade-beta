CAEC Prep

Five subjects, just like the real CAEC test. Practice untimed and learn as you go — or take a timed test and see if you beat the 55% pass mark.

  • Reading — Canadian passages + comprehension questions (real test: 50 questions, 75 min).
  • Writing — one persuasive writing task (real test: 75 min). You write, then check yourself against the rubric.
  • Mathematics — Part I without a calculator, Part II with one (real test: 2 hours).
  • Social Studies — Canadian history, government, economics, civics (real test: 40 questions, 90 min).
  • Science — general science (real test: 35 questions, 90 min).
  • The real CAEC is free to write in Saskatchewan. You need 55% on each subject to pass.
  • Every question has a Source button — fact-check as you go, retro TV style.

How to Play

  • Pick a subject, then a mode: Practice is untimed and explains every answer; Timed Test is scored against the clock.
  • Tap an answer. In Practice you see right away if it was right, and why.
  • Reading: read the passage, then answer its questions.
  • Mathematics: Part I questions are no-calculator; Part II allows a calculator.
  • Writing: pick a prompt, write your answer, then tick the rubric honestly — 6 out of 10 passes.
  • The pass mark is 55% on every subject, just like the real CAEC.
  • Every question has a Source button — tap it to see the exact quote from the real source on a retro TV, with the source listed below. In a timed test, the clock pauses while you check.
  • Your best score per subject is saved on this device.
  • Press P to pause.
