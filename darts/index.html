Darts Rules

Everything you need to play 501.

How 501 works

You and the AI both start on 501 points. Every dart you throw subtracts its score from your total. The first player to reach exactly zero wins the leg — and the final dart must land in a double (the thin outer ring) or the inner bull. First to win 3 legs takes the match.

Taking a turn

  • Each turn is 3 darts. Turns alternate between you and the AI.
  • Aim by moving over the board. Your aim ring drifts slightly — time your throw for when it sits where you want it.
  • Tap the board or press Throw dart to throw. Darts stay on the board until the turn ends.

Scoring

Where it landsScore
Single segment (the big areas)The segment number (1–20)
Double ring (thin outer ring)2 × the segment number
Treble ring (thin middle ring)3 × the segment number
Outer bull (green ring)25
Inner bull (red centre)50 — counts as a double
Off the board / outside the double ring0 — a miss

The highest possible turn is 180: three treble 20s.

Bust!

  • If a dart takes your score below zero, leaves you on exactly 1, or reaches zero without a double, your turn is a bust.
  • A bust ends your turn immediately and your score goes back to what it was at the start of the turn.

Checking out

  • When your score is 170 or less, a checkout may be possible — the game shows a suggested finish above the dart slots.
  • Examples: 40 left → double 20. 32 left → double 16. 50 left → the bull. 100 left → treble 20, then double 20.
  • Plan ahead: leaving yourself an even number under 40 gives you a one-dart finish.

Practice round

  • Press Practice round any time for a no-pressure leg: the AI sits out, you throw turn after turn, and hints stay on.
  • Practice doesn't count — no legs, no bests, nothing saved. Checking out just starts you a fresh practice leg.
  • Exit practice (or press New game) to jump back into a real match.

The AI & difficulty

  • Easy — the AI wanders. Great for learning the board.
  • Normal — the AI hits its targets most of the time and will punish a slow leg.
  • Hard — the AI clusters tightly around treble 20 and checks out ruthlessly.
  • Your AI level is saved, so it's set the way you left it next visit.

Board layout

The 20 sits at the top, with numbers arranged so big scores sit next to small ones — 20 is flanked by 1 and 5, which is why a stray dart hurts. The order around the board is: 20, 1, 18, 4, 13, 6, 10, 15, 2, 17, 3, 19, 7, 16, 8, 11, 14, 9, 12, 5.
