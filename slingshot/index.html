How to play — 30-second tutorial

No walkthrough is forced on you — this page is here whenever you want it.

1

Press and hold on the ball at the slingshot, on the left.

2

Pull back and down — the dotted line shows where the ball will fly.

3

Let go and the ball flies. Further back means more power.

Picking a difficulty

Easy is a stronger launch and lighter blocks — learn the arc. Medium is a fair smash. Hard is a weaker launch and heavier blocks that barely budge.

Rules

The game

  • A slingshot on the left, a tower of blocks on the right, and stars perched on top.
  • 5 levels, taller and trickier as you go. 3 shots per level.
  • Knock every star below the dashed line to clear the level.

Aiming & firing

  • Drag back from the slingshot to aim — the dotted line previews the flight. Release to fire.
  • Left / Right arrows change the angle, Up / Down change the power, Space fires.
  • On-screen buttons and a controller work too: stick aims, A fires, Start pauses.

Blocks & stars

  • Blocks fall and topple when hit hard enough, or when the blocks holding them up are gone.
  • A star counts as down once it drops below the dashed line — or gets knocked clear off its perch.
  • The ball bounces off the ground and the blocks. Wait for it to settle before your next shot.

Score

  • +100 per star knocked down, +50 per unused shot, plus a level bonus (+100 × level number).
  • Clear all 5 levels to finish the game. Your best score is saved on this device.

Difficulty

  • Easy: stronger launch, lighter blocks.
  • Medium: normal launch and block weight.
  • Hard: weaker launch, heavier blocks.
  • You can change difficulty between levels — it takes effect on the next shot. Your choice is remembered for next time.

Practice round

  • Tap Practice round to switch to a practice session at any time.
  • Unlimited shots on level 1, no score is kept — just learn the arc.
  • Practice never saves anything. Tap the button again to return to the real game.
