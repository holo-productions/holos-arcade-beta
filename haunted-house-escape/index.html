How to Play

1. Tap to explore. Every room has things worth poking — paintings, rugs, cupboards, cats. Glowing spots are worth a tap. On Hard difficulty the glow hides, so trust your curiosity!

2. Collect keys & clues. Things you pick up land in your pockets at the bottom. Keys open locks, treats open… cats. You'll also hunt for three number clues that open a safe.

3. Crack the safe. In the Library, a cat named Miso guards a safe. Feed Miso, then enter the three numbers in the order you were told: bats, clock, pumpkins. The diary on the desk keeps the order straight.

4. Escape! The safe holds the Golden Key. Take it back to the front door in the Foyer and you're free. Stuck? The Hint button always knows what's next. Good luck!

Rules

You're visiting a very friendly haunted house — but the front door has locked behind you. Rude! Find the Golden Key and let yourself out.

How it works

  • Tap objects in each room to search them. Move between rooms with the doorways or the room buttons.
  • Keys and items go into your pockets and are used automatically when you tap the right lock.
  • Three number clues are hidden around the house. Together they form the safe's 3-digit code — order matters!
  • Some residents are… negotiable. Cats accept treats.

Difficulty

  • Easy: no time limit, unlimited hints, hotspots glow.
  • Medium: 10-minute timer, 5 hints, hotspots glow.
  • Hard: 6-minute timer, 2 hints, hotspots don't glow. Spooky!
  • Your difficulty choice is saved for next time.

Scoring

  • You start with 1,000 points. Every 5 seconds costs 1 point, and each hint costs 50.
  • Run out of time on Medium or Hard and the house keeps you a little longer — just try again.
  • Practice mode is untimed with unlimited hints, and no score is saved. Perfect for a first wander.
  • Best score and fastest escape are saved per difficulty on this device.
