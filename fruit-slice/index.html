Picking a difficulty

Easy is slow fruit, rare bombs and 4 lives. Normal is a fair pace with 3 lives. Hard is fast fruit, more bombs and only 2 lives.

Rules

The round

  • A 60-second round. Fruit is tossed up from the bottom and arcs back down under gravity.
  • You start with 3 lives (4 on Easy, 2 on Hard). The round ends when the clock hits zero or you lose all your lives.
  • Missing a fruit is fine — there is no penalty for letting fruit fall.

Slicing

  • Drag across the board (mouse or touch) to swipe the blade through fruit. Sliced halves fly apart.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move the blade cursor, hold Space and move to slice.
  • Controller: stick / D-pad moves the blade, hold A to slice, Start pauses.

Bombs

  • Slicing a bomb costs 1 life and resets your combo and streak. Bombs look round and black with a sparking fuse — let them fall.

Score

  • Each fruit sliced is +10 points.
  • Combo: slicing 2 or more fruit in one swipe earns +5 per extra fruit in that swipe.
  • Streak: slicing fruit again within a couple of seconds of your last slice builds a streak — every 5th streak slice earns a +10 streak bonus.
  • Your best score, best fruit count and best streak are saved on this device.

Difficulty

  • Easy: slow fruit, rare bombs, 4 lives.
  • Normal: moderate fruit speed and bombs, 3 lives.
  • Hard: fast fruit, more bombs, 2 lives.
  • You can change difficulty between rounds — it takes effect on the next round. Your choice is remembered for next time.

Practice round

  • Tap Practice round to switch to a practice session at any time.
  • Same 60-second round, but no score is kept — slice freely and learn the timing.
  • Practice never saves anything. Tap the button again to return to a scored round.
