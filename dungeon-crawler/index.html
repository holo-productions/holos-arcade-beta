📖 Rules: Dungeon Crawler

🎯 Goal

Descend as deep as you can, collect as much gold as you can, and survive. Your run ends when your health reaches zero. Final score is your gold plus 50 points for each floor you cleared, times your difficulty multiplier.

🕹️ How to play

  • Move one tile at a time with the arrow buttons or arrow keys and WASD.
  • Walls block you. Enemies move after you move, so every step counts.
  • Walk into an enemy to attack it. Enemies have health too. Watch the log to see each hit.
  • Walk onto treasure to collect gold. Walk onto a potion to heal.
  • Walk onto the stairs to descend to the next floor. A new floor is generated each time.
  • Use Wait to let an enemy come to you. Use End round any time to finish early and bank your score.

💰 Treasure and potions

  • Treasure piles are worth 10 to 30 gold each.
  • Potions heal 6 health, up to your maximum.
  • Descending heals 2 health as a small breather. Plan your route.
