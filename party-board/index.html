📖 Party Board — Rules

Goal

Finish with the most stars ⭐. Tied on stars? Most coins 🪙 wins.

Setup

  • 2–4 players race around a 24-space loop board on one device (pass-and-play).
  • Any seat without a human is played by an AI rival. AI skill follows the difficulty setting.
  • Everyone starts with 10 coins and 0 stars.
  • Pick 5, 10, or 15 rounds. One round = every player takes one turn.

Your turn

  • Tap Roll dice and move that many spaces. Passing Start pays +5 coins.
  • 🪙 Coin space: +3 coins. 💸 Ouch space: −3 coins (never below 0).
  • ⭐ Star space: buy a star for 10 coins if you can afford it.
  • 🎮 Mini-game space: everyone plays a mini-game. Coins: 1st = 10, 2nd = 5, 3rd = 3, 4th = 1.
  • ❓ Chance space: a surprise — coins, a move, or a star discount.
  • 🌀 Warp space: zoom forward or backward a few spaces.
  • After every full round, everyone plays a bonus mini-game too.

The 8 mini-games

  • 👆 Tap Race — tap the big button as fast as you can.
  • 🧠 Memory Pads — watch the lights, repeat the pattern.
  • ⚡ Reaction — tap the instant it turns green.
  • 🎁 Lucky Chests — pick chests and find the coins.
  • ❓ Quick Quiz — 3 speedy questions.
  • 🎯 Perfect Timing — stop the needle in the green zone.
  • ⭐ Star Catcher — tap falling stars before they vanish.
  • 🧱 Dodge Blocks — sidestep the falling blocks.

Modes & saving

  • Practice mode: play freely — results are not saved to your bests.
  • Difficulty, bests, and feedback are stored only in this browser on this device (localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • All art is original emoji/CSS. This is a generic party game — no licensed characters.

▶ How to play Party Board

1 · Set up. Choose 2–4 players and how many are humans sharing this device. AI rivals fill the rest. Pick a difficulty and game length, then tap Start game.

2 · Roll & move. On your turn, tap Roll dice. Your token hops around the loop board. Passing Start pays +5 coins. Pass the device to the next human when their turn comes.

3 · Spaces. 🪙 coins pay you, 💸 takes coins, 🌀 warps you, ❓ is a surprise, 🎮 starts a mini-game for everyone, and ⭐ lets you buy a star for 10 coins.

4 · Mini-games. There are 8 quick games: tapping, memory, reaction, luck, quiz, timing, catching, and dodging. Best score takes 10 coins. Everyone also plays one after each full round.

5 · Win. When the rounds run out, most stars wins (coins break ties). Use Practice mode any time — practice scores are never saved.
