How to play ▾
  1. Your card: A classic 75-ball card — B is 1–15, I is 16–30, N is 31–45 (the centre square is a free space, already daubed), G is 46–60, O is 61–75.
  2. Call balls: Tap Call ball for one ball at a time, or switch on Auto-call and let the caller keep them coming.
  3. Daub: When a called number is on your card, it gets a gold dot — tap it to daub it. Missed one? No problem, you can daub it any time later. The three AI players — Ruby, Sage and Bruno — daub their own cards automatically.
  4. Shout BINGO: Complete the pattern and the big red button lights up. Tap BINGO! to claim it — but be quick: if an AI completes the pattern first, they'll claim after a short delay, and the first claim wins.
  5. False alarm: Shouting BINGO without the pattern just gets you a shake of the caller's head (and a 3-second wait before you can shout again). Check your daubs!

Practice round: Tap Practice round up top (or Start practice round below the hall) any time. Practice is untimed and relaxed — no score is saved to your bests, the AI will not race you or claim a win, and hints are on: tap Show hint to highlight numbers ready to daub or see exactly which numbers your closest pattern still needs. Shouting BINGO early in practice just gives you a hint, no lock-out. Tap Practice again to return to a scored game.

Patterns: Any line — any full row, column or diagonal. 4 corners — just the four corner squares. Blackout — every square on the card. Changing the pattern starts a fresh game.

Difficulty (Easy / Normal / Hard, up top) sets the auto-call pace, how fast the AI claims a win, and how sharp the AI's eyes are: Easy calls every ~4.5 seconds, gives you a long claiming window, and the AI miss about 3 in 10 of their daubs; Normal ~3.2 seconds and the AI miss about 1 in 10; Hard ~2.1 seconds and the AI never miss a daub. On any difficulty you can always call balls by hand.

Your wins, streaks and quickest victory are saved as bests on this device, below the hall. Play money only — there is no money in bingo here, just bragging rights.

Rules ▾

The game. 75-ball bingo for four cards: yours plus Ruby, Sage and Bruno (AI). Balls are drawn without replacement from the full set of 75, in random order. There is no money, staking or prize of value — bragging rights only.

The card. Each card is a 5×5 grid. Columns are fixed ranges: B = 1–15, I = 16–30, N = 31–45, G = 46–60, O = 61–75, with five different numbers per column. The centre square (N column, middle row) is a free space and counts as daubed from the start.

Daubing. A square can only be daubed after its number has been called. You daub your own card by tapping; a called-but-undaubed square shows a gold dot. There is no time limit on daubing — a missed number can be daubed at any later point while the game is running. The AI cards daub automatically the moment a number is called — except on Easy, where they miss roughly 3 in 10 daubs, and on Normal, where they miss roughly 1 in 10. On Hard they never miss. A daub an AI misses stays missed for the rest of that game.

Win conditions — patterns. One pattern is chosen per game: Any line (any complete row, column, or either diagonal — 12 possible lines), 4 corners (the four corner squares only), or Blackout (all 24 numbered squares). The free space counts toward any pattern it sits in.

Claiming — the race. Completing the pattern does not win by itself: you must tap the BINGO! button to claim. When an AI card completes the pattern, that AI claims after a delay — about 6 seconds on Easy, 3.8 seconds on Normal, 2.2 seconds on Hard. The first valid claim wins the game. If you and an AI complete on the same ball, whoever claims first wins.

False claims. Tapping BINGO without a complete pattern is a false claim: no penalty to your card, but you cannot claim again for 3 seconds. The game continues.

Running out of balls. If all 75 balls are called and nobody has claimed, the card with the most daubed squares wins; you win any tie on daub count.

Scoring & bests (saved on this device). A game counts as a win only when you claim a complete pattern (or win the daub-count finish above); an AI claim is a loss. Your bests track games played, wins, losses, win rate, current and best win streaks, blackout wins, and your quickest win — the fewest balls called in any game you have won. Changing pattern starts a new game; changing difficulty mid-game changes the calling pace, the AI claim delay and the AI's daub sharpness from that point on.

Practice rounds. A practice round uses the same cards, patterns and draw as a scored game, with three differences: (1) nothing is recorded in your bests — wins, losses, streaks and quickest win are unchanged; (2) the AI never claim, so there is no race and no time pressure — auto-call, if you use it, runs at a relaxed pace; (3) hints are available at any time via Show hint, and a false BINGO claim gives a hint instead of the 3-second lock-out. Completing the pattern in practice ends the round with a practice-complete message, not a scored win.

Fair play. Every card is generated fresh and randomly each game, the draw order is shuffled before the first ball, and the AI gets no information you don't — they simply never forget to daub.
