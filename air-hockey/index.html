Picking a difficulty

Easy is for learning the controls. Normal is a fair game. Hard reads your shots and attacks — beat it and your win counts double toward bragging rights (not points, just pride).

Rules

The game

  • One player (you, blue, bottom) against the computer (red, top) on a single air hockey table.
  • First player to 7 goals wins the match. There is no time limit — the clock just tracks your match time.

Play

  • Your mallet can move anywhere in your half only — it can't cross the centre line.
  • The puck bounces off the side walls and the ends, except through the goal slots in the middle of each end.
  • A goal counts the moment the whole puck crosses the line into a goal slot.
  • After a goal, play restarts from the centre with the puck served toward the player who conceded.
  • Your shot speed comes from your mallet: a moving mallet transfers its speed to the puck.

Difficulty

  • Easy: the computer moves slower, reacts late, and rarely attacks.
  • Normal: the computer tracks the puck, defends its goal, and takes its chances.
  • Hard: the computer moves at full speed, predicts bounces, and attacks whenever the puck is in its half.
  • You can change difficulty mid-match — it takes effect on the next puck movement. Your choice is remembered for next time.

Practice round

  • Tap 🏋 Practice round to switch to a practice session at any time.
  • No score is kept, the clock doesn't run, and there is no winner — goals just re-serve so you can keep drilling.
  • A coaching hint shows above the table and changes after every goal.
  • Practice goals never count toward your bests. Tap the button again to return to a first-to-7 match.

Bests

  • Wins, matches played, fastest win, and best win streak are stored on this device only.
