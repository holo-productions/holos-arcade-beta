Rules of the Playground

  • Pick a grade band. Grades 7–9 focus on the big ideas with friendly numbers. Grades 10–12 add formulas and calculations, like the ones in class and on exams.
  • Each round has 8 questions. Tap the answer you think is right. You will always see a short explanation after you answer, right or wrong — that is where the learning happens.
  • Star rounds: score 4–5 for 1 star, 6–7 for 2 stars, and 8 out of 8 for 3 stars. Your best score for each topic and grade band is saved on this device, so you can come back and beat it.
  • Practice rounds: the questions keep coming for as long as you like — no stars, no timer, and a hint button on every question. Tap "End practice" whenever you are done to see how you did. Practice as much as you like — mistakes here are free.
  • There are no timers and no losing. A low score just means that topic is ready for another try. Every round can be replayed as many times as you want.
  • Launch Lab is a sandbox: change the speed, angle and planet, and launch as often as you like. Nothing in the lab affects your stars.
  • Formulas to remember: speed = distance ÷ time · force = mass × acceleration · weight = mass × gravity · kinetic energy = ½ × mass × speed² · work = force × distance · power = work ÷ time.
For parents and guardians: Physics Playground has no chat, no accounts, and never asks for a name, email or any personal information. There are no ads and no links that leave this page. Star progress is stored only on this device. Use the Parent Feedback button at the top to share thoughts — the form stays on this page and simply lets you copy or download your message.
