Island log

    Resource Island — Rules

    Settle a hex island, harvest what the dice give you, trade shrewdly, and be the first islander to reach 10 victory points (VP).

    Victory points

    Piece / bonusVP
    Settlement1
    City2
    Longest Road (a road chain of 5+, longer than every rival's)2

    Setup

    Each islander places two settlements and two roads, one pair at a time, in turn order and then in reverse. Your second settlement pays you one resource from each tile it touches. Settlements must sit at least one corner away from any other settlement or city.

    Your turn

    1. Roll both dice. Every tile showing that number pays each settlement beside it 1 resource, and each city 2. Tiles numbered 6 and 8 pay out most often.
    2. Trade and build as much as you like, in any order.
    3. End turn and pass the dice.

    Building costs

    PieceCostNotes
    Road1 Wood, 1 BrickMust connect to your own road, settlement, or city.
    Settlement1 Wood, 1 Brick, 1 Grain, 1 WoolMust touch one of your roads and keep one corner clear of other buildings.
    City2 Grain, 3 OreUpgrades one of your settlements; it then produces double.

    Trading

    Bank: hand in any 4 matching resources and take any 1 resource. Rivals: offer a 1-for-1 swap. Whether a rival accepts depends on what they need — and on the difficulty you picked. Easy rivals are generous, Hard rivals only trade when it helps them.

    The robber

    Rolling a 7 wakes the robber. Anyone holding more than 7 resource cards discards half (rounded down). The roller then moves the robber to any tile and steals one random card from a rival settled beside that tile. A tile with the robber on it produces nothing until he moves again.

    Difficulty & practice

    Difficulty changes how cleverly Mira and Bram trade, build, and place the robber. Practice mode shows building hints and never saves results to your bests. Both settings are remembered on this device.
