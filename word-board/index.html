How to play

Word Board rules

The goal

Outscore Lexi, the computer, by building words on the 11×11 board. Letters are worth points, bonus squares multiply them, and the game ends when the tiles run out.

Your turn

  • You hold 7 tiles. Tap a tile, then tap a board square to place it. Tap a tile you just placed to pick it back up.
  • All tiles placed in one turn must sit in a single row or column and form one main word, reading left-to-right or top-to-bottom.
  • The very first word of the game must cover the star centre square and be at least 2 letters long.
  • After the first word, every new word must connect to tiles already on the board — by building on them or by touching them to form cross-words.
  • Every word formed — the main word and any cross-words — must be in the game's built-in word list and at least 2 letters long.
  • Blank tiles can be any letter but score 0.

Scoring

  • Letter values are printed on each tile. Letter ×2 and Letter ×3 squares multiply that tile; Word ×2 and Word ×3 squares multiply the whole word.
  • Bonus squares only count on the turn the tile is first placed on them.
  • Cross-words score too, with bonuses from the newly placed tile that forms them.
  • Play all 7 rack tiles in one turn for a +50 Full Rack bonus.
  • After you play, you draw back up to 7 tiles while the bag lasts.

Other moves

  • Exchange: tap Exchange, tap the tiles you want to swap, then tap Exchange again to confirm. This uses your turn.
  • Pass: skip your turn. Six scoreless turns in a row (passes and exchanges by both sides) end the game.
  • Shuffle and Recall are free — they never use your turn.

How the game ends

  • The game ends when the bag is empty and one player plays their last tile, or after six straight scoreless turns.
  • At the end, each player loses the value of tiles left in their rack. If you empty your rack to finish, you also gain the value of Lexi's leftover tiles.
  • Highest final score wins.

Modes

  • Difficulty (Easy / Medium / Hard) changes how sharp Lexi plays. It is saved on this device, so it will be right where you left it next visit.
  • Practice mode is just you and the board — no opponent, no pressure. You get a Hint button, and you can end whenever you like. Practice games do not count in your record.
