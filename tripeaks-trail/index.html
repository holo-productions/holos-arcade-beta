How to play

1
Clear the peaks

Clear all three peaks to win.

2
One up or down

Tap a face-up card one higher or lower than the waste card.

3
Kings wrap

Kings and Aces wrap around.

4
Chain streaks

Each card played becomes the new waste card — chains build your streak bonus.

5
Draw stock

Stuck? Tap the stock for a new waste card — your streak resets.

6
Uncover cards

Cards flip face-up when both cards covering them are gone.

7
Recycle stock

Recycle the stock: Easy twice, Normal once, Hard never.

Rules

  • Play a card one rank above or below the waste card (A–K wraps).
  • Face-down cards flip when both covering cards are removed.
  • Each play scores 10 + 5 per streak step. Clearing a peak top scores +100.
  • Drawing from stock resets the streak.
  • Clear all 28 peak cards to win. Practice mode never saves to your best.
