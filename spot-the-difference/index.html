How to play

Compare Scene A and Scene B tile by tile. When you spot something different, tap that tile in either scene. Found differences get a green tick in both scenes.

Pick a scene with the buttons at the top — Beach Day, Garden Party, or Deep Space. Easy hides 5 differences, Normal 6, Hard 7. Stuck? Use a Hint to flash one unfound tile (costs 30 points, 3 per game).

Rules

  • Each correct tap scores +100. A wrong tap costs 25 points.
  • Hints cost 30 points each; you get 3 per game.
  • Finish fast for a time bonus: up to +200 for a speedy solve.
  • Find every difference to complete the scene.
  • Practice mode never saves to your best.
