📖 Rules — Ring Toss

Welcome to the booth! Toss rings onto the bottle necks to win tickets. Simple to learn, tricky to master — just like the real fair. 🎪

How a toss works

  1. Aim: the aim marker sweeps left and right across the bottle rack. Tap Lock Aim (or tap the booth, or press Space) when it lines up with the bottle you want.
  2. Power: the power marker sweeps up and down. Tap Toss! to throw. Gentle power lands on the front row, medium on the middle, strong on the back row — too strong and your ring sails right over the rack!
  3. Your ring flies in an arc. Land it on a bottle neck for a ringer and tickets. Clip the bottle or land beside it and it's a miss — so close!

Levels

  • You get a set of rings each level (5 rings; 6 on Easy). Ring the number of bottles shown in the goal to clear the level and move on — your rings refill.
  • Run out of rings before hitting the goal and the run ends. Your tickets, ringers, and highest level are compared to your bests.
  • Levels get spicier: more bottles, a golden bottle worth 50🎟️, a sliding rack you have to time, and wind that nudges your ring in flight (watch the wind arrow!). Later levels combine everything, and past Level 10 the booth keeps scaling up.
  • Clearing a level unlocks it — tap New game any time to restart from Level 1, or keep climbing from where you left off.

Practice

  • Tap Practice for unlimited rings on the current level layout, with coach hints after every toss ("a touch left!", "too strong!").
  • Nothing is saved in Practice — no tickets, no bests, no level unlocks. Tap Exit practice to return to your run.
