How to play

1

Set up your roundChoose a mode (Sprint, Challenge, or Practice), a difficulty, and a category — drill one operation, or pick Mixed to face all four. Harder difficulties pay more points. New or warming up? Start with Practice — no clock, no penalty.

2

Answer fast, answer rightType the answer on the keypad (or your keyboard) and hit Answer — or press Enter. Each correct answer scores points and builds your streak. In Practice, take all the time you need — there's no clock.

3

Protect the streakEvery 5 correct in a row earns a streak bonus. A wrong answer resets the streak to zero — and in Sprint mode it also knocks 3 seconds off the clock. In Practice there's no penalty at all.

4

Beat your bestWhen a Sprint or Challenge round ends you get a grade and your stats. Your best scores and streak are saved on this device, ready to chase next time. Practice rounds aren't saved — they're just for learning.

Rules

Objective

Solve as many arithmetic questions as you can, as accurately as you can. There are no opponents — you are playing against the clock, the question count, and your own saved bests.

Modes & win conditions

  • Sprint (60 seconds): Answer as many questions as possible before time runs out. You "win" the round by beating your previous best Sprint score at that difficulty. The game ends the moment the timer hits zero.
  • Challenge (20 questions): A fixed set of 20 questions with no countdown. Finish all 20 to complete the round; your grade is set by accuracy (below). You "win" by earning a higher grade or score than your saved best.
  • Practice (unlimited): No timer, no time penalty, and no saved score. Answer as many or as few as you like, take as long as you need on each one, and tap End practice whenever you're done. Wrong answers show the correct answer a little longer so you can learn from it. Practice doesn't change your bests or grades — it's just for warming up and drilling.

Scoring

DifficultyPoints per correct answer
Easy — small numbers, tables to ×510
Normal — numbers to 100, tables to ×1220
Hard — numbers to 500, two-digit multiplication30
Expert — numbers to 1,000, negative answers possible50
  • Streak bonus: every 5th correct answer in a row adds a bonus equal to half the question's points (rounded down). The streak counter turns orange while a streak of 5+ is live.
  • Wrong answers score 0 and reset the streak to zero.
  • There is no partial credit and no skipping — every question must be answered to move on.
  • Practice is not scored: points show during the round so you can see how you're doing, but nothing is saved to your bests and no grade is given.

Special rules

  • Sprint time penalty: a wrong answer in Sprint mode subtracts 3 seconds from the remaining time. Challenge mode has no time penalty.
  • Division is always exact: ÷ questions are built backwards from a whole-number answer, so you will never need decimals or remainders.
  • Subtraction: on Easy, Normal and Hard the answer is never negative. On Expert, subtraction can produce negative answers — use the +/− key.
  • Category: pick Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication or Division to drill one skill, or Mixed to draw questions from all four operations.
  • Ending early: "End game" finishes the round immediately and shows results for the questions answered so far. A round with zero answered questions is not recorded.

Saved bests

Your best Sprint score, best Challenge score, and longest streak are stored in this browser on this device only. They are kept separately for each difficulty. Clearing your browser data resets them.
