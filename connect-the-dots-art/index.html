Tap the numbers in order and watch the picture appear.

Easy: next dot glows. Normal: no glow. Hard: no glow, and finished pictures are counted.

Pick a picture to start.

How to Play

1. Pick a picture (or Surprise me).

2. Tap dot 1, then 2, then 3… each correct tap draws a line to the next dot.

3. Wrong taps just wiggle — no penalty, keep going.

4. When the outline is finished, pick a colour and tap Colour it in to fill your picture.

Rules

Connect the dots in number order to reveal the picture. There is no losing and no timer.

Difficulty, mode and finished-picture counts save only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

Practice mode always shows hints and never counts finished pictures.

Be kind in feedback: no personal information needed.
