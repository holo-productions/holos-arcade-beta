Rules & Lab Safety

🎮 How scoring works

  • Each quiz round has 8 questions.
  • Finish a round to earn stars: 4–5 right = ★, 6–7 right = ★★, all 8 = ★★★.
  • Exploring 8 different elements in the Periodic Table earns ★.
  • Each Reaction Lab check question answered correctly adds to your round.
  • Your stars are saved on this device only. No account, no name, nothing leaves the device.
  • Wrong answers never take stars away. Trying again is always allowed.
  • Practice rounds: switch to Practice mode at the top of the lab (or tap “Practise this round” on a results screen) for a warm-up round with a hint on every question. Practice is untimed, and no score or stars are saved.

🧤 Real-world lab safety

  • All mixing in this game happens on screen. Never mix household chemicals — especially cleaners — at home.
  • Real experiments need an adult, safety goggles, and a teacher-approved plan.
  • Never taste, sniff directly, or touch unknown substances.
  • If a real chemical splashes skin or eyes, tell an adult and rinse with lots of water right away.
  • Curious about a reaction you saw here? Ask a science teacher how it is done safely in a school lab.

💛 How we play kindly

  • There are no timers and no losing. Go at your own pace.
  • Mistakes are data — scientists learn from every result.
  • No chat, no other players, no ads, and no links leaving this page.

👨‍👩‍👧 For parents & guardians

  • Content covers standard middle- and high-school chemistry: elements, the periodic table, states of matter, and common reaction types.
  • Reactions are explained, never given as home instructions.
  • No personal information is collected. Stars are stored only in this browser on this device.
  • Use the Parent feedback button to note what worked — feedback is saved as a file you choose to download; nothing is sent anywhere.
