Rules

  • 1–4 players, pass-and-play on one device.
  • Each round is one mini-game; every player plays it once, in order.
  • Each mini-game scores 0–100. Round winner = highest score (ties share the win).
  • Overall winner = most round wins; tie-break = highest total score.
  • Practice mode: scores and bests are NOT saved.
  • Difficulty and best scores are saved in localStorage on this device only.
  • No forced tutorial — use the Tutorial button any time.
