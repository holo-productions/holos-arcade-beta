📜 Rules

  • Players: 2–4 people share one device, taking turns.
  • Drawing: The drawer gets a secret word and draws it on the canvas before the timer ends. No letters, numbers, or writing the word — pictures only!
  • Guessing: Each other player passes the device around, looks at the drawing, and picks 1 of 4 choices (the real word plus 3 decoys).
  • Scoring: A correct guess is worth 10 points. The drawer earns 5 points for every player who guesses correctly.
  • Rounds: In each round, every player draws exactly once. Choose 1, 2, or 3 rounds at setup.
  • Difficulty: Easy = 90 seconds and simple words. Medium = 60 seconds and mixed words. Hard = 45 seconds and trickier words. Your choice is saved on this device.
  • Practice mode: Play a single turn to warm up — scores are not saved and no winner is crowned.
  • Winner: After the last round, the player with the most points wins. Ties share the glory!
  • Fair play: No peeking at the secret word, no hinting out loud, and guessers must not watch the drawing happen.
