How to play

Hit the line

Notes ride down four lanes. Tap the matching lane the moment a note crosses the white line. On a keyboard: D F J K. Early or late still counts — dead-on is a Perfect.

Build the combo

Every hit grows your combo and your score multiplier, up to ×4. A miss breaks the combo and cools the crowd. Keep the crowd meter alive for the best grades.

Pick your pace

Easy is quarter-note friendly with forgiving timing. Normal adds off-beats. Hard is faster, denser, and throws two notes at once. Same track, very different night.

Practice first

Tap Practice round for a short ~15-second warm-up on any stage. Same notes & speed, half the length, misses barely cool the crowd, and nothing is saved to your bests. Perfect for learning a new stage or the D · F · J · K keys, then hit Play for the real run.

Game rules

The full rules — scoring, crowd, grades, difficulty and practice mode.

  1. 🎯 The objective

    Tap every falling note exactly as it crosses the white line, in the matching lane — 🎃 👻 🦇 💀. Tap the lane or the button at the bottom, or press D F J K. Play to the end of the track (about 30 seconds) to clear the stage. Six stages run from 122 to 150 BPM. Stages play their real track through the Spotify player — start it, then start the notes; if Spotify can't load, a built-in beat plays instead and the stage says so.

  2. 🧮 How scoring works

    Timing decides the judgement: Perfect = 300 points, Great = 200, Good = 100 — and the timing windows get tighter on higher difficulties. Every hit grows your combo, and your score multiplier rises by +0.5 every 10 combo, up to ×4. Accuracy weights Perfects fully, Greats at 75% and Goods at 45%. Tapping a lane with no note near the line costs nothing — no penalty for empty taps.

  3. 🎃 Crowd, grades & finishing

    Hits raise the crowd meter (Perfect +3, Great +2, Good +1); a miss resets your combo to 0 and drops the crowd by 7. There's no fail screen — the track always plays to the end. Your grade: S (93%+ accuracy and crowd 55+), A (85%+), B (72%+), C (55%+), D below that. Your best score and grade per stage are kept for this session only — closing the page resets them. One quirk: pausing in Spotify mode stops the track (the game can't pause Spotify); resuming reloads it from the start while the notes pick up where you left off.

  4. 🎚 Difficulty differences

    Easy: notes fall slower, the chart is sparser, and timing windows are wide. Normal: regular fall speed with off-beats in the chart. Hard: fastest fall, densest chart, tightest windows, and occasional two-note chords (two lanes at once). Difficulty is locked while a run is live, so the chart can't change mid-song.

  5. 🧪 Practice mode

    Practice rounds use the same stage, speed and difficulty but run about half the track (~15 seconds). Misses barely cool the crowd (it never drops below 30%) and nothing is saved to your session bests. Perfect for learning a new stage or the keys before a scored run.
