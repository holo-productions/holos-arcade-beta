    📖 Burger Builder — Rules

    🎯 Goal

    Serve as many customers as you can by stacking each burger to match its order ticket exactly — same layers, same order, bottom to top.

    🍔 Building

    • Tap or click an ingredient to add it on top of your stack.
    • Every order starts with a Bottom Bun and ends with a Top Bun.
    • Undo removes the top layer. Clear empties the whole stack. Both are free — no penalty.
    • You can stack up to 12 layers. Extra taps beyond that are ignored.

    🔔 Serving & scoring

    • Perfect serve: 100 points + time bonus (2 points per second left) + streak bonus (10 × current streak).
    • Wrong stack: no points, streak resets, and you lose 1 heart in Arcade mode.
    • Timer runs out: the customer leaves, streak resets, and you lose 1 heart in Arcade mode.
    • Arcade starts with 3 hearts. Lose them all and your shift ends.

    📈 Levels & difficulty

    • Level up every 5 burgers served. Higher levels = more layers per order and less time per order.
    • Easy: 3–5 middle layers max growth, generous timer, all 12 ingredients available from the start but orders stay simple.
    • Normal: balanced orders and timer.
    • Hard: taller orders (double patties and cheese show up), the shortest timer, and the biggest score multiplier (×1.5, rounded).
    • Score multipliers: Easy ×0.8 · Normal ×1 · Hard ×1.5.

    🧪 Practice mode

    • No hearts are ever lost, the run never ends, and the timer is extra relaxed.
    • Practice scores are never saved to your best scores — Arcade only.

    🏆 Best scores

    Your best Arcade score is saved on this device for each difficulty separately. Your chosen difficulty is remembered for next time, too.

    ♿ Good to know

    • Works with touch, mouse, and keyboard focus — every control is a real button.
    • Sound is made live with WebAudio (no audio files). Turn it off any time up top.
    • No real-money anything. Just burgers.
