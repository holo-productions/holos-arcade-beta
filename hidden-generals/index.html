    How to play

    1 · The hidden army

    Each side fields 24 pieces on the 8×8 field, but ranks are secret. Your pieces face you; the enemy's show only a star. The two blue lakes in the middle are impassable — no piece can enter or cross them.

    Your one job: find and capture the enemy Flag before they capture yours.

    2 · Set up your defence

    Before the battle, tap any two of your pieces to swap their places, or press Auto-place for a ready-made formation. Classic trick: tuck your Flag in a back corner and ring it with Bombs — then keep a Miner nearby in case theirs does the same.

    When you're happy, press Start battle.

    3 · Moving

    Tap one of your pieces to see where it can go, then tap a highlighted square. Most pieces move one square up, down, left, or right. Scouts slide any distance in a straight line. Flags and Bombs never move.

    Move onto an enemy piece to attack it — there's no retreating from a fight once you step in.

    4 · Battle rules

    When pieces clash, both are revealed. The lower number wins: a General (1) beats a Colonel (2), who beats a Major (3), and so on down to the Spy (9). Equal ranks destroy each other.

    • Bombs destroy anyone who attacks them — except Miners (7), who defuse them.
    • The Spy (9) loses to everyone… except when it attacks the General (1) — then the Spy wins.
    • Revealed enemy pieces stay marked, so remember what you've learned.

    5 · Winning

    Capture the enemy Flag to win instantly. You also win if the enemy is left with no piece that can move. Lose your Flag — or your last movable piece — and the battle is theirs.

    Tip: on Hard, the enemy commander remembers every piece you've revealed and hunts with a plan. In Practice mode the enemy ranks are face-up so you can learn the game without risking your streak.

    Setup

    • Your army starts on your three back rows; the enemy mirrors you across the lakes.
    • Swap any two of your pieces by tapping them, or use Auto-place. Press Start battle when ready.

    Moving

    • Players alternate turns, one move per turn. You move first.
    • Pieces move one square up, down, left, or right — never diagonally. Scouts may move any number of squares in a straight line but cannot jump over pieces or lakes.
    • The two lakes (2×2 each) are impassable: no piece may enter or pass through them.
    • Flags and Bombs never move.
    • Moving onto an enemy piece starts a battle. You cannot move onto your own piece.

    Battles

    • Both pieces are revealed when they fight, and a revealed piece stays known to the side that saw it.
    • The lower rank wins; the loser is removed. Equal ranks remove each other.
    • Attacking a Bomb destroys the attacker, unless the attacker is a Miner — the Miner defuses it and takes the square.
    • A Spy that attacks the General wins. A General that attacks the Spy wins as usual (rank 1 beats rank 9).
    • If the defender wins, the defender stays put. If the attacker wins, it takes the defender's square.

    Winning

    • Capture the enemy Flag to win immediately.
    • A side left with no movable piece that has a legal move loses.
    • You may resign at any time during a battle.

    Modes & difficulty

    • Practice mode: enemy ranks are face-up. Practice games never change your wins or streak.
    • Easy: the enemy commander moves carelessly and forgets what it has seen.
    • Normal: the enemy remembers revealed pieces and fights sensibly.
    • Hard: the enemy remembers everything, probes with Scouts and its Spy, sends Miners at known Bombs, and marches on your Flag once it's found.
