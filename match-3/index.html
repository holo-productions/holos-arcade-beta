How to play Match-3

Swap gems, line up 3 or more, chase the cascades.

  1. 1
    Tap a gem, then tap a neighbourTap any gem to select it (it gets a gold ring), then tap a gem directly above, below, left or right of it to swap them. Tap the selected gem again to deselect.
  2. 2
    Line up 3 or more of a kindIf the swap makes a row or column of 3+ matching gems, they pop and score. If it doesn't make a match, the gems swap right back and no move is used.
  3. 3
    Watch the gems fallGems above drop down and new gems fall in from the top. If the falling gems line up again, that's a cascade — and each cascade in a row multiplies your points (×2, ×3…).
  4. 4
    Make special gemsMatch 4 to create a glowing line gem — match it later and it blasts its whole row or column. Match 5 to create a colour bomb — swap it with any gem to pop every gem of that colour.
  5. 5
    Pick your mode — or practise firstPractice: a warm-up round with no timer, no move limit and no target — scores don't count toward your best, so experiment freely. Moves: reach the target before your moves run out. Timed: score as much as you can before the clock ends (4- and 5-matches add bonus time). Zen: no clock, no move limit — play until you tap End game.
  6. 6
    Pick your difficultyEasy: 5 gem types (matches are easier to find), lower target, more moves & more time. Normal: 6 gem types, 2,000 points in 30 moves or 90 seconds. Hard: 6 gem types, higher target, fewer moves & less time. Changing difficulty starts a fresh game — your best is saved separately for each mode + difficulty.
  7. 7
    Stuck?Tap Hint and two gems that can make a match will flash. If the board ever has no possible matches, it shuffles itself for free.

Match-3 — Rules

The full rulebook: scoring, specials and win conditions.

The board

The board is 8 × 8. On Normal and Hard there are 6 gem types — ruby (red circle), sapphire (blue diamond), emerald (green triangle), amber (orange square), amethyst (purple hexagon) and opal (teal drop). On Easy there are 5 (no opal), so matches are easier to find. Each gem has its own shape as well as its colour, so the game never depends on telling colours apart.

How a turn works

  • Tap a gem, then tap an orthogonally adjacent gem (up, down, left or right — never diagonal) to swap them.
  • A swap only counts if it creates at least one match of 3 or more in a row or column. Otherwise the gems swap back and no move is used and no time bonus or penalty applies.
  • Matched gems pop, gems above fall to fill the gaps, and new gems drop in from the top.
  • If falling gems form new matches, they pop too. Each chain reaction is a cascade and scores at a higher multiplier (see Scoring).
  • Only one swap can be in progress at a time — wait for the board to settle before swapping again.

Scoring

EventPoints
Each gem popped10
Making a 4-match (bonus)+40
Making a 5-match (bonus)+100
Colour bomb detonation (bonus)+50
Cascade multiplier×1, then ×2, ×3… per chain step

Every point scored in one chain step — gems, bonuses and blasts — is multiplied by that step's cascade multiplier. The first step of a swap is ×1; if the falling gems match again it's ×2, then ×3, and so on until the board settles.

Special gems

  • Line gem (match 4): one gem in the match survives and becomes a glowing line gem of the same type. A horizontal 4-match makes a row-blaster; a vertical 4-match makes a column-blaster. When a line gem is part of any later match — or is caught in another blast — it also pops every gem in its row or column. Each of those gems scores the usual 10 points.
  • Colour bomb (match 5, or an L/T-shaped match): a dark sparkling bomb that matches nothing by itself. Swap it with any normal gem and it detonates: the bomb and every gem of that gem's colour pop. Swapping two colour bombs together pops the entire board.
  • A special gem caught in a line blast goes off too, so blasts can chain.

On Easy there are only 5 gem types, so matches and cascades happen more often. In Zen, difficulty only changes the number of gem types — there is still no clock or move limit.

Modes & win conditions

  • 🎯 Moves: Only successful swaps count as moves. Reach the target for your difficulty (see table above) before the moves run out to win. If the moves run out below the target, the game ends — your score still counts toward your best for that mode + difficulty.
  • ⏱️ Timed: You have the time shown for your difficulty (Easy 120s · Normal 90s · Hard 60s). There is no target — beat your own best score. Making a 4-match adds +2 seconds and a 5-match adds +5 seconds. The game ends when the clock hits zero.
  • 🌿 Zen: No clock and no move limit on any difficulty. Play as long as you like and tap 🏁 End game when you're done; your score counts toward your Zen best for that difficulty.
  • 🎓 Practice: A learning / warm-up round. No clock, no move limit and no target on any difficulty — difficulty only changes how many gem types are on the board. Use Hint as much as you like, try out line gems and colour bombs, and tap 🏁 End practice when you're done. Practice scores are never saved as a best and don't affect any other mode.

Dead boards & hints

  • If no possible match remains, the board shuffles itself automatically, for free, until a move exists. Shuffling never creates matches on its own.
  • The 💡 Hint button flashes two gems that can make a match. Hints are free and unlimited in every mode.
  • Your best score for each mode + difficulty is saved on this device.
