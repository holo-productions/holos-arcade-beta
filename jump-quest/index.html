How to Play — Tutorial

1
Pick Level

Pick a level from Level Select — clear one to unlock the next.

2
Move

Arrow keys / A-D on keyboard, or ◀ ▶ buttons on touch.

3
Jump

Space, W, ↑ or the JUMP button — hold longer for a higher jump.

4
Stomp

Land on a snail or beetle from above (+20) — side touch costs a life.

5
Collect

Grab Coins (+10) and Stars (+50) — watch the timer!

6
Checkpoints

Run past the small flag — you will respawn there if you fall.

7
Finish

Reach the big flag to clear the level.

8
Boss Fight

Stomp the Big Beetle 3 times — avoid its charge!

Rules

  • Lives: Easy 5 • Normal 3 • Hard 2. Practice = unlimited.
  • Timer: Easy 300s • Normal 200s • Hard 140s per level. Time out = lose a life & restart from checkpoint.
  • Enemy speed: Easy 0.6× • Normal 1× • Hard 1.5×.
  • Scoring: Coin 10, Star 50, Stomp 20, Level clear 200 + time bonus. Practice scores are never saved.
  • Hazards: Spikes and pits cost a life instantly.
  • Unlocks: Clear a level to unlock the next. Bests (most coins, fastest time) are saved on this device.
  • Boss: Big Beetle needs 3 stomps. Between stomps it speeds up.
