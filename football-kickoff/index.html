Rules - Football Kickoff

Goal

Score as many points as you can in 10 kicks. Kicks start short and get longer each time. Make kicks in a row to build a streak bonus.

How a kick works

  • Check the wind badge and distance badge at the top of the field. Wind pushes the ball left or right while it flies.
  • The aim needle sweeps left and right. Press the big button or Space to lock it. Center is straight. The green zone is the safe window.
  • Then the power bar fills and empties. Press again to kick. Longer kicks need more power. The green zone shows the sweet spot for this distance.
  • The ball flies. If it passes between the uprights and over the crossbar, it is good. Short, wide, or off the post is a miss.
