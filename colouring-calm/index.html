How to Play

Colouring Calm is simple on purpose: pick a colour, tap a shape, and it fills. There's no timer and no wrong answer.

1
Pick detail level

Gentle is big shapes, Challenge is fine detail. Change any time.

2
Pick a mode

Colour & Complete tracks your best. Practice tracks nothing.

3
Pick a colour

Tap a colour circle, or use “Mix your own” for any colour.

4
Erase a shape

The white eraser circle clears a shape back to blank.

5
Tap to fill

Tap a shape to fill it. Tap again with a new colour to change it.

6
Save favourites

“Save to gallery” keeps finished pages on this device.

Rules

  • Tap a shape to fill it with the colour you've picked. Tap it again with a new colour to change it, or use the eraser to clear it.
  • Undo steps back one fill at a time. Clear page blanks the whole page (Undo can bring it back).
  • Detail levels: Gentle, Normal, and Challenge show different amounts of detail on the same picture. Your level choice is saved on this device.
  • Colour & Complete mode tracks the percentage coloured and saves your best percentage for each page and level. Practice mode tracks nothing — colour freely.
  • Saved pages and work in progress are stored only in this browser on this device (localStorage). Clearing your browser data will remove them.
  • There are no timers, no scores to beat, and no losing. Colour a little or a lot — it's up to you.
  • All pictures are original line art drawn for this game. No outside images are used.
