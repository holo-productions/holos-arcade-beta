📖 Rules - Galaxy Invaders

🚀 Goal

Blast every invader in the wave before they reach your ship line. Clear waves to score big. The game ends when you run out of ships or the invaders land.

🕹️ How to play

  • Move with the on-screen arrows, drag on the play field, or use the Left and Right arrow keys (or A and D).
  • Fire with the FIRE button, tap on the play field, or press Space. Hold to keep firing.
  • 🎮 Controller: left stick / D-pad to move, hold A to fire.
  • Invaders march side to side. Each time they hit an edge, the whole formation drops one step closer.
  • The fewer invaders left, the faster they march. Invaders also shoot back.
  • Hide behind the four shields. Shields block shots from both sides and crumble as they take hits. Shields rebuild every wave.
  • A bonus ship sometimes crosses the top. Tag it for bonus points.
  • Use Pause any time. Use End round to finish early and see your summary.
