📖 Rules: Diner Rush

🎯 Goal

Earn as much as you can before the shift timer ends. Serve seated guests their combo orders correctly and quickly to earn the combo price plus tips. Guests who run out of patience leave and cost you a small penalty.

🍽️ How service works

  • Guests sit at one of four tables. Each guest has an order ticket pinned at the top.
  • Tap a guest or their ticket to select that order. The tray title shows who you are serving.
  • Tap ingredients in the exact order listed on the ticket. The ticket crosses off each correct pick and highlights the next one.
  • A wrong tap buzzes and clears the tray for that order. Read the ticket order carefully.
  • When the tray is full and correct, press Serve tray. You get the price plus a tip based on patience left.
  • Use Clear tray to restart an order. Use End round any time to finish early.

How this differs from Kitchen Rush

Kitchen Rush is about chopping and cooking one dish at a time on a counter. Diner Rush is about seated guests, pinned order tickets, and building combo trays by tapping ingredients in order. No chopping, no stove, just fast and accurate tray building.
