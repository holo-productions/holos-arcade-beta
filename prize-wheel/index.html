How to play ▾

The idea: Spin the carnival wheel and win play-money tickets. Spend tickets on pretend prizes for your shelf. That's it — pure luck, zero skill, zero money.

Spinning: Tap the big red SPIN! button. The wheel spins for about 5 seconds, then the pointer shows what you won. Tickets are added to your balance straight away.

Getting spins: You get 1 free daily spin every day (tap Claim daily spin). You can also earn spins by landing on a Free Spin segment, by hitting ticket milestones (every 100 lifetime tickets = +1 spin), or by trading 50 tickets for a spin. New players start with 3 spins to get going.

Wheel styles (saved automatically): Prize Wheel is a game of luck, so there's no skill difficulty — instead you pick a wheel style. 😌 Casual has 8 friendly segments with no duds. 🎡 Classic has 12 segments with a mix of small wins, one dud, and a jackpot. 🔥 Carnival Chaos has 16 segments with bigger jackpots but more duds. Your pick is remembered next visit.

Practice mode: Tap 🎯 Practice any time for unlimited spins that don't use your spins, don't keep tickets, and don't touch your bests. Perfect for just enjoying the spin.

Prize shelf: Spend tickets on pretend prizes below the wheel. Owned prizes stay on your shelf. Tickets and prizes have no cash value and can't be exchanged for anything real.

Rules ▾

Play-money only. All tickets, spins, and prizes in Prize Wheel are pretend. They have no cash value, cannot be bought, sold, transferred, or exchanged for money, goods, or anything of value, and no purchase is ever needed or offered. This is not gambling — nothing is wagered and nothing of value can be won or lost.

How a spin works. Each spin picks one segment at random (weighted — smaller prizes come up more often, jackpots are rare). The wheel animation then lands on that segment. Results are pure chance; timing your tap doesn't change the outcome.

Spins. One daily spin can be claimed per calendar day on this device. Earned spins come from Free Spin segments, lifetime-ticket milestones (every 100 tickets earned = +1 spin), and trading 50 current tickets for 1 spin. Spins don't expire. Practice spins are unlimited and free.

Wheel styles. Casual (8 segments, no duds, modest prizes), Classic (12 segments, one dud, one jackpot), Carnival Chaos (16 segments, three duds, biggest jackpot). Switching style is free and takes effect on the next spin; your choice is saved on this device.

Tickets & prizes. Tickets won on scored (non-practice) spins go into your balance. Prize-shelf items cost tickets and, once bought, are yours to keep on this device. Buying a prize spends tickets — it never costs real money.

Bests (saved on this device). Your bests track total scored spins, lifetime tickets earned, biggest single-spin win, jackpot count, Free Spin count, and prizes collected. Practice spins never change your bests. Use Reset my Prize Wheel data to clear everything.

Fair play. Everything is stored only in your browser on this device. Clearing browser data resets the game. If the wheel ever looks stuck, refresh the page — your tickets, spins, and shelf are saved.
