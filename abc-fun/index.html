Rules

  • Each round has 6 questions. Tap one of the 4 big answers for each question.
  • Tap the yellow speaker any time to hear the question read aloud. You can listen as many times as you like.
  • If an answer isn't right yet, you'll get a friendly hint and can try again. There are no timers, no losing, and no points taken away.
  • A correct answer earns 1 star. Finishing all 6 questions earns 1 bonus star.
  • Practice rounds (the “Practise” buttons on the home screen) are just for learning: every question's hint is showing from the start, and no stars are counted — nothing is saved. Play them as often as you like, then switch to a regular round when you're ready to earn stars.
  • Stars are counted while you play on this device and start fresh when the page is closed — they're just for celebrating, not for competing.
  • You can change levels or switch games at any time from the home screen. Your stars for this visit stay counted.
  • There is no chat, no accounts, no ads, and no links that leave this page. Nothing about the player is collected or typed in.

For grown-ups

ABC Fun practises letter recognition and phonics for the youngest players, then grows with them: blends and word families in Grades 1–3, spelling patterns with prefixes and suffixes in Grades 4–6, Greek and Latin word roots in Grades 7–9, and advanced academic vocabulary in Grades 10–12. Encouragement is always gentle — a missed answer gets a hint and another try, never a penalty.

For Grown-Ups

ABC Fun is a practice playground, not a test. Kids pick a level, play short rounds of 6 questions, and earn stars for correct answers. Wrong answers bring a hint and another try — the tone stays encouraging at every level, including the Grade 10–12 vocabulary rounds.
