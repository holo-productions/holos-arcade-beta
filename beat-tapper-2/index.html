How to play

1
Pick Difficulty

Easy, Normal or Hard. Your choice is remembered.

2
Press Start

Hear a 4-beat count-in, then the beat loop begins.

3
Tap The Beat

Tap the TAP! pad or Space when the rings meet and the kick hits.

4
Build Combo

Perfect or Good taps build combo. A miss resets it.

5
Three Rounds

3 rounds of 16 beats. Tempo creeps up each round.

6
Practice Round

No score saved, steady tempo, TAP NOW! cue and timing hints.

Tip: tap with the kick drum, not after it. Headphones help a lot.

📜 Rules & scoring

Goal

Tap on every beat of the synthesized loop as accurately as you can. Your taps are judged against the exact beat time.

Judgement windows

DifficultyTempo (round 1)Perfect windowGood window
😌 Easy80 BPM±90 ms±180 ms
🎯 Normal100 BPM±65 ms±140 ms
🔥 Hard124 BPM±45 ms±100 ms

A tap inside the Perfect window scores Perfect; inside the Good window scores Good. A tap outside both windows, or no tap before the Good window closes, is a Miss. Each beat can be scored only once — extra taps don't count.

Scoring

JudgementBase pointsCombo effect
Perfect100Combo +1
Good50Combo +1
Miss0Combo resets to 0

Combo bonus: every 8 combo adds +1× multiplier step to your tap points (×1 at 0–7 combo, ×2 at 8–15, ×3 at 16–23, ×4 at 24+). Accuracy = (Perfect × 1 + Good × 0.5) ÷ total beats judged.

Rounds

A scored game is 3 rounds of 16 beats (4 bars of 4). The tempo rises by 4 BPM each round. After round 3 your final score is compared with your saved best for that difficulty.

Practice round

Practice is just for warming up and learning the beat. It uses the same difficulty tempo and judgement windows, but: no score is saved and your best is never changed, there are no rounds and no end — it plays at one steady tempo until you press Stop — and hints are on (a “TAP NOW!” cue plus early/late timing in milliseconds after each tap). Taps still show Perfect / Good / Miss and combo while you practise, so you can see yourself improving before you play for a score.

Controls

Tap/click the TAP! pad, or press the Space bar. Difficulty and best scores are saved on this device (localStorage).
