Rules - Star Hopper

Goal

Run and jump through 3 levels. Collect stars, avoid or stomp the Glooms, and reach the flag at the end of each level. Clear all 3 levels to win the run. Your score is stars collected plus a time bonus for finishing quickly.

How to move

  • Run with the Left and Right buttons, or the Left and Right arrow keys, or A and D.
  • Jump with the Jump button, or Space, or W, or the Up arrow. Hold jump a touch longer for a higher hop.
  • Land on platforms to cross gaps. Falling into a pit costs a life.

Stars, coins and score

Score is stars plus coins plus stomps plus time bonus. Your best score is saved on this device.

Glooms and lives

  • Glooms patrol back and forth. Land on top of one to stomp it and bounce.
  • Touching a Gloom from the side costs a life. You flash and respawn at the level start.
  • When lives run out, the run ends. Start again from Level 1.

Levels

  • Level 1 Meadow Hop: short gaps and 2 Glooms. Learn to run and jump.
  • Level 2 Sunset Cliffs: higher platforms, wider gaps and 4 Glooms.
  • Level 3 Starlight Peaks: the longest hops and 6 Glooms. Watch your timing.

Practice mode

Untimed, no score saved, and falling just sends you back to the start of the level. Practice is purely for learning the jumps. End any time with End round.
