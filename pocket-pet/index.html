How to play

  1. Adopt: Choose one of 8 pets — cat, dog, bunny, horse, panda, fox, turtle, or owl — pick a fur colour and pattern, and give them a name.
  2. Customize: Any time, open Look & Dress Up to change fur colour/pattern and equip hats, collars, and bows, and Room & Decor for room themes and colours. Everything saves automatically. Items with a Pass badge are premium — locked until the Arcade Pass launches.
  3. Watch the meters: Food, Fun, Clean, and Energy slowly fall. Happiness is the average — keep them all up.
  4. Care: Tap Feed, Play, Groom, or Sleep. Each action earns XP.
  5. Pet your pet: Tap or stroke (drag across) your pet right in their room. They'll react with hearts and a happy sound, and Fun goes up — a few good strokes, then a short cooldown so petting stays special.
  6. Grow: Level up to grow from Baby → Young → Adult. Levelling up also pays coins.
  7. Play mini-games: Catch the Treats, Bubble Pop, and Memory Match earn extra coins and Fun.
  8. Practice first, if you like: Tap the 🎓 Practice Round button inside any mini-game (or the Park Stroll walk) for a single practice round with hints on and generous timers — it switches back to real play automatically when the round ends. Or turn on Practice Mode at the top of the game screen to practise everything, including care actions. Practice is clearly marked, and nothing from it is saved to your pet's progress, coins, bests, or streaks.
  9. Decorate & dress up: Spend coins in the Shop on accessories and furniture, then place them in Room & Decor.
  10. Inventory: Everything you buy — outfits, accessories, and furniture — is stored in your Inventory tab. Equip or unequip outfits there, and place furniture in the room or back into storage, any time.
  11. Seasonal outfits: The Shop's Seasonal section sells Halloween, Christmas, Spring, and Valentine's outfits for coins. Once bought, a seasonal outfit is yours forever in your Inventory — wearable all year.
  12. Go places: In Places & Walk, visit the Park, Beach, Vet, and Pet Shop, teach tricks at Training, and take your pet for a Park Stroll walk to collect items.
  13. Come back daily: Visiting on consecutive calendar days builds your care streak and pays a daily bonus. While you're away meters drift gently — capped, never below 25, and your pet never gets sick.

Rules

  • Meters: Food, Fun, Clean, and Energy run 0–100. At 0 your pet is miserable — Happiness drops fast.
  • Real-time & away: Meters also change in real time while the game is closed, based on how long you were away — but gently: total away drop is capped (Food −30 max, Fun −20, Energy −20, Clean −15) and absence alone never takes a meter below 25. Your pet can never die or get sick from absence.
  • Places: Park (Fun +15), Beach (Fun +20, Clean −10), Vet checkup (20 coins, Clean +10 + health report), Pet Shop (spend earned coins on food/accessories/furniture), Training (Energy −10, teaches one trick per session).
  • Walks: Park Stroll lasts 40 seconds, needs Energy 20+. Collect items for coins; finishing gives Fun +18, Energy −12, Hunger −5.
  • Customization: Fur colours, patterns, accessories, and room themes are saved with your pet. Free basics are available now; items with a Pass badge are premium previews — locked, not purchasable yet.
  • Inventory & seasonal: All owned outfits, accessories, and furniture are stored in the Inventory tab (equip/unequip outfits; place/remove furniture). Coins are the game's fun currency, earned by care and mini-games — never real money, and nothing in the Shop can be bought with cash. Seasonal outfits (Halloween, Christmas, Spring, Valentine's), once bought or earned, are kept in your Inventory forever.
  • Petting: Tap/stroke your pet for hearts, a reaction, and a happy sound. Each stroke gives Fun +2, up to 5 strokes (+10) — then petting cools down for 60 seconds (hearts still show, no more boost) so it can't be spammed for infinite happiness. Cooldown is saved, so reloading doesn't reset it.
  • Difficulty: Easy / Normal / Hard changes how fast meters fall. Hard gives +50% care XP. You can change it any time; it's saved.
  • XP & levels: Care actions give 8–14 XP. Mini-games give XP for finishing. Level = every 100 XP. Stages: Baby (Lv 1–2), Young (Lv 3–5), Adult (Lv 6+).
  • Coins: Daily bonus 20 coins (+5 per streak day, max +50), level-up 50 coins, mini-games pay your score. Food treats, accessories, and furniture cost coins — Everyday Kibble is always free.
  • Sleep: While sleeping, Energy refills quickly and other meters fall more slowly. Wake your pet to feed or play.
  • Streaks: A streak counts consecutive calendar days you open Pocket Pet with a pet adopted. Missing a day resets it to 1.
  • Practice Mode & Practice Rounds: Optional and clearly marked with a gold banner. Each mini-game and the Park Stroll walk has a 🎓 Practice Round button that starts one practice round and returns to normal play automatically when it ends; the Practice Mode toggle at the top practises everything until you turn it off. In Practice, mini-games use generous timers (Catch 90s, Bubble Pop 60s, Walk 90s), hints are shown, rocks and puddles cost no points, Memory cards flash face-up at the start, and care actions/places show what they would do without charging or changing anything. Practice results are never saved: no coins, XP, bests, meter changes, tricks, or streak effects. Turning Practice off returns to normal play exactly as it was.
  • Saving: Your pet, room, accessories, difficulty, and best scores are saved on this device only.
  • Starting over: “Start over with a new pet” deletes the current pet permanently.
