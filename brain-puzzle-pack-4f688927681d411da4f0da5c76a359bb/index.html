Rules

How scoring works

Each correct answer = 100 points, plus a time bonus if you finish fast. A streak of 3+ adds +25 per answer. Wrong answers break your streak but never end the run — you always finish all rounds.

The 5 puzzles

  • Odd One Out: 3 cards share a rule, 1 doesn't. Tap the one that doesn't belong.
  • Sequence Solver: Find the missing number in the pattern.
  • Spot the Difference: Two scenes look alike — tap every cell in the right scene that is different. Find them all to clear the round.
  • Pattern Pop: A shape / colour pattern repeats — pick what comes next.
  • Clue Crack: Read the clue and pick the only answer it describes.

Difficulty

  • Easy: 5 rounds • 90 seconds • simpler patterns, 3 differences to find.
  • Normal: 7 rounds • 75 seconds • trickier distractors, 4 differences.
  • Hard: 10 rounds • 60 seconds • two-step sequences & sneaky odd-ones, 5 differences.

Mix Mode

Play All 5 shuffles one round from each puzzle type, then keeps cycling until the round count for your difficulty is done.

🧪 Practice Round

Practice is the pressure-free way to learn a puzzle. Pick 🧪 Practice on any card, or start a Practice Mix from the top of the home screen.

  • No timer — the clock is off, so you can think it through.
  • No score — nothing is saved, and your best scores & streak records don't change.
  • Hints on every round — tap 💡 Hint for a nudge toward the rule, and Show why after you answer to see the full explanation.
  • Try again — a wrong tap doesn't end anything; keep trying, or tap Skip → to move on.
  • Same number of rounds as your difficulty. When you're ready, tap Play for Score → to switch to a normal scored round.

Best scores are saved on this device per puzzle + difficulty. No account needed.
