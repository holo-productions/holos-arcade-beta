How to play

Tap a tube to pick it up, then tap another tube to pour. You can only pour onto the same colour or into an empty tube, and only as many balls as fit. Sort every colour into its own tube to win.

Stuck? Use Undo, Restart, or add an extra tube (it costs points).

Rules

  • Each tube holds 4 balls.
  • Pour only onto a matching top colour or an empty tube.
  • Win when every tube is empty or full of one colour.
  • Fewer moves = more points. Extra tubes cost 100 points each (max 2 per level).
  • Practice mode never saves to your best.
