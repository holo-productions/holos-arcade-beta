Rules

  • Words must be 3 or more letters long.
  • Letters only. No numbers, spaces, or symbols.
  • No repeats. A word already used in the round cannot be played again.
  • Each new word must start with the last letter of the previous word.
  • First word of a round can start with any letter.
  • Invalid word: you get 1 strike and keep your turn. 3 strikes loses the round.
  • Timer: 30 seconds per turn when on. Time out loses the round. You can turn the timer off.
  • vs Computer: the computer picks a valid word from its built-in list. If it has no valid word, it loses the round.
  • Give Up Round ends the round and gives the win to the other player.
