Rules

The game

  • You are the duck at the bottom. Hop across the road, the safe bank, and the river to reach a home nest at the top.
  • Fill all 5 nests to win the day. You have 3 lives.

Play

  • Hop one cell at a time with arrow keys, WASD, a swipe, the on-screen arrows, or a controller D-pad / left stick.
  • The road has 3 lanes of cars and trucks moving at different speeds and in different directions. Touching one costs a life.
  • The river has 3 lanes of logs. You must stand on a log — a log carries you along, and drifting off the edge or landing in open water costs a life.
  • The bank (between road and river) and the grass at the bottom are safe — no traffic, no water.
  • Only the 5 nests count as home. Hopping into the gaps between nests is blocked — aim for a nest.
  • Each safe trip scores 100 points plus a time bonus (faster trip, bigger bonus). Traffic gets a little faster after every trip.

Difficulty

  • Easy: slow traffic, wide gaps between vehicles, slow logs.
  • Medium: a fair crossing — moderate speed and density.
  • Hard: fast, dense traffic and quick logs.
  • You can change difficulty between games — it takes effect on the next game. Your choice is remembered for next time.

Practice round

  • Tap 🏋 Practice round to switch to a practice session at any time.
  • No score is kept, the clock doesn't run, and lives are endless — a splash or a bump just puts you back at the start.
  • Practice trips never count toward your bests. Tap the button again to return to a real game.

Bests

  • Best score, days won, safe trips, and games played are stored on this device only.
