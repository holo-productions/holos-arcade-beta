Ready to pounce?

Move Gizmo toward the bouncing pom poms to catch them. Chain catches quickly to build your combo — and tap Pounce for a burst of speed when a golden pom appears.

Pounce

How to move: Mouse or Touch — Gizmo follows your pointer  ·  ←↑↓→ or W A S D  ·  Space to pounce  ·  P to pause. On phones, drag on the rug and use the big Pounce button.

How to play

  1. 1
    Chase the pom poms. Purple poms bounce around the rug. Touch one with Gizmo to catch it. A new one bounces in straight away — they get faster every level.
  2. 2
    Build your combo. Catch another pom within 3 seconds and your multiplier climbs: ×2, ×3, up to ×5. Let it lapse and you're back to ×1.
  3. 3
    Pounce the golden pom. Golden poms are rare, fast, and worth 50 × your combo. Hit Pounce (2-second cooldown) to lunge for them.
  4. 4
    Dodge the yarn. Rolling yarn balls tangle Gizmo up — touching one slows her for 2 seconds and breaks your combo. Fish treats are safe: +15 points and +2 seconds.

Practice round

New to the chase, or just warming up? Pick Practice in the Round selector above the rug — or tap 🧪 Practice — for a round with no timer. You still chase poms, build combos, pounce and move through the rooms at your chosen difficulty, but the points are practice-only: they don't count toward your best score, the Chase log, or achievements. Tap ■ End practice whenever you're done, then switch back to Scored for a real 60-second round.

Rooms & levels

Every 8 catches you level up and the chase moves rooms: Sunbeam Rug → Yarn Basket Corner → Sofa Fort → Window Ledge → The Whole House. Poms get quicker and yarn gets busier each room.

Game rules 📋

The full rules for a round — separate from the Tutorial, so you can check scoring and difficulty any time without replaying the walkthrough.

  1. 1
    Goal & round length. One round is 60 seconds. Catch pom poms to score as many points as you can before the timer reaches zero. There is no losing and no lives — your final score is your result, and your best score this visit is tracked in the HUD.
  2. 2
    Scoring. Purple pom 10 points, teal pom 20 points, golden pom 50 points — each multiplied by your current combo. Fish treat 15 points (not multiplied) plus +2 seconds on the clock. A golden pom disappears after about 7 seconds if you don't catch it.
  3. 3
    Combo rule. Catch another pom within 3 seconds to raise your combo: ×2, ×3, ×4, up to ×5. If 3 seconds pass without a catch, the combo resets to ×1. Touching a yarn ball also resets it to ×1.
  4. 4
    Yarn & pounce. Rolling red yarn balls are hazards: touching one tangles Gizmo, slows her for 2 seconds, and resets your combo — it does not end the round. Pounce is a speed burst with a 2-second cooldown; it doesn't change scoring, it just helps you reach a pom in time.
  5. 5
    Levels & rooms. Every 8 catches you level up and move rooms: Sunbeam Rug → Yarn Basket Corner → Sofa Fort → Window Ledge → The Whole House. Each level, pom poms move faster and yarn appears more often. Your level at the end of the round is recorded in the Chase log.
  6. 6
    Difficulty. Pick Cozy, Playful, or Zoomies above the rug before you play. Cozy: slower poms, max 2 yarn balls, gentlest chase. Playful (standard): normal pom speed, max 3 yarn balls. Zoomies: fastest poms, max 5 yarn balls, most frequent yarn. Changing difficulty mid-round selects it for your next round; starting a new round applies it fully. Difficulty changes speed and yarn only — points, combo, and the 60-second limit stay the same on every setting.
  7. 7
    Pause & this visit only. Press P or Pause to pause — the timer stops while paused. Scores, achievements, and the Chase log live only for this visit and reset when you close the page.
  8. 8
    Practice rounds. Choose Practice in the Round selector (or the Practice button) before starting. A practice round has no timer and runs until you tap End practice. Catches, combos, levels, and difficulty work exactly as in a scored round so you can learn the game, but practice points never count: no best score, no Chase log entry, and no achievements. Switch back to Scored for a normal 60-second round. Changing the Round selector mid-round applies to your next round.
