📖 Rules - Kings Cup Party

🎯 Goal

Take turns drawing cards from a shuffled deck. Each rank has a party rule. When someone draws a King, they add a pour to the centre cup. The round ends when the last King is drawn (the 4th King in a full deck), and that player may take a sip from the centre cup if they want to.

🥤 Play with water or soda

Every rule works with water, soda, juice, or any drink you like. Nobody has to drink alcohol to play, and every sip in this game is optional. Swap in a snack, a dance move, or a silly face instead if that suits the table better.

💧 Drink responsibly

Drink responsibly. Know your limits. Water counts too. Eat food, pace yourself, look out for your friends, and plan a safe ride home. This game is for adults 18 and older.

❓ How to play

A quick tour, then the deck is yours:

1
Set the Table

Pick deck size and players, put an empty cup in the middle, then press Start round.

2
Draw and Read

Player 1 taps Draw a card and reads the rule aloud; sips are optional and water or soda counts.

3
Pass the Device

After each draw, hand the device to the next player around the table.

4
Kings Pour

Every King adds a pour to the centre cup.

5
Last King Ends

The last King ends the round; that player may sip from the centre cup, then shuffle and go again.

6
Party Tip

New group? Start with free draw mode to learn the rules with zero pressure.
