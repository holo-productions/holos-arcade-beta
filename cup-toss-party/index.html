📖 Rules - Cup Toss Party

🎯 Goal

Toss the ball into the triangle of cups and clear as many as you can before your tosses run out. Clear the whole triangle for a big finish bonus.

🏀 How a toss works

  • The gold aim marker sweeps side to side under the cups. Watch it move.
  • Tap TOSS THE BALL to lock your aim. The ball flies to the spot you picked.
  • Land dead centre on a cup and it clears. Land close and you might still splash it. Land far off and the ball bounces away.
  • The dashed outline shows the cup nearest your aim marker, so you can time your toss.
  • In two player mode, players take turns after every toss. Pass the device each time.

❓ How to play

A quick tour, then the table is yours:

1️⃣ Pick a difficulty and start

Easy gives you 6 cups and a slow aim marker. Hard gives you 15 cups, a fast marker, and double points. Your pick is remembered for next time. Press Start round.

2️⃣ Watch the aim marker

The gold marker sweeps side to side under the cups. The dashed outline shows which cup you are lined up with. Time your tap so the marker sits right under a cup.

3️⃣ Toss it

Hit the big TOSS THE BALL button. Dead centre clears the cup for sure. Close calls can still splash in. Wild tosses bounce away and break your streak.

4️⃣ Chase the streak

Every cup in a row adds bonus points. Clear the whole triangle for a 50 point bonus. In two player mode, pass the device after every toss.

💡 Party tip

Warm up in Practice mode first, it has unlimited tosses and a slower marker. And remember, the cups on your real table can hold water or soda. Hydration is a power move.
