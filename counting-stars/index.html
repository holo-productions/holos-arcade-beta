Rules

The game

  • Each round has 10 questions.
  • Tap an answer to choose it. Wrong answers are never punished — you get a hint and another try.
  • 2 stars for a first-try answer, 1 star after another try. Stars add up in your star bank and are saved on this device.
  • No timers, no lives, no losing. Play at your own pace.

Practice rounds

  • Choose Practice Round above the game to play a learning round instead of a stars round. You can switch back to Play for Stars any time — switching starts a fresh round.
  • Practice rounds never add to or take away from your star bank, and nothing about them is saved.
  • Hints are always showing in practice, and the “Peek at the answer” button reveals the right answer when you're stuck.
  • At the end of a practice round you see how many of the 10 questions you got right, so you know when you're ready to play for stars.
