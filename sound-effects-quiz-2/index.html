How to play

This is the quick start. For full scoring and win conditions, open Rules.

  • 1. Pick a difficulty, then press Start quiz.
  • 2. Press the big ▶ play button and listen to the mystery sound.
  • 3. Pick what you think made the sound from the choices below.
  • 4. Stuck? Use Hear it again — up to 3 replays per sound, no penalty.
  • 5. Correct answers build your streak for bonus points. 10 sounds per game.
  • Just warming up? Pick Practice mode on the setup screen: endless sounds, unlimited replays, no score — plus a tap-to-hear library of all 12 sounds so you can learn them first.

🔊 Turn your volume up — and if you hear nothing, check the Sound on button at the top and your device's silent switch.

Rules — Sound Effects Quiz

Listen to a mystery sound generated live in your browser, then choose what made it. There are 10 sounds per game and 12 possible sounds in the pool: doorbell, alarm clock, phone ringtone, applause, rain, car horn, microwave beeps, camera shutter, referee whistle, thunder, door knock, and water drip.

Scoring

Difficulty — the arcade standard

LevelWhat changes
Easy3 choices. Wrong options come from very different sound families, so they're easy to rule out.
Normal4 choices. A mix of similar and different sounds. The balanced default.
Hard4 choices. Wrong options are sneaky look-alikes from the same sound family (e.g. alarm vs microwave vs phone beeps).

Good to know

  • Practice mode is endless and unscored, with unlimited replays — and the setup screen has a tap-to-hear library of all 12 sounds. Nothing from practice touches your best score.
  • Every sound is synthesized with WebAudio at the moment you press play — no audio files, no recordings.
  • Your difficulty choice and best score are saved on this device (localStorage).
  • Sounds never repeat back-to-back within a game.
  • The Sound on/off button mutes the game; your progress is kept.
