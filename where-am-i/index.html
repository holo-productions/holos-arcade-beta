Rules — Where Am I?

Every place in this game is real, and every clue card was written for this game. No photos, no street views — just words. Clues run from vaguest to most obvious, so early guesses are worth the most.

How a round works

  • A game is 10 rounds. Each round hides one place: a city, a country, or a famous landmark.
  • You start with one clue showing. Tap Reveal another clue whenever you want more — each extra clue lowers what the round is worth.
  • Tap a place name to lock in your guess. One guess per round — right or wrong, the answer and a fact about the place are revealed.
  • Places never repeat within a single game.

Scoring

Hard100 pts−25 pts4, plus a 30-second timer per round
  • Streak bonus: on Normal and Hard, each correct answer in a row adds +10 pts per streak step (2nd in a row +10, 3rd +20, and so on). A wrong answer resets the streak.
  • On Hard, if the timer runs out the round scores 0 and the answer is shown.
  • A wrong guess scores 0 for that round.

Practice round

  • Tap Practice round on the start screen for a single warm-up place — great for learning the ropes or showing a friend.
  • In practice, extra clues are free (the round stays worth 100), there's no timer even on Hard, and you use the same number of choices as your chosen difficulty.
  • Practice doesn't touch your score, streak bests, or best scores — nothing is saved. Play as many practice rounds in a row as you like.
  • When you're warmed up, jump straight from the practice result into the real 10-round trip.

Bests & settings

  • Your best score is kept separately for each difficulty and saved on this device.
  • Your difficulty choice is remembered for next time.
  • Changing difficulty mid-game restarts the current game.
