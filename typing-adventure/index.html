📋 Rules

For players

  • Type the letters, words, or sentences exactly as shown, from left to right.
  • A wrong key never ends the game — just try again. There are no lives and no losing.
  • Stars: finish a round = 1 star · 80% accuracy or better = 2 stars · 95% or better = 3 stars.
  • Grades 7–9 and 10–12: capitals, spaces, and punctuation count — type them just like you see them.
  • Grades 10–12 goal rounds: aim for the WPM and accuracy goal shown above the paragraph. Beating the goal is a bonus, not a pass/fail.
  • You can restart or skip a round any time with the big buttons.
  • Practice Rounds 🧪: pick Practice Round under “Pick how you play” (or the Practice Round button under the keyboard) to warm up on the same words with no timer, no speed score, and hints always on. Practice rounds earn and save no stars and don't change your saved progress — switch back to Star Quest to earn stars again.

Levels at a glance

  • Ages 3–5 — Letter Friends: single letters and very short words, huge type, no timing.
  • Grades 1–3 — Word Explorers: short words and tiny sentences, lower-case friendly.
  • Grades 4–6 — Sentence Quest: longer words, phrases, and simple sentences.
  • Grades 7–9 — Punctuation Pro: full sentences with capitals, commas, quotes, and questions.
  • Grades 10–12 — Speed Master: full paragraphs with WPM + accuracy goals.

For parents & guardians

  • No chat, no accounts, no personal information, no ads, and no external links in this game.
  • Only the chosen level, stars, and progress are saved — on this device only, in this browser.
  • Encouragement is always gentle: wrong answers get a “try again,” never a penalty or a scary sound.
  • Use the Parent Feedback button to rate the game and save your notes as a file on your device.
