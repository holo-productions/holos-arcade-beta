How to play

Memory Trainer is a gentle pattern game. There is no rush — take as long as you like.

1
Tap Start

Tap Start. Tiles light up one by one with a soft note — just watch and listen.

2
Your turn

When it says Your turn, tap the tiles back in the same order.

3
Pattern grows

Get it right and the pattern grows by one step — that’s the next level.

4
Your score

Your score is your longest sequence plus the rounds you cleared.

5
Practice mode

Removes score pressure, keeps hints on (Watch again + Hint), never uses a timer.

6
Difficulty

Changes how fast the pattern plays and how long it starts. Gentle lets you retry.

7
Sound on/off

Turn Sound off any time for a quiet game.

Rules

  • The pattern: tiles light up one at a time. Repeat the exact order by tapping the tiles. Each cleared round adds one more step.
  • Score: longest sequence you repeated correctly + rounds cleared this run. Bests are kept separately for each difficulty.
  • Gentle: slower playback, starts at 2 steps. A wrong tap replays the pattern so you can try again — the run doesn't end.
  • Normal: medium pace, starts at 3 steps. A wrong tap ends the run.
  • Challenge: faster playback, starts at 4 steps. A wrong tap ends the run.
  • Practice mode: no scoring, hints always available (Watch again replays the pattern; Hint glows the next tile), no timer, and a wrong tap never ends the run.
  • Saved on this device: your difficulty, practice and sound choices, and your best scores — all in this browser only.
  • This game is for relaxation and fun. Not medical advice.
