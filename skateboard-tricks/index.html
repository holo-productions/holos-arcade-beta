Rules - Skateboard Tricks

Goal

Roll as far as you can, jump every obstacle, and stack trick points in the air. Your final score is distance points plus trick points. The run ends when you run out of lives in a real run.

How to play

  • Tap Jump (or tap the park, or press Space) to ollie into the air. You can only jump from the ground.
  • While in the air, tap one trick: Kickflip, 360 Spin, or Board Grab. Only one trick per jump counts.
  • Land on flat ground to bank the trick points. Land on a cone, rail, or in a gap and you crash.
  • A crash in a real run costs 1 life and gives brief invincibility so you can recover. Lose all lives and the run ends.
  • Speed increases gradually with distance. Obstacles also come a little more often on higher difficulties.

Obstacles

  • Cone: low and narrow. A short hop clears it.
  • Rail: long and low. Jump early so you clear the whole rail.
  • Gap: a break in the ground. Jump across it and land on the far side.
  • Ramp: a friendly boost. Roll over it for a higher jump and more air time for tricks. Ramps never hurt you.

Practice mode

Endless and untimed. Crashes do not cost lives and there is no game over. It is perfect for learning jump timing. Practice scores do not save. End any time with End run.

Safety note

Real skateboarding needs a helmet and pads and a safe place to practice. This cartoon version keeps the falls silly and painless.

How to play

A quick tour, then you roll:

1. Pick a difficulty and start

Easy starts slower with fewer obstacles. Hard starts fast and gives you fewer lives. Your pick is remembered for next time. Press Start Run to begin rolling.

2. Jump the obstacles

Tap the big Jump button, tap the park, or press Space to ollie. Time it so you clear cones and rails and land on flat ground. Ramps give you extra height.

3. Trick in the air

While you are airborne, tap Kickflip, 360 Spin, or Board Grab. One trick per jump. Bigger tricks pay more but the 360 Spin needs a clean landing just like the others.

4. Land clean and keep rolling

Land on flat ground to bank your trick points. Land on an obstacle and you lose a life. Speed rises with distance, so later jumps need earlier timing.

Pro tip

Do not trick on every jump. If an obstacle is wide, save the trick for a ramp jump where you have more air time. Warm up in Practice mode first, where crashes cost nothing.
