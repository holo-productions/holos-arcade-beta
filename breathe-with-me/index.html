📖 How to play

Breathe With Me is a gentle breathing pacer game. Follow the soft circle and the numbers on screen.

1
Pick Pattern

Box, 4-7-8 Style, or Simple In-Out.

2
Pick Difficulty

Gentle, Normal or Challenge — longer counts as it gets harder.

3
Pick Length

1, 3 or 5 minutes, or Practice with no timer or scoring.

4
Tap Start

Breathe in as the circle grows, hold, then out as it shrinks.

5
Earn Points

10 calm points per breath cycle, 50 bonus for finishing.

6
Sounds On/Off

Toggle gentle tones. Pause or stop anytime — no way to lose.

Tip: sit comfortably, relax your shoulders, and let your breath be easy and natural. If you feel dizzy or uncomfortable, stop and breathe normally.

📋 Rules

  • This game is for relaxation and fun. Not medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.
  • Breathe at a pace that feels comfortable. Never force your breath. Stop anytime if you feel dizzy, light-headed, or uncomfortable, and breathe normally.
  • Practice mode is free breathing with no timer, no points, and no streak tracking.
  • Timed sessions (1/3/5 min) earn calm points: 10 per completed breath cycle, plus a 50-point bonus for finishing the session.
  • Your day streak counts consecutive calendar days with at least one completed timed session.
  • Points, streaks, bests, difficulty, and feedback are saved only on this device (localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Children should play with a grown-up nearby. Don't play while driving or doing anything that needs your full attention.
