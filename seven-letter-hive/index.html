How to play

Tap the letters in the hive (or type on your keyboard) to build a word, then press Enter. Every word must include the golden centre letter and be at least 4 letters long. You can reuse letters as many times as you like.

Use Shuffle to rearrange the outer letters when you're stuck. A word that uses all seven letters is a pangram — worth a big bonus! The hive changes each day, or tap New hive for a fresh set.

Easy gives friendlier letter sets and first-letter hints. Hard uses trickier sets with no hints.

Rules

  • Words must be 4+ letters, use only the 7 hive letters, and include the centre letter.
  • Letters may be reused within a word.
  • Scoring: 4-letter word = 1 point. Longer words = 1 point per letter. Pangram (all 7 letters) = +7 bonus points.
  • Climb the ranks from Beginner to Queen Bee as your score grows.
  • Each word can only be found once per hive.
  • Practice mode never saves to your best.
