Picking a difficulty

Easy is 2 slow cops and 8 loot bags — learn the streets. Medium is 3 cops and 6 bags. Hard is 4 fast cops and 5 bags, and they multiply quicker.

Rules

The chase

  • A top-down chase through a city grid. You are the robber; the patrol cops chase you.
  • Loot bags are scattered on the streets. Run over one to grab it: +100 points, and a fresh bag appears somewhere else.
  • You have 3 lives. A cop tagging you costs one life, then you respawn at the south-west corner with a short safe window (you blink while safe).
  • The round ends when all 3 lives are gone. Your score and bests are saved on this device.

Moving

  • Arrow keys / W A S D run up, down, left and right. On-screen pad buttons work on touch screens. A controller works too: stick / D-pad moves, Start pauses.
  • Buildings are solid — you can only run on streets and through intersections. Corners are your friend: cops turn slower than you do.

Escalation

  • Every 5 loot bags a new cop joins the patrol (up to 6), and all cops get a little faster.
  • Cops also speed up slowly as the round goes on — the longer you survive, the hotter the chase.

Score

  • Score = loot × 100 + seconds survived × 5.
  • Your best score, best loot haul and longest run are saved on this device.

Difficulty

  • Easy: 2 cops, slower, 8 loot bags.
  • Medium: 3 cops, steady, 6 loot bags.
  • Hard: 4 cops, faster, 5 loot bags.
  • You can change difficulty between rounds — it takes effect on the next round. Your choice is remembered for next time.

Practice round

  • Tap Practice round to switch to a practice session at any time.
  • No score is kept and no lives are lost — getting caught just sends you back to the corner so you can learn the streets.
  • Practice never saves anything. Tap the button again to return to a scored round.
