Rules

Classic arcade Skee-Ball: roll balls up a lane, they jump the ramp at the end, and wherever they land in the rings is what you score.

How to Play

  • Flick: swipe or flick upward on the lane (touch or mouse). A faster, straighter flick rolls farther and straighter.
  • Or charge: hold the Hold to Roll button (or hold Space) to build power, release to roll. Use ◀ ▶ or the arrow keys to nudge your aim first.
  • You get 9 balls per game. Add up your ring scores — that's your total.
  • Too soft and the ball rolls back down the lane (0 points). Too wild and it bounces out past the rings (0 points).

Tickets

Tickets are just for fun: you earn 1 ticket for every 10 points, plus a 5-ticket bonus for every 100-pocket hit. Beat 300 points in a game for a 10-ticket bonus. Play-money only — tickets have no cash value, there are no prizes, and no real money is ever involved.

Difficulty

  • Easy — bigger rings, barely any wobble, aim guide on. Great for learning the flick.
  • Normal — classic ring sizes and a little wobble on every roll.
  • Hard — smaller center and 100 pockets, more wobble, no aim guide. For steady hands.

Your difficulty choice and your best score + best tickets for each level are saved on this device.

Practice

Practice gives you unlimited balls with hints after every roll (too soft / too strong / too far left…). No score is saved, so experiment freely, then switch to the 9-Ball Game when you're ready.

Tips

  • Medium power + dead-straight usually lands near the 50. Full power tends to fly long.
  • For a 100 pocket, aim toward a top corner and use strong-but-not-max power.
  • On Hard, release the charge button a touch early — the wobble grows with power.
