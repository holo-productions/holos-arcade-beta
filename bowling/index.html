How bowling scores

A game is 10 frames. In frames 1–9 you get up to two balls to knock down all 10 pins. The 10th frame gives you up to three balls if you strike or spare.

The basics

  • Strike (X) — all 10 pins with your first ball. The frame scores 10 plus the pins from your next two balls.
  • Spare (/) — all 10 pins across two balls. The frame scores 10 plus the pins from your next one ball.
  • Open frame — fewer than 10 pins in two balls. The frame scores exactly the pins you knocked down.
  • Gutter ball (–) — the ball leaves the lane before reaching the pins. Zero pins, zero points.
  • A perfect game — 12 strikes in a row — scores 300.

The 10th frame

  • Strike on ball one: the rack is reset and you bowl two more balls. Strike again and the rack resets once more.
  • Spare across balls one and two: you get one bonus ball at a fresh rack.
  • No strike or spare: the game ends after two balls.

Playing this game

StepWhat to do
AimDrag the ball left or right on the approach, and use the Aim buttons (or arrow keys) to angle your shot.
PowerTap Charge power, then tap Lock power to stop the needle. The yellow zone is the sweet spot.
SpinThe spin needle sweeps side to side. Tap Throw! to release — left of centre hooks left, right of centre hooks right.

Practice round

Tap Practice round in the toolbar to warm up without pressure. Practice plays a full 10 frames, but your score is not saved to your bests, there's no timer, coaching hints appear after every ball, and you get a Fresh rack button to reset all 10 pins whenever you want to retry a leave. Tap Practice round again (or “Play a real game” at the end) to return to a scored game.

Difficulty

  • Easy — slower meters, pins topple generously, no lane drift.
  • Normal — standard meters and pin action.
  • Hard — faster meters, stingier pin action, and the ball drifts on the oil as it travels.
