    ❓ How to play — Tutorial
    1. Split into 2 teams. Pick a category, difficulty and rounds above.
    2. Tap Start Game, then pass the phone to the first team's actor.
    3. The actor taps “I'm the actor” — a secret word appears with a timer.
    4. Act it out without talking! Your team shouts guesses.
    5. Tap ✅ Got It! when they guess right — you score and a new word appears.
    6. Stuck? Tap ⏭ Pass (limited on Classic/Hard) for a new word.
    7. When time runs out, pass the phone to the other team. Most points after all rounds wins!

    This tutorial only opens when you tap “How to play” — it never pops up on its own.

    📜 Rules
    • Teams: 2 teams, pass-and-play on one device. Each team acts once per round.
    • No talking, no mouthing words, no spelling letters while acting. Gestures, mime and sounds like animal noises only if your group agrees before starting.
    • Scoring: Easy/Classic = 1 point per “Got It!”. Party Hard = 2 points per “Got It!”.
    • Timer: Easy 90s · Classic 60s · Party Hard 45s per turn.
    • Passes: Easy unlimited · Classic 3 per turn · Party Hard 1 per turn. A pass scores 0.
    • Categories: Animals, Actions, Jobs, Food, Sports, Objects, or All Mixed. Prompts are original word lists made for this game — no movie titles.
    • Winner: Highest score after the chosen rounds. A tie is a tie — rematch encouraged!
    • Practice mode: Play freely; scores and bests are not saved.
    • Fair play: Only the actor sees the prompt. Guessing team must say the exact word (close enough is up to the other team — be kind!).
