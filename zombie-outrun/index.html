How to play

1. Move between lanes

Your runner dashes down a spooky lane-way in three lanes. Tap ◀ Left / Right ▶, swipe sideways, or use the arrow keys to hop between lanes.

2. Jump the zombies

Zombies shuffle toward you one lane at a time. Dodge sideways — or tap Jump (or press ↑ / Space) to leap clean over one. They're more silly than scary: big green grins, zero gore.

3. Grab supplies

Run through water bottles, apples and bandages to collect them. Each one adds bonus points and keeps your runner fuelled.

4. Survive the speed-up

The longer you run, the faster everything gets. If a zombie tags you, the run ends — unless Practice mode is on, in which case you just keep going.

Rules

  • Goal: run as far as you can and collect as many supplies as possible.
  • Scoring: +1 point for every moment you stay ahead of the horde, +25 points per supply collected.
  • Getting caught: running into a zombie (same lane, feet on the ground) ends the run.
  • Jumping: while you're in the air, zombies pass right under you.
  • Difficulty: Easy, Normal and Hard change zombie speed and how often they appear. Your pick is saved.
  • Practice mode: zombies can't catch you and the score doesn't count toward your best — it's just for learning the ropes.
  • Best score: your best non-practice run is saved on this device.
