How to play Merge Garden

✦

1 · Plant seeds

Tap the Seed Basket to plant. Each plant costs 1 energy and lands on a random free plot. Most are Seeds, but now and then you'll get a Sprout — or even a Seedling — for free.

✦

2 · Merge two of a kind

Tap a plant, then tap a matching plant (or drag one onto the other). The two merge into the next plant in the chain: two Seeds become a Sprout, two Sprouts become a Seedling, and so on up to the Golden Bouquet.

✦

3 · Fill neighbour orders

Neighbours post orders for specific plants. When your garden holds everything an order asks for, tap Deliver to earn coins and score — and free up plots.

✦

4 · Spend coins in the shop

Coins buy an energy top-up, fertilizer (grows a selected plant one step), or a cleared plot (unlocks a fenced-off square for good).

✦

5 · Don't let the garden jam

If every open plot is full and no two plants match, the garden is jammed and the game ends. Keep merging, keep delivering — and see if you can grow the Golden Bouquet!

✦

6 · Just want to practise?

Tap Practice round any time for a relaxed garden: unlimited energy, a free shop, every plot open, and no game over if the garden jams — compost a plant to make room and keep experimenting. Practice scores don't touch your saved bests. Tap Exit practice when you're ready for a real garden.

Rules — Merge Garden

The goal

Grow plants up the 11-step merge chain — Seed → Sprout → Seedling → Leafy Plant → Clover Patch → Potted Plant → Tulip → Cherry Bloom → Hibiscus → Rose → Golden Bouquet — fill neighbour orders, and score as much as you can before the garden jams.

Planting & energy

  • Tapping the Seed Basket plants one item on a random free plot and costs 1 energy.
  • What you get is luck of the basket: mostly Seeds, sometimes a Sprout, rarely a Seedling. Harder difficulties skew more toward plain Seeds.
  • Energy refills automatically over time, up to your difficulty's maximum. An energy top-up from the shop can push you above the maximum.

Merging

  • Two plants of the same tier merge into one plant of the next tier. Tap one then the other, or drag one onto the other.
  • Tap an empty plot with a plant selected to move the plant there. Tap a different plant to switch your selection.
  • The Golden Bouquet (tier 11) is the top of the chain — it can't merge further, but it scores big and neighbours pay a fortune for one.
  • Merge points = (new tier × new tier) × 10 × difficulty multiplier.

Orders

  • Up to two orders are open at once. Each asks for specific plants and pays coins + score when delivered.
  • Delivering removes those plants from your garden. Order reward = 25 × the sum of the tiers delivered, plus a 50-point bonus, times the difficulty multiplier (coins are not multiplied).
  • Every 3 orders delivered raises your Garden Level and fully refills your energy.
  • You can skip an order for free — a new neighbour shows up right away.

Shop

  • Energy top-up (25 🪙): +10 energy immediately.
  • Fertilizer (60 🪙): grows your currently selected plant one tier. It can't be used on a Golden Bouquet.
  • Clear a plot (80 🪙): permanently unlocks one fenced-off plot (Normal and Hard start with some plots fenced off).

Game over

The game ends when every open plot is full and no two plants on the board share a tier — there's nothing left to merge. Your score, orders delivered, and best plant are recorded as your records for that difficulty.

Practice round

Tap Practice round for a relaxed round with unlimited energy, a free shop, no fenced-off plots, and no jam game-over — if the board fills with no matches, select a plant and tap Compost selected plant to free a plot and keep going. Practice score and coins stay in practice and never change your saved per-difficulty bests. Tap Exit practice (or change difficulty) to start a real garden again.
