📖 Rules - Prairie Buck Hunt

🦌 Goal

Tag as many critters as you can across three timed rounds. Tagged critters poof away in a puff of stars, happy and unharmed. Your total score is compared with your best for that difficulty.

🕹️ How to play

  • Tap or click directly on a critter to tag it. The crosshair follows your mouse or finger.
  • Critters run left to right and right to left at three depths. Far critters look smaller and pay the same.
  • There is a short pause between shots, so steady taps beat frantic mashing.
  • When a round timer ends, the next round starts right away with a faster herd. After round 3, your final score is shown.
  • Use Pause any time. Use End round to finish early and see your summary.
