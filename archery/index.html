How to play — 30-second tutorial

No walkthrough is forced on you — this page is here whenever you want it.

1
Aim

Drag on the range or use the arrow keys. The crosshair is where you are aiming.

2
Draw

Hold Space, hold the DRAW button, or hold mouse / touch on the range. Power builds.

3
Loose

Release to shoot. A full draw flies fast and flat; a weak draw drops short.

4

Picking a difficulty

Easy is light wind, a slow gentle sway and a big target. Medium is a fair breeze. Hard is strong gusty wind, a fast sway and a smaller, farther target.

Rules

The round

  • A side-view archery range. 10 arrows per round, one target at the far end.
  • Your archer stands on the left. The target's rings score 10 / 20 / 30 / 40, and the gold bullseye scores 50.
  • The clock runs from your first shot until your tenth arrow lands.

Aiming & shooting

  • Drag on the range, or use the arrow keys, to move the aim point. On-screen nudge buttons work on touch screens.
  • Hold Space (or hold mouse / touch on the range, or hold the DRAW button) to draw the bow — power builds while you hold.
  • Release to loose the arrow. A full draw flies fast and flat; a weak draw arcs and drops short.
  • Your aim sways gently, and the sway grows the longer you hold the draw. Loose when the crosshair drifts over the gold.

Wind & gravity

  • Wind blows up or down across the range and pushes the arrow sideways in flight. The wind arrow and strength bar show it — it changes every shot.
  • Gravity pulls every arrow down, so aim a little above where you want to hit.
  • An arrow that misses the target and hits the ground scores 0.

Score

  • Score = the sum of all 10 arrows. A perfect round is 500.
  • Your best score, rounds played, bullseyes and best average per arrow are saved on this device.

Difficulty

  • Easy: light wind, slow sway, big target.
  • Medium: moderate wind and sway.
  • Hard: strong gusty wind, fast sway, smaller target set farther back.
  • You can change difficulty between rounds — it takes effect on the next round. Your choice is remembered for next time.

Practice round

  • Tap Practice round to switch to a practice session at any time.
  • Endless arrows, no round end, no score is kept — just shoot and learn the wind.
  • Practice never saves anything. Tap the button again to return to a 10-arrow round.
