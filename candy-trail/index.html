How to Play

Step 1 of 4 — The big idea

You and Sprinkle start at the Gingerbread House. Follow the winding trail of coloured squares all the way to the Candy Castle. First buddy there gets the crown — and everybody gets sprinkles, because that's the rule.

BLUE
BLUE

Step 2 of 4 — Draw a card

Tap Draw a Card. Your card shows one or two colour squares. One square? Hop to the next square of that colour. Two squares? Hop past the first one and land on the second square of that colour. The colour's name is written under it, so you can learn as you go.

Step 3 of 4 — Shortcuts & sticky spots

Land on a lollipop space and — wheee! — you slide along a secret shortcut, way ahead on the trail. Land on a honey pot space and you get stuck in the sticky stuff: you miss your next turn while you wiggle free. Sprinkle can get stuck too!

Step 4 of 4 — Be a good sport

There's no timer and no way to lose your progress — just a happy race. In Practice Solo mode there's no race at all: draw cards and wander the trail at your own pace until you reach the castle. Ready?

Candy Trail Rules

The whole game, nice and simple.

Goal

Be the first cookie buddy to travel the whole trail and reach the Candy Castle (the last space). If your card would hop you past the end of the trail, you go straight to the castle.

The colours

On your turn

  • Tap Draw a Card.
  • One square: move forward to the next square of that colour.
  • Two squares: move forward to the second square of that colour.
  • Then it's Sprinkle's turn (unless you're in Practice Solo mode).

Special spaces

  • Lollipop shortcut: landing on one whisks you ahead to a space further along the trail. Free ride!
  • Honey pot sticky spot: landing on one means you miss your next turn while you wiggle free. It happens to Sprinkle too.

Modes & difficulty

  • Race Sprinkle: take turns against Sprinkle the cookie cub.
  • Practice Solo: no opponent, no race — just you, the cards, and the trail.
  • Easy: Sprinkle is a sleepy cub and sometimes only hops one square on a two-square card. Medium: fair and square. Hard: Sprinkle has lucky sprinkles and sometimes hops an extra square.
  • Your difficulty, mode, and buddy choices are saved on this device, so they're right where you left them next visit.

Good-sport promise

No timers, no points taken away, no game over. If Sprinkle reaches the castle first, you still finish your adventure — and everybody gets sprinkles.
