📖 Rules - Surfing Waves

🎯 Goal

Stay on your board as long as you can. Collect stars, keep your balance in the green, and avoid the dark wipeout foam zones.

🕹️ How to surf

  • Hold Lean left or Lean right, drag on the wave, or use the left and right arrow keys. Leaning also steers you along the wave.
  • The wave constantly pushes your balance to one side. Lean the other way to bring the needle back to the green.
  • If the needle hits either red edge, you wipe out and lose a heart.
  • Touching a dark foam wipeout zone also costs a heart.
  • You have 3 hearts. Lose them all and the ride ends. You can also tap End round any time.

How to play

A quick tour, then the wave is yours:

1
Pick Difficulty

Easy is a calm wave with slow drift; Hard is wild and fast.

2
Watch the Meter

Hold Lean left or Lean right to push the needle back to the green middle.

3
Collect Stars

Leaning moves you along the wave; ride through gold stars for points.

4
Avoid Foam

Dark foam costs a heart, just like letting the needle hit the red edge.

5
Stay Up

You have three hearts; survive as long as you can, or practice with unlimited hearts.
