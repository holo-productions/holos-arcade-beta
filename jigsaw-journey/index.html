How to play

Tap a piece in the tray, then tap where it belongs on the board. Correct pieces snap in with a click. Finish the whole picture to win. Easy is a 3×3, Normal 4×4, Hard 5×5.

Rules

  • Tap a piece, then tap its home square.
  • Correct snaps score +20 each.
  • Finish the picture for a bonus.
  • Practice mode never saves to your best.
