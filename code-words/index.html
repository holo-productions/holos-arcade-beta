    Code Words — Rules

    The board holds 25 code words. Hidden underneath: 9 are yours (blue), 8 belong to the rival team (red), 7 are neutral bystanders, and 1 is the trap card. You can't see which is which — only your spymaster can, and they can only talk in clues.

    Your turn

    1. Your AI spymaster gives a one-word clue plus a number — for example, OCEAN — 2 means two of your words on the board connect to “ocean.”
    2. Tap cards to guess, one at a time. You get exactly that many guesses this turn. A correct blue card lets you keep guessing while guesses remain.
    3. Tap End my turn any time to stop early — smart when the rest of the clue feels like a coin flip.

    What a tap can reveal

    • Your word (blue) — one step closer. Keep guessing if you have guesses left.
    • Neutral — nobody scores, and your turn ends on the spot.
    • Rival word (red) — you just helped the other team, and your turn ends.
    • The trap card — mission over. You lose instantly, no matter the score.

    The rival's turn

    After your turn, the rival spymaster gives its own clue and the rival operative guesses out loud in the field log. The rival can make the same mistakes you can: it may hit neutral cards, hand you one of your own words, or even spring the trap — which wins the game for you.

    Winning and losing

    • Win by revealing all 9 of your words before the rival reveals its 8 — or when the rival springs the trap.
    • Lose if you spring the trap yourself, or the rival completes its set first (including when you reveal its last word for it).
    • Clues only ever point at words still hidden. A word from an old clue can still be guessed on a later turn — your guess count is the only limit.

    Practice round

    Switch Mode to Practice round any time for a relaxed board to learn on:

    • No timer — take as long as you like. The clock stays off.
    • No score — practice results are never added to your bests and don't change your played / won counts.
    • Hints on demand — tap Get a hint and a word that fits the current clue is highlighted in gold, with a short note on why it fits.
    • Forgiving trap — tap the trap card and you'll be warned which card it is and can keep practising. In a scored game that same tap ends the mission instantly.
    • Everything else plays the same — same clues, same rival turns — so what you learn carries straight into a scored game.

    Your bests

    Games played, games won, your fewest rounds in a win, and your fastest win are kept for each difficulty on this device, shown under the board. Practice rounds are not counted.

    How to play

    You and the rival team each have code words hidden on a 25-card board. Spymasters know where everything is — operatives (that's you) only get one-word clues.

    1
    Read the clue

    “OCEAN — 2” means two of your words connect to ocean.

    2
    Every tap counts

    Your word keeps you going. Neutral or rival ends your turn.

    3
    Avoid the trap

    Hit the trap card and you lose on the spot.

    4
    Know when to stop

    Shaky on a guess? Use “End my turn” — a pass beats a free point.

    5
    Beat the rival

    First team to reveal all its words wins. You move first.

    6
    Practice round

    No timer or score, hints when stuck, trap only warns.
