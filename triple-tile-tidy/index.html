How to play

1
Tap A Tile

Tap a bright, uncovered tile to move it into your tray.

2
Match Three

Three tiles with the same symbol in the tray clear.

3
Uncover First

Dimmed tiles under others can't be tapped until uncovered.

4
Clear The Pile

Clear the whole pile to win!

5
Shuffle Helps

Stuck? Shuffle re-deals what's left (costs 50 points).

Rules

  • The tray holds 7 tiles. If it fills without a triple, you lose.
  • Only uncovered tiles can be tapped.
  • Each triple scores 100. Clearing the pile scores a 300 bonus.
  • Shuffle costs 50 points and re-deals what's left.
  • Practice mode never saves to your best.
