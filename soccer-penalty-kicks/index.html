Rules — Penalty Kicks

Five kicks. One keeper. No extra time, no excuses. Score as many as you can.

How a kick works

  • Aim: Tap or drag on the goal to move the target. Corners are hardest for the keeper to reach — and easiest to miss.
  • Power: Press and hold SHOOT. The power bar fills and drains back and forth. Release to kick.
  • Under 25% power: a soft shot — the keeper saves it easily unless it's tucked right in the corner.
  • 55–85% power: the sweet spot — fast enough to beat a diving keeper.
  • Over 92% power: danger zone — the higher it goes, the more likely you sky it over the bar.

Scoring

  • Each match is 5 kicks. A goal = ⚽, a save = 🧤, a miss = ✕ in the match tracker.
  • Streaks count consecutive goals across kicks — and carry across matches until you fail to score.
  • Your bests (best match, best streak, total goals, matches played) are saved on this device.

The keeper

The keeper watches your aim and guesses a dive. Perfectly placed shots in the corners beat even a correct guess — placed shots down the middle rarely do.

DifficultyKeeper reads your aimDive speed
EasyGuesses right ~30% of the timeSlow — corners are wide open
NormalGuesses right ~55% of the timeMedium
HardGuesses right ~75% of the timeFast — you need power + placement

Practice Round

  • Tap 🎯 Practice Round in the Mode row to switch — tap 🏆 Match to switch back.
  • No score: practice kicks never change your match, streak, or bests, and never count as a played match.
  • Unlimited & untimed: take as many kicks as you like — there is no 5-kick limit and no full-time.
  • Hints on: a blue ghost target shows a suggested corner, and a coach hint under the goal suggests a power range (for example, low left at 65–75%). A new hint appears after every kick, with quick feedback on your last shot.
  • You can still change difficulty in Practice — try Hard with hints before playing a Hard match for real.

Tips

  • Vary your corners — on Hard, the keeper learns nothing, but predictable aim still gets read.
  • A low corner at 70% power is the most reliable finish in the game.
  • Chasing 5/5? Don't max the power. Placement beats pace.
  • New to the game? Do a Practice Round first — learn the sweet spot with hints, then play a match.
