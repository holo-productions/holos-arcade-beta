Firefighter Rescue

Fires are breaking out all over the apartment block. Aim the hose, spray them out, and rescue the people and pets trapped inside — before a fire burns out of control.

Best score: 0  ·  Difficulty: Normal

How to Play

  • Aim with your mouse or finger — drag across the building. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys.
  • Spray by holding the mouse button or your finger down. On a keyboard, hold Space.
  • Water puts fires out. A small fire is worth 10, a medium one 20, a big one 30.
  • If a fire is in a window with a person, cat or dog, putting it out rescues them for +25.
  • A fire left burning too long burns out and costs a life. You have 3 lives.
  • Every 8 fires out, you level up — fires come faster and grow quicker. Level up is worth +50.
  • Practice mode: no lives lost, no score saved — just learning the ropes.
  • Press P to pause.
