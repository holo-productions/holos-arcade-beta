    Bubble Race — an original pop-dice board game. Part of Holo's Arcade.

    How to play

    Bubble Race rules

    Race all 4 of your pegs around the bubble track and into your golden home tray. First racer to get every peg home wins!

    Getting started

    • Your pegs begin in your yard (the dashed corner pen).
    • You need to roll a 6 to move a peg out of the yard onto its start bubble.

    On your turn

    • Tap the big bubble in the middle to pop it and roll the dice.
    • Tap one of your glowing pegs to move it that many bubbles. (Buttons appear beside the board too, if tapping pegs is fiddly.)
    • Roll a 6 and you get another turn — pop again!
    • If no peg can move, your turn passes. If you rolled a 6 but still can't move, pop again.

    Bumping

    • Land exactly on a bubble where a rival peg sits and — pop! — that peg is sent all the way back to its yard.
    • There are no safe bubbles on the main track. Watch your back!
    • Your own pegs can happily share a bubble.

    Coming home

    • After a full lap, your peg turns down its own coloured lane toward the middle.
    • You need the exact roll to land in the home tray — too big a roll and that peg has to stay put.

    Modes

    • Difficulty changes how clever the three rival racers play. Your pick is remembered for next time.
    • Practice mode keeps the rivals gentle, shows a gold ring on a suggested move, and doesn't count the game in your score.
