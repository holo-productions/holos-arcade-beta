Reversi — Full Rules

The complete rulebook for this version: setup, legal moves, scoring, special rules and how a game is won.

Setup

  • Played on an 8 × 8 board (64 squares), coordinates A–H across and 1–8 down.
  • Four discs start in the centre, crossed: White on D4 and E5, Black on E4 and D5.
  • In this game you always play Black and always move first. The computer plays White.

Legal moves & flipping

  • A move is placing one disc of your colour on an empty square.
  • The placement is legal only if it outflanks at least one opponent disc: one or more opponent discs in an unbroken straight line — horizontal, vertical, or diagonal (8 directions) — ending at a disc of your colour.
  • All outflanked discs flip to your colour, in every direction the placement outflanks at once. A single move can flip discs in up to 8 lines.
  • A placement that flips nothing is not a legal move and cannot be played. There is no voluntary passing while a legal move exists.
  • Discs are never removed from the board and never move — they only change colour.

Turns & passing

  • Players alternate turns, one disc per turn.
  • If a player has no legal move, their turn is automatically skipped (a forced pass) and the other player moves again. The game announces every pass.
  • Passing is never a choice — it happens only when no legal move exists.

Scoring

  • Your score is simply your disc count, shown live: Black discs vs White discs.
  • There are no points for corners, edges, or big flips beyond the discs they give you — position matters only because it changes the final count.
  • Empty squares at the end count for nobody. They are not awarded to either player.
  • The winning margin is the difference between the two disc counts (for example, 40–24 is a margin of 16).

Win conditions & game end

  • The game ends when the board is full, or when neither player has a legal move — even if empty squares remain.
  • The player with more discs when the game ends wins.
  • Equal disc counts is a draw.
  • A real game only counts toward your records (games, wins, best margin, best streak) once it reaches this natural end. A practice round never counts, however it ends.

Special rules in this version

  • Stable discs: a disc in a corner square can never be outflanked, so it can never flip. Discs connected to a taken corner along an edge can become equally safe.
  • AI levels change only the opponent, never the rules. Casual, Club and Master all play by exactly the rules above; they differ only in how far ahead they think.
  • Hint suggests a move but never plays it for you, and using it does not affect scoring or records.
  • Practice round (mode menu) plays by exactly these rules against the same AI, but the result is never recorded — no game, win, loss, draw, margin, or streak is added. In practice only, Undo also stays available after the game ends, so you can take back the finish and try a different line.
  • Undo takes back your most recent move together with the AI's reply. In a real game it is available until the game ends; a finished real game cannot be undone.
  • New game abandons the current game at any point. An abandoned game is not recorded — no win, loss, or draw.
  • Show legal moves is a display aid only. Turning it off hides the dotted guides; the legal-move rules do not change.
  • There is no timer and no move limit — take as long as you like over any move.
