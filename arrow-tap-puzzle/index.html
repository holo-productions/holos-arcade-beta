Rules

The goal

Clear every arrow off the board. Tap an arrow and it slides all the way off the edge in the direction it points — but only if every cell between it and the edge is empty. Another arrow anywhere in its path blocks it, even if it's two or three cells away, not just right next to it.

Blocked taps

Tapping a blocked arrow costs you a life (it shakes and flashes red). Run out of lives, or run out of time on Normal and Hard, and the run ends. Work out an order that opens paths up — sometimes you have to free an arrow at the far end first.

Scoring

  • Each arrow cleared: 10 points × your combo.
  • Clear arrows quickly one after another to build a combo — up to ×5. Wait more than 3 seconds between clears and the combo resets.
  • Finishing a board adds a bonus: 2 points per second left on the clock (Normal/Hard) plus 50 points per life left.
  • Your score carries across levels in a run. Your best score, highest level, and fastest board are saved per difficulty.

Practice round

Flip on Practice round any time: no timer, unlimited lives and hints, and nothing is saved to your bests. Good for learning the ropes or warming up.

Hints

The Hint button pulses an arrow that can move right now. It never tells you the whole order — just one safe tap.
