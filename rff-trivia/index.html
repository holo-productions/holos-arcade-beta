How to play

Quick version — under a minute, then pick any level.

  1. 1
    Pick any level

    Each level is one podcast episode. There is no unlocking and no set order — tap ▶ Play on any card, or tap 🎲 Surprise me.

  2. 2
    Pick a difficulty

    Use the Difficulty panel on the map before you play. Easy shows 3 choices with no timer for 50 points per correct answer. Normal is the standard game: 4 choices, no timer, 100 points. Hard gives you 15 seconds per question for 150 points — if time runs out, that question counts as wrong. Your choice is saved and applies to the next level you start.

  3. 3
    Read the scene, tap an answer

    A short ridiculous scene sets up each level. For every question, tap one of the answers (A, B, C — plus D on Normal and Hard). You will see straight away if you were right, plus a short explanation.

  4. 4
    Score points and build a streak

    Points per correct answer depend on your difficulty (50 / 100 / 150). Get answers right in a row to build a streak and earn bonus points. Tap Next question → to keep going.

  5. 5
    Finish the level — you can’t lose

    Answer every question in the level to finish it. All correct earns 3 stars; most correct earns 2 stars; finishing earns at least 1 star. Earn badges and beat your best score by replaying any level — try a harder difficulty when a level starts to feel easy.

  6. 6
    Or just practise — it doesn’t count

    Tap Start practice round for 10 random questions from across all episodes, or tap Practice on any level card to practise just that episode. Practice uses your chosen difficulty, but earns no stars, no saved score and no badges — and it never touches a level you have in progress.

Rules

The full rules — scoring, stars, difficulty and practice mode.

  1. 1
    🎯 The objective

    Every level is one episode of Ridiculously Fit and Fed. Answer all of a level's questions to complete it. There's no unlocking and no set order: play any level from the map, search or filter by series, or tap 🎲 Surprise me. You can't lose a level — finishing always completes it.

  2. 2
    🧮 How scoring works

    Correct answers score base points by difficulty: 50 on Easy, 100 on Normal, 150 on Hard. Streaks add bonus points: from your 2nd correct answer in a row, each streak step adds +10 (Easy), +25 (Normal) or +40 (Hard) — so your 3rd correct answer in a row on Normal is worth 150 points. Wrong answers and Hard-mode timeouts score 0 and reset your streak. Only your best score per level is kept.

  3. 3
    ⭐ Stars & badges

    Stars come from accuracy: all correct = 3 stars, 60%+ correct = 2 stars, and finishing always earns at least 1 star. There are 9 badges to collect — including First Clear, Perfect Level, Hot Streak (3 correct in a row), Star Collector (50 stars) and Ridiculously Complete (finish every level). Best scores, stars, badges and even a level left mid-way are saved on this device; Reset progress on the map wipes them.

  4. 4
    🎚 Difficulty differences

    Easy: 3 choices, no timer, 50 points. Normal: 4 choices, no timer, 100 points. Hard: 4 choices, 15 seconds per question (running out of time counts as wrong), 150 points. Difficulty is chosen on the map, saved, and locked in for each level when you start it — change it any time between levels.

  5. 5
    🎯 Practice mode

    Practice uses your chosen difficulty but never counts: the mixed Practice Round draws 10 random questions from across all episodes, and every level card has a Practice button for just that episode (questions shuffled). No stars, no saved score, no badges — and practice never touches a real level you have in progress.
