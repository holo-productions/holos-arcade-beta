How to play

Dice Kings — the rules

Dice Kings is a 13-round game of luck and nerve played with five dice. Every round you fill in one category on the scorecard. When all 13 are filled, the highest total takes the crown.

Your turn

  • Roll all five dice. You get up to 3 rolls per turn.
  • Between rolls, tap any die to hold it (it turns gold). Tap again to release it. Held dice stay put; the rest re-roll.
  • When you're happy — or out of rolls — tap a score on the scorecard to lock it in. That ends your turn. You must score something every turn, even if it's a zero.

Upper section

  • Ones through Sixes: add up only the dice showing that number.
  • Bonus: if your upper section totals 63 or more, you earn a +35 bonus. (That's like rolling three of every number.)

Lower section

  • Three of a Kind: at least three dice the same — score the total of all five dice, otherwise 0.
  • Four of a Kind: at least four the same — total of all five dice, otherwise 0.
  • Full House: three of one number + two of another = 25 points.
  • Small Straight: any four dice in a row (1-2-3-4, 2-3-4-5, or 3-4-5-6) = 30 points.
  • Large Straight: all five in a row = 40 points.
  • Five of a Kind: all five dice the same = 50 points. The crown jewel.
  • Chance: no pattern needed — just add up all five dice.

Modes

  • Solo score chase: just you vs the dice. Chase your personal best.
  • Vs CPU: you and the computer take turns on the same 13 rounds. Chill CPU plays loose, Standard plays a solid game, Sharp hunts the bonus and rarely wastes a big score.
  • Practice mode: same game, zero pressure. You get a hint for the best score on the table after every roll, and practice games never touch your saved bests.
