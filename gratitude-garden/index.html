How to play

  1. Read today’s prompt. A new gentle prompt appears each day.
  2. Write a little. One word is enough on Gentle. A sentence on Normal. Three things on Challenge.
  3. Tap “Plant it”. Your entry is saved on this device and a seed is planted in your garden.
  4. Watch it grow. As you keep writing on later days, seeds become sprouts, then flowers.
  5. Revisit anytime. Open My Garden and tap any plant to re-read that day’s entry.
  6. Practice mode lets you write without saving — handy for a quick scribble.
  7. Missed a day? No problem. Your garden waits for you. Nothing wilts, nothing is lost.

Rules

  • One entry per day grows your garden. You can edit today’s entry before the day ends — just save again.
  • Prompt depth: Gentle = one word or a few words. Normal = a sentence. Challenge = three things. Change it anytime; it is saved on this device.
  • Growth stages: a new entry plants a seed 🌰. After you write on more days, it becomes a sprout 🌱, then a flower 🌸.
  • Streaks are gentle. The counter shows consecutive days you wrote. Missing a day never removes plants or entries.
  • Practice mode never saves. Turn it off to plant for real.
  • Privacy: entries, plants, streak, and settings are stored only in this browser’s local storage on this device. Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing your browser data will clear your garden.
  • Wellness note: this is a small daily reflection game for relaxation and fun. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.
