Christmas Trivia

Pick a category, pick a difficulty, then answer 5 questions. Every question has a Source button — check the real source, retro TV style.

Ready?

    How to Play

    • Pick one of 4 categories, then a difficulty: Easy has no timer (100 pts per right answer), Medium gives 30 seconds per question (150 pts), Hard gives 15 seconds per question (200 pts).
    • Answer 5 questions. Tap an answer — right is green, wrong is red, and the right one lights up too.
    • Hint (one per question, no score penalty): removes two wrong answers.
    • Source on every question opens the real source on a retro TV — exact quote, source name and link. The timer pauses while you check.
    • Practice mode: no score, no best saved — just learning the ropes.
    • Your best score per category + difficulty is saved on this device.
