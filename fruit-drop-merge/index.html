Rules

Goal

Score as high as you can before fruit piles past the dashed line. Merge your way to a Watermelon (1,000+ bonus points).

How merging works

  • When two fruits of the same type touch, they merge into the next fruit in the chain.
  • Chain merges in quick succession earn a combo multiplier.
  • Only Cherry through Apple can drop. Bigger fruit must be earned by merging.

Scoring

  • Strawberry merge: 3 pts • Grape: 6 • Orange: 10 • Apple: 15 • Pear: 21 • Peach: 28 • Pineapple: 36 • Melon: 45 • Watermelon: 55 + 100 bonus, multiplied by combo.

Difficulty

  • Easy: Only Cherry–Orange drop, slower gravity, longer overflow grace (2.5s).
  • Normal: Cherry–Apple drop, balanced.
  • Hard: Faster gravity, Apples drop more often, only 1.2s overflow grace.

Game over

If any settled fruit stays above the dashed line longer than the grace time, the game ends. The line flashes red when you're in danger.

🌱 Practice Round

Tap Practice Round above the board to warm up with no pressure:

  • No game over — fruit can pile past the line and the round keeps going. The line turns green and is just a guide.
  • Score doesn't count — practice points are tallied so you can see merges and combos, but they never update your Best for any difficulty.
  • Undo Last Drop — take back your most recent drop (including any merges it caused) and try a different spot.
  • Clear Pile — empty the board without ending the round, so you can test a new setup.
  • Difficulty still applies (drop pool & gravity), so you can rehearse Easy, Normal, or Hard exactly as scored play feels.
  • Switch back to Scored at any time — that starts a fresh scored round.

How to Play

1
Aim

Move finger, mouse, or arrow keys. The guide line shows where fruit falls.

2
Drop

Tap, click, press Space, or hit DROP FRUIT. Wait for the next fruit.

3
Merge

Match two of a kind to grow: Cherries → Strawberry → Grape → Watermelon!

4
Don't Overflow

Keep the pile below the dashed line. Big fruit goes in corners.

5
Practice First

Practice Round: no game over, score doesn't count, undo drops while you learn.
