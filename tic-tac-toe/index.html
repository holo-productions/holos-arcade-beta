How to play

1
X and O

Player 1 is X. Player 2 is O.

2
Place mark

Tap empty square to place.

3
Pass phone

Pass to next player with a friend.

4
Three in a row

3 in row, column or diagonal wins.

5
Play again

Tap New Round. Scores carry over.

6
Vs Computer

You are X. Computer is O.

Rules

  • Players take turns. X always starts a round.
  • You can only tap an empty square.
  • Three in a row wins. That line lights up.
  • If all squares fill with no winner, the round is a draw.
  • Restart clears the board and the scores. New Round keeps scores.
