Rules

The game

  • Table football, top-down. You play blue (left) and attack the right goal; the red side attacks the left goal.
  • First player to 5 goals wins the match. There is no time limit — the clock just tracks your match time.
  • Each side has four rods — goalie, defence, midfield, attack — and all of one side's rods slide together.

Play

  • Slide rods up and down to line a little player up behind the ball, then kick to shoot it toward the far goal.
  • The ball bounces off the top and bottom walls and the ends, except through the goal mouths in the middle of each end.
  • A goal counts the moment the ball crosses the line into a goal mouth.
  • After a goal, play restarts from the centre with the ball served toward the player who conceded.
  • Your kick is strongest when a man is right behind the ball — a kick from far away does nothing.

Modes

  • Vs Computer: you play blue with W/S (or arrow keys) and Space or D to kick.
  • 2 Players (pass-and-play): blue uses W/S + Space/D, red uses ↑/↓ + Enter to kick. Both players share one screen.
  • On touch screens, drag on the table to slide rods and tap the KICK button. Dragging the left half slides blue, the right half slides red in 2-player mode.
  • With a controller: stick / D-pad slides, A kicks, Start pauses. Player 1 is controller 1, Player 2 is controller 2.

Difficulty

  • Easy: the computer slides slowly, reacts late, and rarely kicks.
  • Normal: the computer tracks the ball, defends its goal, and kicks when it gets a chance.
  • Hard: the computer is fast, reacts quickly, and kicks at almost every chance.
  • Difficulty only affects the computer. You can change it mid-match — it takes effect right away. Your choice is remembered for next time.

Practice round

  • Tap 🏋 Practice round to switch to a practice session at any time.
  • No score is kept, the clock doesn't run, and there is no winner — goals just re-serve so you can keep drilling.
  • A coaching hint shows above the table and changes after every goal.
  • Practice goals never count toward your bests. Tap the button again to return to a first-to-5 match.

Bests

  • Wins, matches played, fastest win, and best win streak are stored on this device only. Two-player wins count as a blue win.
