World Explorer · A calm geography game — explore at your own pace.

How to Play

1

Pick your level

Choose Ages 3–5, Grades 1–3, Grades 4–6, Grades 7–9, or Grades 10–12 at the top. Questions match your level — younger explorers get picture clues and animals, older explorers get capitals, history, and Earth science.

2

Pick a game

Tap a game tab: explore the map, match flags, follow clues, name capitals, or dig into history. Each round has 8 questions.

3

Answer big, friendly questions

On the map, tap the continent. Everywhere else, tap your answer. All the buttons are big and easy to tap.

4

Mistakes are part of exploring

If an answer is not right yet, you get a helpful hint and one more try. After that, the game shows you the answer kindly — no points are ever taken away.

5

Collect stars

You earn a star for every question you get right on the first try, plus a bonus star for finishing a round. Your stars are saved on this device.

6

Warm up with a practice round

Tap 🎈 Practice above the games any time to play a just-for-learning round: the hint is shown right away on every question, and no stars or streaks are saved. Tap it again to go back to earning stars. Switching Practice on or off starts the current game fresh.

Rules

Playing

  • Each round has 8 questions. There is no timer — take as long as you like.
  • You get two tries per question. The second try comes with a hint.
  • After two tries, the correct answer is shown so you can learn it for next time.
  • You can switch levels or games at any time. A round in progress simply starts fresh.

Stars

  • 1 star for each question answered correctly on the first try.
  • 1 bonus star for finishing a round, however it went.
  • Stars are never lost. They are saved on this device only.

Practice rounds

  • Tap 🎈 Practice to switch practice on or off. Practice can be used with any level and any game.
  • In a practice round, the hint is shown right away on every question — no need to miss first.
  • Practice rounds are just for learning: no stars are earned (not even the finishing bonus) and streaks are not saved.
  • Questions are badged 🎈 PRACTICE so you always know which kind of round you are in.
  • Switching Practice on or off starts the current game fresh, so a round is never half one kind and half the other.

Levels

  • Ages 3–5: continents, animal homes, and flag colours — big pictures, short words.
  • Grades 1–3: continents, famous places, and simple flags.
  • Grades 4–6: countries, capitals, landmarks, and trickier flags.
  • Grades 7–9: capitals, world regions, and history clues.
  • Grades 10–12: world history, physical geography, and advanced capitals.

For parents & guardians

  • No chat, no accounts, no personal information is asked for, and there are no ads and no outside links in the game.
  • The map is a simple learning sketch, drawn for this game — shapes are approximate so young explorers can tap them easily.
  • Use the Parent Feedback button to note anything you would like changed; the note downloads as a file you can share however you choose.
