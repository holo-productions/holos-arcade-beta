    Life Path — Rules

    The goal

    Reach Retirement (space 48) with the highest net worth: cash + your house's sale value − any loans still owing. Richest life wins. Ties go to whoever retired first.

    Starting out

    • Everyone starts with $10,000 cash at Start.
    • College Road: take a $40,000 student loan, unlock high-salary careers.
    • Career Road: no loan, start in a skilled hands-on career.

    On your turn

    • Spin the wheel (1–10) and move that many spaces. Overshooting Retirement still lands you there.
    • The Career space early on the board is a mandatory stop — you pick 1 of 2 job offers there.
    • Payday spaces pay your full salary. Married players add a $3,000 household bonus each payday.
    • Life spaces draw a Life Card — small fortunes and small disasters, all original.
    • Career spaces later on mean a promotion: +$10,000 salary.
    • House spaces let you buy (20% down, the rest becomes a mortgage) or pass. Your house sells at retirement for 25% more than you paid.
    • Family spaces offer marriage and kids — always your choice, never forced. Marriage brings $5,000 in gifts plus the payday bonus. Each kid costs $4,000 now and adds $8,000 to your retirement total (priceless, but we priced it anyway).
    • Tax spaces take 10% of your cash. Bonus spaces pay $5,000 found money.

    Retirement

    • First to retire pockets a $20,000 early-bird bonus.
    • Your house auto-sells (+25%), loans are subtracted, and each kid adds $8,000.
    • The other player keeps taking turns until they retire too — then totals are compared.

    Difficulty & Practice

    • Easy: Riley's wheel is a little sleepy (−1 per spin, min 1) and Riley earns 10% less.
    • Normal: a fair fight.
    • Hard: Riley earns 10% more and houses appreciate 40% for Riley.
    • Practice mode: play freely — results don't touch your saved win record.
