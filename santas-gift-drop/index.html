SANTA'S GIFT DROP

Santa's sleigh is loaded and the reindeer are restless. Catch the presents as they tumble from the night sky — but watch out for the coal. That is the naughty list falling on your head.

Best score: 0

Medium: steady snow, some coal

▶ PRESS START

HOW TO PLAY

  • Move the sleigh with the arrow keys, your mouse, or a finger drag. Santa does the catching — you do the steering.
  • Presents are worth +10. Catch them in the sleigh before they hit the snow.
  • Stockings hung by the chimney with care are worth +25.
  • Cookies & milk are rare — Santa's fuel. Catch a plate and he shares the energy back: +1 life (or +25 if you are already full).
  • Coal is straight off the naughty list. Catch a lump and you lose 1 life. Best not to ask whose stocking it was meant for.
  • Drop a present and you lose 1 life too. Some kid wakes up to an empty stocking. No pressure.
  • You have 3 lives. Every 10 presents caught, you level up — the snow falls faster — and you earn a +25 bonus.
  • Practice mode: no lives lost, no score saved — just a rehearsal before the big night.
  • Press P to pause. The reindeer will wait. Probably.

PAUSED

Take a breath. The reindeer are getting a snack.

GAME OVER

The sleigh is empty and the reindeer are laughing at you.
