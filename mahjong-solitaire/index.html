How to play

Mahjong Solitaire is a one-player matching puzzle played with a full set of mahjong tiles stacked in a pattern. Your job is to remove every tile from the board.

Quick tips

  • Free up the top layers and the long edges first — they block the most tiles.
  • If you see three identical free tiles, think about which pair to take: leave the one that unblocks more.
  • Flowers match any other flower, and seasons match any other season — save them for when you're stuck.

Rules

Objective

Remove all tiles from the board by matching them in pairs. You win when the board is completely empty. If tiles remain but no free matching pair exists, the board is blocked — undo a move or shuffle the remaining tiles to continue.

The free-tile rule

A tile may be selected only when it is free:

  • No other tile overlaps it from above, and
  • It has no neighbouring tile on its left side or no neighbouring tile on its right side (at least one long side must be open).

Both tiles of a pair must be free at the moment you match them. Blocked tiles are dimmed on the board and cannot be selected.

What counts as a match

  • Suits (Dots, Bamboo, Characters 1–9): both tiles must be the same suit and the same number.
  • Winds (East, South, West, North) and Dragons (Red, Green, White): both tiles must be identical.
  • Flowers: any flower tile matches any other flower tile.
  • Seasons: any season tile matches any other season tile.
  • Flowers do not match seasons.

Your score never drops below zero. The timer starts on your first match and stops when the board is cleared.

Deals & shuffling

Every deal is generated so that at least one complete solution exists. Shuffling rearranges only the faces of the tiles still on the board — tiles never change position or layer, and shuffling is allowed any number of times while tiles remain.

Practice round

Practice is a learning mode, not a scored game. It uses the same layouts, tiles, and free-tile rule, with extra help switched on:

  • Hints and shuffles are free on every difficulty.
  • Free tiles that are part of an available match get a gold outline.
  • The moves counter is always shown, even on Hard.
  • Pairs still show +100 as you play so you can learn the scoring, but there is no clear bonus or time bonus, and the round is not recorded in your bests, fastest clear, games played, or boards cleared.
  • Switching between Practice round and Standard game starts a fresh deal.

Layouts

  • Garden — 88 tiles, three layers, wide and forgiving.
  • Fortress — 72 tiles with a hollow centre; the inner walls free up late.
  • Bridge — 64 tiles, the shortest board — good for a quick game.
