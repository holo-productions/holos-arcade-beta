How to Play

📜 Rules of the Patch

🎯 The goal

Grow the legendary Grand Jack-o'-Lantern (the crowned one!) by merging pumpkins. The game ends when every column is full to the top and no pumpkin can drop.

🎃 How merging works

  • Tap a ▼ arrow (or anywhere in a column) to drop the next pumpkin.
  • When your pumpkin lands touching one or more pumpkins of the same size (up, down, left or right), they merge into the next size up.
  • Merging 3 or more at once earns bonus points — merge 4 or more and your pumpkin grows two sizes!
  • Merges can chain: after pumpkins settle, a new match may pop on its own. Each chain link multiplies your points.
  • Two Grand Jacks merging clears the extras for a big bonus — and makes room to keep growing.

Points are multiplied by how many pumpkins merged and by your chain count — bigger merges, bigger boos… er, boosts!

🌙 Difficulties

  • Cozy — a roomy 5 × 7 patch. Best for learning and relaxed merging.
  • Classic — the standard 5 × 6 patch.
  • Spooky — a snug 4 × 6 patch with trickier drops. Plan ahead!
  • Your difficulty and best score for each patch are saved on this device.

🧪 Practice mode

Practice is untimed and endless: scores aren't saved, a full patch just clears a little breathing room, and you get a Hint button and Undo for your last drop. Perfect for trying strategies.

💡 Patch tips

  • Keep your biggest pumpkins together in one corner so they can meet and merge.
  • Set up chains: drop so that one merge tumbles another pair together.
  • Watch the "On deck" pumpkin — plan two drops ahead, not one.
