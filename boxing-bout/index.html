Rules - Boxing Bout

Goal

Land timing punches to empty Rival health before yours runs out. Win 2 rounds to win the match. Score points for every clean hit and build a combo for bonus points.

How a punch works

  • One zone on Rival glows gold: Head, Left body, or Right body. The matching punch button also lights up.
  • Tap that zone on the ring or tap the matching punch button before the glow fades.
  • A fast correct punch lands for damage and points. A late or wrong punch is blocked and breaks your combo.
  • Every few seconds Rival winds up with a warning flash. Tap Block before the swing lands to take no damage. Miss the block and you lose health and your combo.
  • Each round lasts 60 seconds in a real match. If time runs out, the boxer with more health left wins the round. Practice has no timer.

Practice mode

Untimed sparring. Rival swings softer and the glow window is longer. Health refills when a round ends so you can keep learning. Practice scores do not save. End any time with End round or End match.

Safety note

This is a cartoon game with stars, boings, and wobbly knees. There is no gore and no real contact. It is play money only in spirit: points are just for fun, with no prizes.
