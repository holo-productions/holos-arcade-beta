How to play

1
Tap a bolt

Tap a bolt to unscrew it.

2
Match the box

It flies into a same-colour box at the top.

3
Fill with 3

Fill a box with 3 bolts to clear it.

4
Drop the plate

A plate with no bolts left drops.

5
Clear to win

Clear every plate to win.

6
Hidden bolts

Bolts under a plate can't be tapped until it drops.

Rules

  • Each box holds 3 bolts of one colour, then clears.
  • A bolt under a plate is blocked until the plate above drops.
  • Tap a bolt with no matching box space and nothing happens — clear a box first.
  • +20 per bolt, +100 per plate, +200 win bonus.
  • Practice mode never saves to your best.
