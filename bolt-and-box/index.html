How to play

Tap a bolt to unscrew it. It flies into a box of the same colour at the top. Fill a box with 3 bolts to clear it and reveal the next colour. When a plate loses all its bolts, it drops. Clear every plate to win!

Bolts hidden under another plate can't be tapped until that plate drops.

Rules

  • Each box holds 3 bolts of one colour, then clears.
  • A bolt under a plate is blocked until the plate above drops.
  • Tap a bolt with no matching box space and nothing happens — clear a box first.
  • +20 per bolt, +100 per plate, +200 win bonus.
  • Practice mode never saves to your best.
