Rules - Volleyball Rally

Goal

Keep the ball off the floor on your side and land it on the CPU side to score. Win sets to win the match. First to win 2 sets wins.

How a rally works

  • The winner of the last rally serves next. Tap SERVE or press Space to serve.
  • Move with Left and Right buttons, arrow keys, or by dragging on the court. Stay on your side of the net.
  • When the ball is close, tap HIT, tap the court, or press Space. Your player bumps the ball over the net. Where you stand and whether you jump changes the angle.
  • Use Jump for high balls near the net to hit with more power. Jump too early and you will whiff.
  • If the ball hits the floor on your side, the CPU scores. If it lands on the CPU side, you score. Hitting the net or sending it out the back also loses the rally.

How to play

A 20 second tour, then you are on the court:

1. Pick a difficulty and start

Easy has a slow CPU and short sets to 11. Hard has a fast CPU and long sets to 21. Your pick is remembered for next time. Press Start match to begin.

2. Serve the ball

When it is your serve, tap SERVE. When the CPU serves, get ready to move. The winner of each rally serves next.

3. Move under the ball

Hold Left and Right, use arrow keys, or drag on the court. Watch the ball shadow on the sand to judge where it will land.

4. Hit it over the net

Tap HIT when the ball is close to your player. Jump first for high balls near the net. Aim by where you stand: closer to the net sends a flatter ball, farther back sends a higher arc.

5. Win sets and the match

Land the ball on the CPU side to score. Reach the set target with a lead of 2 to win the set. Win 2 sets to win the match. Warm up in Practice mode for endless gentle rallies.
