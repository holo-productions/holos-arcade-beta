📜 Rules

Goal: Answer more trivia questions correctly than your friends. The player with the most points at the end wins.

How a game works

  • Pick 2, 3 or 4 players and give everyone a name.
  • Pick one category — or choose Mixed for questions from every category.
  • Pick how many questions each player answers: 5, 10 or 15.
  • Players take turns. The banner at the top shows whose turn it is — pass the device to that player.
  • Tap one of the four answers. Correct answers earn points; wrong answers earn 0.

Difficulty & points

  • 😌 Chill: No timer. 100 points per correct answer.
  • 🎯 Classic: 20 seconds per question. 100 points + up to 50 speed bonus for fast answers.
  • 🔥 Expert: 12 seconds per question. 150 points + up to 50 speed bonus. Harder questions come first.
  • If time runs out, that question scores 0 and play moves on.
  • Your difficulty choice is saved on this device for next time.

Winning

  • When everyone has answered all their questions, the winner screen shows the final standings.
  • If two or more players tie for first place, it's a shared win — everyone tied gets the trophy!

Practice mode

  • Practice is solo and endless. Tap "Practice solo" to warm up on your own.
  • Practice scores are not saved and don't count toward your bests.

Bests

  • Your best game score, best streak and games played are saved on this device (in your browser) and shown on the setup screen.

❓ How to play

  • 1. Choose 2–4 players and type in everyone's name.
  • 2. Pick a category (Animals, Food, Sports, Movies, Geography, Science) or Mixed for a bit of everything.
  • 3. Pick a difficulty and how many questions each player gets.
  • 4. Tap Start game. The banner shows whose turn it is — pass the device to them.
  • 5. Read the question and tap one of the four answers before the timer runs out.
  • 6. Watch the scoreboard after every question. Most points at the end wins!
  • 🎓 Practice solo lets one player warm up — practice scores aren't saved.
