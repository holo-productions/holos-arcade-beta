📖 Rules - Canoe Rapids

🎯 Goal

Paddle 2,600m down the rapids, thread all 6 pink flag gates, and stay upright. Three capsizes ends your run.

🕹️ How to paddle

  • Paddle left / right (A / D or the buttons) — each stroke adds speed. Alternating strokes gives a bonus; same-side strokes in a row make you veer.
  • Steer with ← → or by dragging on the river. The current pushes you around — steer early.
  • Brace (↑, hold) — steadies the balance meter fast, but slows you down. Use it when the meter swings wild.
  • Other side (Space) — a quick stroke on whichever side you didn't just use. Handy for keeping the rhythm.
  • Grey rocks and brown logs cost one capsize and knock your balance sideways. The riverbank also shoves your balance around.
