▶ How to Play

Three friendly games, one idea each. Here's how they work:

1
Pick your level

Ages 3–5 up to Grades 10–12. Higher levels add angles, symmetry and 3D shapes.

2
Match It

Read or listen for the clue, then tap the matching shape.

3
Sort It

One shape appears — tap the basket it belongs in.

4
Name It Quiz

Tap a big button to answer. Older levels ask angles and patterns.

5
Practice Round

Same questions, no stars or score. Tap “I’m Ready” when confident.

6
Earn stars

Right answers earn stars in a Real Game. No timers, no losing.

📖 Rules

The games

  • Each Real Game lasts 10 rounds. Tap the big buttons to answer.
  • A right answer in a Real Game earns 1 star (2 stars on the last round of a perfect game!). Stars are saved on this device.
  • Practice Round 🌱 is always available before you play: same questions, unlimited rounds, no stars, no score, and nothing is saved as a result. Use it to warm up or try a harder level. Tap “I’m Ready — Play Real Game” to switch back anytime.
  • Not right yet? You can keep trying — the round only moves on when you find it. Nothing is ever taken away.
  • There are no timers. Take as long as you like.

The levels

  • Ages 3–5: first colours and shapes — circles, squares, triangles, stars, hearts.
  • Grades 1–3: more colours and shapes, colour + shape together, simple patterns.
  • Grades 4–6: counting sides and corners, 2D shape families, first symmetry and patterns.
  • Grades 7–9: geometry names, angle types (acute, right, obtuse), lines of symmetry.
  • Grades 10–12: 3D shapes (faces, edges, vertices), transformations (slide, flip, turn, enlarge), pattern logic.

Staying safe

  • No chat, no accounts, no personal information, no ads, and no links to other websites.
  • Stars and the chosen level are stored only in this browser on this device.
  • Parent feedback stays on this page — you copy or download it yourself; nothing is sent anywhere.
