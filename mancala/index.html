How to play Mancala

Mancala — full rules

This game uses the classic Kalah rules for two players.

The board & setup

  • Each player has 6 small pits on their side of the board and one large store at their right-hand end.
  • Every small pit starts with 4 stones — 48 stones in total. Both stores start empty.
  • Against the AI, you play the bottom row and Zuri plays the top row. In 2 Players mode you share the device and take turns.

On your turn — sowing

  • Choose one of your own non-empty pits. You pick up all of its stones.
  • Moving counter-clockwise, drop one stone into each space you pass: your following pits, your store, then your opponent's pits, and so on around the board.
  • You skip your opponent's store entirely — no stone is ever dropped in it, and it doesn't count as a space.
  • If you have more stones than spaces in a full lap, you simply keep going around.

Special rule — extra turn

  • If the last stone you sow lands in your own store, you take another turn immediately.
  • Extra turns can chain: each time your last stone lands in your store, you go again.

Special rule — capture

  • If the last stone you sow lands in an empty pit on your own side, and the pit directly opposite on your opponent's side holds at least one stone, you capture: that last stone plus all stones in the opposite pit move straight into your store.
  • If the opposite pit is empty, there is no capture — the stone simply stays where it landed.
  • Captures only happen on your own side, and only with your final stone of the move.

Scoring & how the game ends

  • Your score is the number of stones in your store. Stones still sitting in small pits don't count until they're captured, sown in, or swept.
  • The game ends as soon as all six pits on either side are empty — it doesn't matter whose turn it is.
  • When that happens, every stone remaining in the other player's pits is swept into that player's store.

Winning

  • After the final sweep, the player with the most stones in their store wins. With 48 stones in play, 25 or more guarantees the win.
  • A 24–24 finish is a draw.
  • In Vs Zuri games, wins, losses, draws, your best winning margin, and your current win streak are saved on this device. Two-player games aren't recorded.

Practice round

  • Tap Practice at the top to play a practice round. Same Kalah rules, but Zuri plays gently (like Easy) so you can learn at your own pace.
  • Practice rounds don't count — they never change your Played / Won / Lost record, best win, or streak.
  • Use Hint and Undo as much as you like, restart anytime with Restart practice, and switch to Vs Zuri when you're ready for a real game.
  • Short coaching notes appear under the board during practice to explain extra turns and captures as they happen.

Difficulty (Vs Zuri)

  • Easy — Zuri plays casually, picks mostly at random, and only sometimes spots a capture or extra turn. Best while you're learning.
  • Normal — Zuri thinks a few moves ahead and takes captures and extra turns when she sees them. A fair, standard game.
  • Hard — Zuri plans many moves ahead, chains extra turns, and sets up captures. Beating her takes real strategy.
  • Pick a level with the Easy / Normal / Hard switch above the board. Changing it starts a fresh game, and your choice is remembered on this device. Difficulty doesn't apply in 2 Players mode.
