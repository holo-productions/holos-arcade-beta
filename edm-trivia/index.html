How to play

EDM Trivia in 4 steps

Answer as many questions as you can before the clock runs out — fast, correct answers score the most.

  1. 1
    Pick your set — or practice firstChoose a mode (Quick Fire, Deep Dive, Daily Drop, or Practice Round), a category, and a difficulty — Easy (easy questions, +5s), Normal (mixed questions, standard time), or Hard (tougher questions, −5s, ×1.5 points). Practice Round is 10 untimed, unscored questions with unlimited 50/50 and Skip — it never counts toward your score or badges. Then tap Start.
  2. 2
    Tap an answer, beat the clockEach question has 4 choices and a timer — 15 seconds in Quick Fire, 30 in Deep Dive, adjusted by your difficulty (+5s on Easy, −5s on Hard). Tap one answer before time runs out.
  3. 3
    Use your lifelines wiselyYou get one of each per game: 50/50 removes two wrong answers, Skip moves on with your streak kept, and + Time adds 10 seconds.
  4. 4
    Score, streak, and finish strongCorrect = base points (×1.5 on Hard) + a time bonus + 🔥 streak bonus. A wrong answer or a timeout breaks your streak. There's no sudden loss — finish the set to earn stars and badges based on your accuracy.

Rules

EDM Trivia rules

The full rules — objective, scoring, stars, difficulty and practice mode.

  1. 1
    🎯 The objectiveAnswer EDM trivia questions correctly before the clock runs out. Quick Fire is 10 questions at 15 seconds each, Deep Dive is 20 questions at 30 seconds each, and Daily Drop is 10 questions in a fixed mix for the day. Pick one category or All mixed. Every set runs to the last question — there's no sudden-death loss.
  2. 2
    🧮 How scoring worksCorrect answer = 100 base points + a time bonus (up to +50 — the more time left, the more points) + a streak bonus (🔥 +10 for each correct answer already in your current streak). On Hard the total is multiplied ×1.5. A wrong answer or a timeout scores 0 and resets your streak to 0. Skip scores 0 but keeps your streak. Lifelines are one each per scored game: 50/50 removes two wrong answers, Skip moves on, +10s adds 10 seconds.
  3. 3
    🏆 Stars, badges & finishingYou can't lose mid-set — finish all the questions, or tap End set to finish early with your current score. Stars come from accuracy: 90%+ = 3 stars, 60%+ = 2 stars, 30%+ = 1 star. Badges: 🎟 First Drop (finish your first scored set), 🔥 On Fire (5+ streak), 💯 Perfect Set (100%), 🎧 Festival Headliner (90%+), 📅 Daily Drop Done, 🔥 Hard Mode Clear (60%+ on Hard). Best score, games played and badges last for this session only.
  4. 4
    🎚 Difficulty differencesEasy: easy questions only, +5 seconds per question, ×1 points. Normal: easy + medium mix, standard time, ×1 points. Hard: medium + hard questions, −5 seconds per question (never below 10 seconds), ×1.5 points. If your category doesn't have enough questions at your difficulty, the set tops up from the nearest difficulty levels.
  5. 5
    🧪 Practice modePractice Round is 10 questions with no timer and no points. 50/50 and Skip are unlimited (they reset every question); there's no +Time because there's no clock. Practice never changes your best score, games played, streaks or badges — it's purely for learning, with the full explanation after every answer.
