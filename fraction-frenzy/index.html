Rules

Objective

Answer fraction questions — visual fractions, simplifying, equivalents, comparing, adding and subtracting, and (on Hard) mixed numbers and improper fractions. There are no opponents — you are playing against the clock, the question count, and your own saved bests.

Modes & win conditions

  • Sprint (60 seconds): Answer as many questions as possible before time runs out. You "win" the round by beating your previous best Sprint score at that difficulty. The game ends the moment the timer hits zero.
  • Challenge (20 questions): A fixed set of 20 questions with no countdown. Finish all 20 to complete the round; your grade is set by accuracy (below). You "win" by earning a higher grade or score than your saved best.
  • Practice (unlimited): No timer, no time penalty, and no saved score. Answer as many or as few as you like, take as long as you need on each one, and tap End practice whenever you're done. Wrong answers show the correct answer and the explanation a little longer so you can learn from it. Practice doesn't change your bests or grades — it's just for warming up and drilling.

Scoring

DifficultyPoints per correct answer
Easy — visual fractions, halves, quarters, thirds10
Medium — simplify, equivalents, compare, like denominators20
Hard — unlike denominators, mixed & improper fractions30
  • Streak bonus: every 5th correct answer in a row adds a bonus equal to half the question's points (rounded down). The streak counter turns pink while a streak of 5+ is live.
  • Wrong answers score 0 and reset the streak to zero. A wrong answer shows the correct answer and a one-line explanation.
  • There is no partial credit and no skipping — every question must be answered to move on.
  • Practice is not scored: points show during the round so you can see how you're doing, but nothing is saved to your bests and no grade is given.

Difficulty bands

DifficultyWhat it covers
EasyVisual fractions, halves / quarters / thirds — below / early Grade 4.
MediumThe Saskatchewan Math Grades 4–6 core: simplify, equivalent fractions, compare, add / subtract with like denominators.
HardAbove Grade 6: unlike denominators, mixed numbers, improper fractions.

Special rules

  • Sprint time penalty: a wrong answer in Sprint mode subtracts 3 seconds from the remaining time. Challenge mode has no time penalty.
  • Lowest terms: on Hard visual questions, and on Medium / Hard adding and subtracting, answers are given in lowest terms. Watch out — the unreduced form often appears as a wrong option.
  • Subtraction: the answer is never negative.
  • Ending early: "End game" finishes the round immediately and shows results for the questions answered so far. A round with zero answered questions is not recorded.

Saved bests

Your best Sprint score, best Challenge score, and longest streak are stored in this browser on this device only. They are kept separately for each difficulty. Clearing your browser data resets them. Your difficulty choice is also remembered on this device.

Source

How answers are checked

Every answer in this game is generated and checked by computation — greatest common divisor for simplifying, common denominators for adding and subtracting, cross-multiplication for comparing — not copied from a trivia source. Aligned to Saskatchewan Math, Grades 4–6 (fractions outcome).
