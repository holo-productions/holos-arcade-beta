Rules — Simplified 8-Ball

Goal: Pot all balls in your group (solids 1–7 or stripes 9–15), then pot the black 8-ball to win.

  • Open table: At the start no one owns solids or stripes. The first ball legally potted (after the break) assigns groups.
  • Keep shooting: Pot a ball from your group and you shoot again. Miss or pot nothing and the turn passes.
  • The 8-ball last: Only aim for the 8 after your group is cleared. Potting the 8 early loses the game instantly.
  • Scratch = foul: If the cue ball goes in a pocket, your opponent gets ball in hand — they can place the cue ball anywhere.
  • Wrong group: Potting only your opponent's balls doesn't keep your turn — play passes over.
  • Break: You always break. Balls potted on the break stay down, but groups stay open until the next shot.
  • Winning / losing: Clear your group + pot the 8 = win. Pot the 8 early or pot the 8 + scratch on the same shot = loss.

🎱 Practice Round

Practice is separate from match play: solo table, no opponent, no timer, and nothing is saved to your wins, streak or fewest-shots stats.

  • Any order: Pot any ball — no groups are assigned in practice.
  • No losing: Potting the 8 early respots it; a scratch just gives you ball in hand.
  • Hints on: “Show hint” names the easiest ball, pocket and power; “Aim hint for me” lines it up for you.
  • Retry tools: Ball in hand any time, bring potted balls back, re-spot the 8, scatter the balls for a fresh layout, jump to the next drill, and drills for breaks, straight pots, scatter clears and the 8-ball finish.

This is a friendly simplified version: no called pockets, no rail-after-contact rule, and no double-hit or jump-shot penalties — just aim, pot, and plan.
