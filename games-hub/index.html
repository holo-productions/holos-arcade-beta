          How do I register?

          Use your invite link. It lands you straight on the Create Account form with your invite code already filled in — that's the register page. The plain login page is sign-in only.

          • Pick a username and a password, then type the password again to confirm it.
          • Add your email — on the beta site, Firebase sends a verification link to it (see the next question).
          • Tick the box to agree to the Terms & Policies.
          • Tap create — you'll see a “You're registered!” message.
          • Then head to the login page and sign in with the username and password you just chose.

          No invite link handy? On the login page, tap “New here? Create your account” to open the same form. Keep your username and password somewhere safe — sign-in is always your name + password.

          How does email verification work?

          Sign-up on the beta site (beta.holosarcade.ca) runs on Firebase Auth. When you create your account there, Firebase sends a verification email to the address you registered with — just tap the link in that email to verify it's really yours.

          • The email comes from Firebase, the arcade's sign-in provider — check your spam or junk folder if it doesn't show up within a few minutes.
          • The link brings you back to beta.holosarcade.ca — then sign in with your username and password as usual.
          • Registering on this preview link saves your email with your account, but the verification email itself is only sent from the beta site, where Firebase Auth is switched on.
          How do I sign in next time?

          Type your username and password, and tick the Terms & Policies box — it's ticked fresh every time you sign in, on purpose. Tick “Remember me” if you want to stay signed in on this device; leave it unticked and you'll be signed out when you close the tab.

          How do I play?

          Everything plays right here on this page — nothing to install, nothing to download. Browse the shelves, use Library for one A–Z list of every game, Categories for the games grouped by shelf, or search by name — then tap ▶ Play.

          There's no set order and no checklist: play any game you fancy, in any order, for as long as you like. Game of the Day up top is a daily pick if you want a suggestion, and 🎲 Surprise me picks one at random.

          Where are How to Play and the Rules?

          Inside each game, first in its options: ❓ How to play opens a quick opt-in tutorial (goal, controls & scoring), and 📜 Rules covers scoring, special features and how the game ends. Every game has both — open them any time, as often as you like.

          What about difficulty and modes?

          Most games let you pick a difficulty and a mode on their start screen before you play — for example, trivia offers Quick Fire, Deep Dive or One Subject Only, and Capture the Werewolf has 3 difficulties plus Timed and Endless Night hunts. New to a game? Start on the easiest setting and step up when it feels too easy. You can change your pick whenever you start a new game.

          What's a practice round?

          Games with a 🎓 Practice button let you learn the ropes first — depending on the game that means no timer, extra hints or refilled hearts. Practice scores are not saved and don't count, so experiment freely. When you're ready, tap ▶ Play for real in the practice banner at the top of the game.

          How do I leave feedback or a review?

          Tap 🦆 Report back — the button at the bottom right, and also in the game bar while you're playing. Give stars, tap the quick picks (Was it fun? Did anything go wonky? How was the difficulty?), tag it 🐞 Bug, 💡 Suggestion or 💬 Other, and write whatever you like. No essays needed — honest and specific beats long.

          Reports autosave as you type and live in this browser on this device. Your Fans page (top nav) keeps your stamps, fun challenges and all your reports in one place, with buttons to copy them all and paste them to Holo. Found a bug? Report it there — Holo watches tester reports and fixes bugs as they come in.

          Do my points count? What are points for?

          Yes — points count. You earn points just by playing games. Playing also earns stamps — one for every different game you try — plus badges and Game-of-the-Day streaks, all waiting on your Fans page and in Your Arcade on the home page.

          Which devices can I play on?

          Any device with a web browser — phone, tablet, laptop or desktop. There's no app to install. For now, your account lives in the browser on the device where you created it — accounts don't travel between devices yet (one login everywhere arrives later, with the planned online accounts). So register and sign in on each device you want to play on, and your best scores, badges, streaks and paused games are saved per device too — your progress can look different on your phone than on your laptop.
