📖 Rules - Maze Chase

🎯 Goal

Eat every dot in the maze to clear the level while avoiding the Chompers. Clear levels to keep going. Each new level is a little faster. You lose a life when a Chomper catches Pip, and the game ends when lives run out.

🕹️ How to play

  • Move Pip with the direction pad, a swipe on the maze, arrow keys, or WASD. Pip keeps moving until a wall blocks the way, and turns as soon as the path is open.
  • Small dots are worth 10 points. Big spark pellets in the corners are worth 50 points.
  • A spark pellet gives Pip power for a few seconds. Chompers turn pale blue and run away. Catch one while powered for 200 points and it goes back to the middle pen.
  • When power is about to end, Chompers flash white. Get clear before they turn back.
  • Use Pause any time. Use End round to finish early and see your summary.
