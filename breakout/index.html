Rules

The full rules for this version of Breakout — scoring, special rules, and how a game is won or lost.

Practice round

  • Practice is a separate mode, chosen with the Mode buttons under the board. It applies from your next new game (or straight away from the start screen's Practice button).
  • In Practice: no score is saved, your Best never changes, there is no game over (unlimited balls), play is untimed, the ball speed stays at a steady slower pace and does not ramp up, and hints + a dashed aim guide are shown.
  • Bricks, power-ups, levels, and paddle controls work exactly as in Play mode, so practice transfers directly. Level-clear bonuses are not awarded in Practice.
  • Switching back to Play starts scoring again on your next new game.

Difficulty

  • Easy: start with 5 balls · paddle 138px wide · ball base speed 4.2 (cap 7.5) · power-up drop about 1 in 5 bricks · armoured bricks only from Level 3 and at half the Normal rate.
  • Normal (default): start with 3 balls · paddle 110px wide · ball base speed 5.2 (cap 9.5) · power-up drop about 1 in 7 bricks · armoured bricks from Level 2.
  • Hard: start with 3 balls · paddle 92px wide · ball base speed 6.6 (cap 12) · power-up drop about 1 in 9 bricks · armoured bricks from Level 2 at a higher rate.
  • Pick difficulty under the board. A change made while a run is in progress applies from the next new game — it never changes a ball, paddle, or wall mid-run.
  • Extra life (★) can raise your reserve to at most 2 above your difficulty's starting balls (Easy max 7, Normal / Hard max 5).

Objective & win condition

  • There is no final level — play is endless. Clear a wall to advance to the next level; each level adds challenge (see below).
  • A run ends when you run out of balls. Your final score is compared against your saved best.
  • Clearing a level awards a 500-point bonus plus 100 points per ball in reserve.

  • Combo: bricks smashed in a row without the ball touching the paddle build a combo. Every 5th brick in a combo earns double points for that brick. Touching the paddle resets the combo.
  • On higher levels some bricks are armoured (darker, with a border): they need 2 hits. The first hit cracks them (no points); the second smashes them for full points.

Lives & the ball

  • You start with 5 balls on Easy, 3 on Normal / Hard. Losing the last ball in play costs 1 reserve ball.
  • The ball speeds up slightly with every brick smashed and every level, up to a cap. Your Difficulty sets the starting speed and the cap (see Difficulty above).
  • The ball never bounces perfectly horizontally — a minimum vertical angle is enforced so rallies can't stall forever.
  • The ball bounces off the left, right, and top walls. There is no bottom wall.

Power-ups — special rules

  • How often a smashed brick drops a power-up depends on difficulty: about 1 in 5 on Easy, 1 in 7 on Normal, 1 in 9 on Hard. It only works if the paddle catches it; if it falls past, it's gone.
  • Wide (W) lasts 12 seconds. Catching another Wide while active resets the timer — it doesn't stack wider.
  • Slow (S) lasts 10 seconds and cuts ball speed by 30%. It doesn't stack.
  • Extra ball (+) adds one ball in play, launched from the paddle at an angle. Max 3 balls in play at once.
  • Extra life (★) adds 1 reserve ball, up to 2 above your difficulty's starting balls (max 7 on Easy, 5 on Normal / Hard).
  • All timed effects end immediately when you lose a ball or clear a level.

Levels

  • Level 1 starts with 6 rows. Each new level adds 1 row, up to 10 rows.
  • Wall patterns rotate by level: full wall, checkerboard gaps, columns, and a diamond.
  • Armoured bricks appear from Level 2 onward, more of them each level.

Pausing & fairness

  • Press P, Space (while playing), or the Pause button to pause. The game also auto-pauses if you switch tabs.
  • Changing Difficulty applies from the next new game — it never changes a ball, paddle, or wall already in play.
