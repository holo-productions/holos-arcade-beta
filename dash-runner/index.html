How to play

Easy, Normal, or Hard — your choice is remembered.

2

Press Start Run; your runner takes off automatically.

3
Jump obstacles

Jump crates and spikes with button, tap, or Space.

4

Tap jump again mid-air for a double jump.

5
Duck drones

Hold Duck under drones — jumping into one still hits.

Run through gold stars for 25 points each.

Hitting an obstacle costs a heart; lose all and run ends.

The track speeds up the longer you survive.

9

Practice: steady speed, hits don't cost hearts or count.

📜 Rules & scoring

Goal

Run as far as you can on an endless track. Dodge every obstacle and collect stars. The run ends when you lose your last heart.

Difficulty

DifficultyStart speedHeartsObstacle spacing
😌 EasySlow3Wide
🎯 NormalMedium2Medium
🔥 HardFast1Tight

On every difficulty the track keeps speeding up the longer you run, up to a top-speed cap. Obstacles appear in a random order — no two runs are the same.

Practice round

A practice round uses your chosen difficulty's starting speed, but the track never speeds up. Hitting an obstacle doesn't cost a heart and never ends the run — hits are just counted so you can see how you're improving. Practice scores and stars are shown at the end but are never saved as a best score. End the round any time with the ⏹ End practice button.
