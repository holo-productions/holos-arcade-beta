How to play

A quick tour — no forced walkthrough, just the basics. Read it once and you're set.

🎯 The goal

  • Tap (or click) a balloon to throw a dart at it. Each tap uses one dart.
  • Pop the target number of balloons (shown as “Popped x/y” up top) before your darts run out to clear the level.
  • Clearing a level earns a bonus: +2 tickets for every dart you have left. Then the next board is bigger and trickier.
  • Run out of darts before hitting the target and the game ends — your ticket total is your score.

🎈 Know your balloons

  • Normal — pops in one dart, worth 2 tickets.
  • Gold ⭐ — worth 5 tickets and gives you an extra dart. Pop these first!
  • Star ✨ — rare and worth a whopping 10 tickets.
  • Tough 🛡 — needs two darts (the first one just cracks it), worth 4 tickets.
  • Soggy 💧 — worth nothing and breaks your streak. Skip these when you can spot them (they're the dull grey ones).

🔥 Streaks

Pop balloons back-to-back without hitting a soggy one to build a streak. Every 3 pops in a row adds a +1 ticket bonus. A soggy balloon resets the streak to zero.

🎯 Practice round

  • Pick 🎯 Practice under the board (or the Practice button on the start screen) to warm up.
  • Unlimited darts, no score saved, no game over — your Best never changes.
  • Bonus balloons glow in Practice so you can learn to spot gold and star balloons.
  • Pop the whole board (or hit the target) and use Next board to keep practising. Switch back to ▶ Play and start a New game when you're ready to score.

⚙️ Difficulty

Easy gives you more darts and almost no soggy balloons. Hard gives fewer darts, more tough and soggy balloons, and rarer gold. Your pick is saved on this device and applies from your next new game.

Rules

Objective

Pop at least the level's target number of balloons before your darts run out. Each cleared level advances you to a bigger, harder board. The game ends when you run out of darts before reaching the target. Your score is your total tickets.

Darts & popping

  • Every tap/click on an unpopped balloon throws one dart and uses it up — including taps on tough balloons that only crack them.
  • Tapping an already-popped space does nothing and costs no dart.
  • Gold balloons refund a dart (+1) when popped, so a gold pop is effectively free.

Tickets (scoring)

  • Normal balloon: 2 tickets.
  • Gold ⭐ balloon: 5 tickets + 1 dart.
  • Star ✨ balloon: 10 tickets.
  • Tough 🛡 balloon: 4 tickets (awarded when it finally pops on the second dart; the cracking dart scores nothing).
  • Soggy 💧 balloon: 0 tickets and your streak resets to 0.
  • Streak bonus: every 3rd pop in an unbroken streak adds +1 ticket (so pops 3, 6, 9… of a streak pay an extra ticket).
  • Level-clear bonus: +2 tickets per unused dart when you hit the target. Leftover darts do not carry to the next level — each level starts with a fresh set.

Levels

  • Level 1 starts with a 4×3 board. Boards grow as levels rise (up to 6×6), targets rise, and tough/soggy balloons get more common.
  • After Level 10 the boards stay at their biggest size but targets and tricky balloons keep creeping up — how far can you get?
  • Your best level reached is saved alongside your best ticket total, per difficulty.

Difficulty

  • Easy: +4 darts per level, very few tough balloons, no soggy balloons, gold is common.
  • Normal: standard darts, some tough balloons, a few soggy ones.
  • Hard: −2 darts per level, many tough and soggy balloons, gold is rare.
  • Difficulty is chosen under the board, saved on this device, and applies from your next new game — it never changes a board mid-level. Best scores are tracked separately for each difficulty.

Practice round

  • Practice is a separate mode chosen with the Mode buttons (or the Practice buttons on the start screen / under the board).
  • In Practice: unlimited darts, no score is saved (Best and best level never change), no game over, and bonus balloons glow as a hint.
  • Tickets shown in Practice are just for fun and are never recorded. Hitting the target (or popping the whole board) offers the next board.

Play money only

Tickets are play money for fun inside this game only. There is no real-money wagering, no cash prizes, and no way to buy or cash out tickets. Balloon Pop is part of the Ridiculously Fit and Fed games arcade — free and just for fun.
