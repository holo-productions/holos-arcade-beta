Rules

Everything you need to command the squad.

Goal & turns

Defeat every enemy on the field to win the battle. Win all 5 campaign battles to clear the campaign. If your whole squad falls, the battle is lost — retry it with the HP your squad had when the battle began.

You and the enemy alternate turns. On your turn, each of your units may move once and act once (attack, heal, or use its ability), in either order. Press Wait to finish a unit early, or End turn to hand over. Surviving units keep their remaining HP between battles, with a healing choice after each victory.

Terrain

  • Forest: costs 2 movement to enter. A unit standing in forest takes 2 less damage from ranged attacks (range 2+).
  • Hill: a unit attacking from a hill gets +1 range and +2 damage.
  • Water: completely blocked — nobody can enter or cross it.

Damage preview

Tap a highlighted target once to see the exact damage (after hill and forest modifiers), then tap again or press Confirm to strike. Healing shows a green preview the same way.

Enemies

Brigands, Slingers, Warlocks, Acolytes and Brutes — the same five classes in enemy colours, growing in number and strength each battle. Battle 5 is led by Warlord Brakk, a boss Knight with extra HP and damage.

Difficulty & modes

  • Easy: enemies have less HP and damage, and the AI plays loosely, sometimes wandering.
  • Normal: standard stats; the AI advances and attacks the nearest unit.
  • Hard: enemies have more HP and damage; the AI focuses your weakest unit, seeks hills and forest, and uses abilities whenever they pay off.
  • Practice mode: same battles, but wins don't count toward your bests and you can Undo your moves and actions.

Scoring & bests

Winning a battle scores 100 points, +10 for each surviving squad member, +25 if you finish in 6 rounds or fewer, and +50 if you press on without healing between battles. Your battles won and campaigns cleared are kept as bests on this device.
