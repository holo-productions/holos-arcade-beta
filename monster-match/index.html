Rules

The goal

Find all the matching monster pairs in as few moves and as little time as you can.

How a turn works

Two flips make one move. Match the pair and both cards stay face-up; miss and they flip back — remembering where they were is the whole game!

Difficulty

  • Easy: 6 pairs (12 cards) — great for little monsters.
  • Medium: 8 pairs (16 cards) — the classic game.
  • Hard: 12 pairs (24 cards) — for memory masters.
  • Your difficulty choice is saved automatically for next time.

Scoring (Play & Score mode)

  • Each match: +100 points.
  • Finish fast and in few moves for a time & moves bonus.
  • Hints cost 25 points and Peek costs 50 points in scored play. In Practice Mode they're free.
  • Your best score, time and moves are saved per difficulty on this device.

Practice Mode

No timer, no score pressure, unlimited hints and peeks. Perfect for learning the monsters or playing with younger kids. Practice games don't overwrite your best scores.
