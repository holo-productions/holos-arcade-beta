    Word Search — Full Rules

    Everything that decides a game: how words are placed, how scoring works, and exactly what counts as a win.

    1 · The puzzle

    • Every puzzle is generated fresh: words from the chosen theme (or your own custom words) are placed in straight lines on the grid, and every remaining square is filled with a random letter.
    • Words may cross and share letters where the letters match. A word that cannot be placed after repeated tries is left out, so a custom list can occasionally come back one word short.
    • Only words on the list count. The same letters appearing somewhere else by chance do not score — a claim must match a listed word, in its placed line, at its full length.

    2 · Directions by size

    SizeGridWordsAllowed directions
    Easy10 × 108Across (left→right) and down only
    Medium12 × 1211Across, down, and both diagonals (forwards)
    Hard15 × 1514All 8 directions, including backwards and straight up
    • A claim can be dragged in either direction along the word's line — dragging last-letter-to-first also counts, on every size.
    • Selections must be one straight line (horizontal, vertical, or 45° diagonal). Bent or L-shaped drags snap to the nearest straight line and normally won't match.

    3 · Claiming a word

    • Drag from the first letter to the last and release, or tap the first letter and then the last letter. Minimum claim length is 3 letters.
    • A correct claim colours the word's letters, crosses it off the list, and adds 1 to your Found count and to your lifetime Total words found.
    • An incorrect claim scores nothing and carries no penalty: no time added, no hint lost, no streak broken (there are no streaks).
    • A word can only be claimed once. A word already claimed — by you or by Lexi — cannot be claimed again.

    4 · The clock & scoring (Solo)

    • The clock starts on your first drag or tap on the grid (or your first Hint), not when the puzzle loads. Opening How to play, the Tutorial tour, or these Rules pauses the clock; it resumes on your next drag.
    • Solo has no points — your score is your finishing time. A puzzle is complete only when every listed word has been found.
    • Your best (fastest) finishing time is kept separately for each grid size (Easy / Medium / Hard) and shown under “Best time (this size)”.
    • Custom-word puzzles count toward Puzzles solved and Total words found, and their time can set the best time for that grid size.
    • Starting a New puzzle, changing theme/size/mode, or generating a custom puzzle abandons the current puzzle with no score recorded.

    5 · Race vs AI — special rules

    • You and Lexi hunt the same grid and the same word list. First to claim a word owns it; the other player can no longer score it. Your words turn colour, Lexi's turn dark with an orange outline and are crossed off in orange.
    • Lexi claims one random unclaimed word on a fixed timer that starts when your clock starts: every 16 seconds on Easy, 11 seconds on Medium, 7.5 seconds on Hard. She never uses hints and never makes a wrong claim.
    • The race ends the moment every word is claimed. Win: you claimed more words than Lexi. Tie: equal claims — recorded as a played race, not a win. Loss: Lexi claimed more.
    • Race results update Races won / played. Race finishing times are shown for bragging rights but do not set Solo best times, and races do not count as Puzzles solved.

    6 · Hints

    • Each new puzzle includes exactly 3 hints. A hint flashes every letter of one randomly chosen unclaimed word for about 3 seconds.
    • Using a hint starts the clock if it hasn't started, but adds no time penalty and does not affect best times or race results.
    • Hints do not carry over between puzzles, and there is no way to earn extra hints mid-puzzle.
    • Exception: in a practice round (see §9), hints are unlimited.

    7 · Custom puzzles (the generator)

    • Custom words must be 3 letters or longer and no longer than the grid size (10 / 12 / 15 letters). Letters only — spaces and punctuation act as separators. Duplicates are removed.
    • You need at least 3 valid words; up to 20 are used. All other rules (directions, claiming, clock, hints) are the same as a themed puzzle at that size.

    8 · Records

    • Puzzles solved, best time per size, races won/played, and total words found are saved on this device only. “Reset my records” clears all of them; it does not change the puzzle in progress.

    9 · Practice rounds

    • Tap Practice round to start a practice grid at the currently chosen theme and size. A green banner stays visible while practice is on.
    • Practice is untimed: the clock never starts, and the Time pill reads “Practice” instead of a time.
    • Hints are unlimited in practice, and Lexi never races — even if Mode is set to Race vs AI, a practice round is always solo.
    • Nothing from a practice round is recorded: found words do not add to Total words found, finishing does not count as a puzzle solved, and no best time or race result is saved.
    • Finishing a practice grid and tapping Play again starts another practice grid. Tap Exit practice in the banner (or New puzzle) to return to a normal, recorded game.
