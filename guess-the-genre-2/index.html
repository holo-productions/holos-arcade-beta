What to listen for

HouseWarm 4/4 groove, offbeat hats, funky piano-style stabs. ~124 BPM.
TechnoDriving and dark, relentless kick, ticking 16th hats. ~130 BPM.
TranceUplifting arpeggios, rolling bass, big euphoric energy. ~138 BPM.
DubstepHalf-time swagger, snare on 3, wobbling bass. ~140 BPM.
Drum & BassVery fast breakbeat drums, deep rolling bass. ~174 BPM.
ChillSlow, mellow chords, soft drums, laid-back. ~85 BPM.

Tip: count the speed first (fast vs slow), then listen to the bass — wobble = dubstep, rolling = trance, funky = house.

Rules

The game

  • 10 rounds per game. Each round plays one synthesized clip in one of six genres: House, Techno, Trance, Dubstep, Drum & Bass, Chill.
  • One guess per round. Tap a genre button to lock in your answer.
  • Every clip is generated live with WebAudio — no recordings, samples, songs, or artist sounds are used.

Difficulty

  • Easy: longer clips (about 6 seconds), 3 choices per round, genres are easy to tell apart.
  • Normal: medium clips (about 5 seconds), 4 choices per round.
  • Hard: short clips (about 3.5 seconds), all 6 genres as choices every round.
  • Your difficulty choice is saved on this device and restored next visit.

Practice rounds

  • Practice is unscored and endless. It never changes, replaces, or counts toward any best score.
  • Replays are free in Practice, including the Hear it again replay after you've answered.
  • Practice tracks only that session's rounds played, correct answers, best streak, and accuracy — shown in your practice summary when you finish.
  • You can switch between Practice and Scored at any time from the start screen; your difficulty choice applies to both.

Scoring (scored games only)

  • Correct answer: 100 points, plus a streak bonus of 10 × your current streak (streak counted after this answer).
  • Wrong answer: 0 points, streak resets to 0.
  • Replay: the first listen is free. Each replay subtracts 15 points from that round's value (minimum round value 20 points).
  • Best score is saved separately for each difficulty on this device.

Fair play

  • Clips are randomly generated each round, so the same genre won't sound identical twice.
  • Use headphones for the bass — that's where most of the clues live.
