Rules

Word Guess Daily — the full rules, all in one place.

The goal

Guess today's hidden 5-letter word. There is one word per calendar day, and it's the same word for everyone playing that day. A fresh word arrives at midnight, your local time.

How a guess works

  • Type a real 5-letter word and press Enter.
  • Every tile changes colour to show how close you were:

Letters can repeat. If the answer has two of a letter and you guess one, only one tile lights up.

Difficulty

  • Easy — 7 guesses, one letter of the answer is revealed before you start, and you get one extra Hint button press during the game.
  • Normal — the classic game: 6 guesses, no help.
  • Hard — 6 guesses, and every hint you've earned must be used: green letters stay in their spot and yellow letters must appear in your next guess.

Your difficulty choice is saved on this device. If you've already started today's word, the new difficulty applies to your next fresh board — switching mid-word isn't allowed, so nobody can farm extra guesses.

Streaks & stats

  • Win today to grow your streak. Miss a day or run out of guesses and the streak resets to zero.
  • Played, win %, current streak, best streak, and your guess distribution are saved on this device.
  • Practice mode (after you finish) uses random words and never touches your stats.

Fair play

Guesses must be real words from the game's word list. Made-up strings aren't accepted — and a rejected word never uses up a guess.

How to play

1
Guess The Word

Guess the hidden 5-letter word in 6 tries (7 on Easy).

2
Type And Enter

Type or tap the letters on screen, then hit Enter.

3
Green Means Right

Green is the right letter in the right spot — like P in PEACH.

4
Yellow Means Close

Yellow is in the word but the wrong spot; grey letters aren't in it.

5
Solve It

Use the clues to solve it before rows run out — a new word comes tomorrow.

6
Strong Start

Start with common letters and vowels — try CRANE, SLATE or AUDIO.
