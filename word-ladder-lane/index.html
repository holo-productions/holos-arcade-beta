How to play

Change exactly one letter at a time to get from the start word to the target word. Every rung must be a real word. Example: CAT → COT → DOT → DOG.

Type your next word and tap Step, or tap a letter tile to change one position. Use Hint if you get stuck (it costs points). Finish in par or better for 3 stars!

Rules

  • Each step changes exactly one letter — no more, no less.
  • Words stay the same length and must be real words.
  • Score: 100 points per step, bonus for finishing at or under par. Hints cost 30 points.
  • Easy uses 3-letter ladders, Normal 4-letter, Hard 5-letter with longer pars.
  • Practice mode never saves to your best.
