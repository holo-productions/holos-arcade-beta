How to play

1
Flap to Fly

Tap, click, press Space / ↑, or use Flap to rise. Keep tapping — gravity pulls you down.

2
Clear the Gaps

Fly through gaps in trunks and rocks. Each gap scores 1 point.

3
Speeds Up

The game gets faster and gaps tighter as your score grows.

4
Pick Difficulty

Easy: wide gaps. Normal: standard. Hard: narrow and fast.

Rules

  • Score 1 point for every gap you clear.
  • Game over if the fox hits a trunk, a rock, the ground, or flies off the top.
  • Speed rises and gaps tighten a little with every point.
  • Tap, click or press Space after a crash to restart.
  • Practice mode never saves to your best.
  • The game pauses when a window is open or the tab is hidden.
