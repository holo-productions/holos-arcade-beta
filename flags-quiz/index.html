Rules — Flags Quiz

Objective

Identify the country from its flag. Finish with the highest score you can. There is no opponent — you are playing against your own best.

Modes & win conditions

ModeLengthHow it ends
Classic10 questionsAfter Q10. 8+ correct = 🌟 Flag Master finish.
World Tour20 questionsAfter Q20. 16+ correct = 🌟 Flag Master finish.

🎓 PracticeEndlessEnds only when you tap Finish practice. No win/lose — just learning.

Practice is unscored: no points, no streak bonus, no timer, and it never counts toward your best score, best streak, or games played. 50/50 and Hints are free and unlimited in Practice so you can learn the flags properly before playing for a score.

Difficulty — the arcade standard

Every game in the arcade uses the same three settings. Pick one before you start; it applies to the whole game.

SettingWhat you get
EasyWidely recognised, famous flags only — good for warming up or learning.
NormalA mix of famous and tricky flags. The balanced default.
HardLess familiar and easily confused flags only — for a real challenge.

Special rules

  • Each flag appears at most once per game. Wrong options are drawn from the same region where possible, so guesses stay tricky.
  • Easy = widely recognised flags. Normal = a mix of famous and tricky flags. Hard = less familiar / easily confused flags.
  • In Blitz, the timer keeps running while feedback is shown — speed matters.
  • In Practice, flags reshuffle and repeat endlessly, and you can leave at any time with Finish practice — nothing is saved to your bests.
  • Best score, best streak, and games played are saved on this device only (localStorage) — scored modes only, never Practice.
