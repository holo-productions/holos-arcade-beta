How to play

Meet Bloop, your round little climber. Bloop never stops bouncing — your job is just to steer.

1
Steer Bloop

←/→ or A/D, or hold left/right of the sky. Edges wrap around.

2
Land Falling

Only land while falling. You'll bounce back up automatically.

3
Platform Types

Green safe, blue moves, brown breaks, spring = huge bounce!

4
Grab Stars

Each star is worth +50 points.

5
Climb High

Height in metres is your score. Higher gets faster, gaps bigger.

6
Don't Fall

Fall off the bottom and the run ends. Practice has a safety net.

Rules

Goal

Climb as high as you can. There’s no finish line — it’s endless. Score = height climbed (in metres) + 50 points per star.

Scoring & bests

  • Your best height is saved on this device and shown at the top.
  • Practice mode never saves a score — it’s just for learning, with a safety net at the bottom.

Difficulty

  • Easy: wider platforms, smaller gaps, fewer breakable platforms.
  • Normal: the classic climb.
  • Hard: narrower platforms, bigger gaps, faster moving platforms.
  • Your difficulty choice is remembered for next time.

Platforms

  • Normal platforms always bounce you.
  • Moving platforms slide left and right — time your landing.
  • Breakable (brown) platforms crack when touched and don’t bounce you. Don’t rely on them!
  • Some platforms have a spring. Land on the spring itself for a super bounce.

Fair play

One run, one score. Pausing, switching difficulty, or turning Practice on starts a fresh run so scores stay honest.
