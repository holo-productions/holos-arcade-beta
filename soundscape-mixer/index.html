    How to play

    1
    Press Play

    Tap Play to start the sound.

    2
    Blend layers

    Slide each layer's volume up or down to blend your soundscape.

    3
    Silent at 0%

    Layers at 0% are silent.

    4
    Master volume

    Set the overall level with the Master volume slider.

    5
    Pick difficulty

    Gentle: 3 layers. Normal: 5. Challenge: all 6 plus the sleep timer.

    6
    Sleep timer

    On Challenge, the sleep timer fades the sound out gently.

    7
    Save your mix

    Name your blend and tap Save mix to keep it.

    8
    Practice mode

    Practice mode lets you experiment freely — nothing is saved.

    All sounds are created live in your browser — no audio files are used.

    📜 Rules

    • This is a relaxation toy for fun. It is not medical advice, and it does not promise any health or sleep outcome.
    • Sound only starts after you tap Play — nothing plays on its own.
    • Keep the volume at a comfortable level for your ears.
    • Difficulty controls which layers are available: Gentle 3, Normal 5, Challenge all 6 plus the sleep timer.
    • Saved mixes and settings live only in this browser on this device (localStorage). Clearing browser data removes them.
    • In Practice mode, mixes are not saved and nothing is tracked.
    • All ambient sounds are synthesized live with WebAudio. No recorded samples are used.
