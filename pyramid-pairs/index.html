How to play

1
Pair to 13

Pair free cards that add up to 13.

2
Kings go solo

A King is 13 and removes on its own.

3
Free cards only

Only uncovered cards can be played.

4
Use the stock

Tap the stock to flip a waste card to pair.

5
Clear the pyramid

Remove the whole pyramid to win.

Rules

  • Pairs must total exactly 13. A King is worth 13 and removes alone.
  • A pyramid card is free only when both cards covering it are gone.
  • The waste card can pair with any free pyramid card. Waste cards pile up — pairing the top card reveals the one below, and a pass reshuffles the whole pile back into the stock.
  • Each pair scores 25; a King scores 15; clearing the pyramid scores +200.
  • Difficulty changes the pyramid: Easy is 5 rows, Normal 6 rows, Hard 7 rows — fewer rows means fewer cards to clear and a bigger stock. Easy and Normal always open with a pair to play.
  • Practice mode never saves to your best.
