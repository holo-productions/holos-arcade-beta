📜 Rules & scoring

The game

  • Each game is 10 rounds. Every round, a beat loop plays at a hidden BPM chosen at random inside your difficulty's range.
  • All sounds are synthesized live with WebAudio — no songs, recordings, or samples are used.
  • You may play/stop the beat as often as you like before guessing. One guess per round.
  • Practice rounds are separate from scored games: endless rounds, no points, no streaks, no timer, an optional hint each round, and they never change your saved best scores.

Difficulty

  • Easy: 60–120 BPM. Multiple-choice options are spaced 15 BPM apart. Slider: full points within 5 BPM, partial points up to 25 BPM away.
  • Normal: 70–160 BPM. Options spaced 10 BPM apart. Slider: full points within 3 BPM, partial up to 20 BPM away.
  • Hard: 80–180 BPM. Options spaced 5 BPM apart. Slider: full points within 2 BPM, partial up to 15 BPM away.
  • Your difficulty and mode choices are saved on this device and restored next visit.

Scoring

  • Multiple choice: correct answer = 100 points. Wrong answer = 0.
  • Slider: points scale with closeness — 100 points inside the full-points window, then fewer the further away you are, down to 0 at the edge of the partial window. An exact guess earns a +25 exact bonus (125 total).
  • Streak bonus: a round counts toward your streak if you earn at least 50 points. Each streak round adds +10 × current streak bonus points (streak 1 = +10, streak 2 = +20, … capped at +50). A round under 50 points resets the streak to 0.
  • Best scores are saved on this device for every difficulty + mode combination, plus an overall best.
