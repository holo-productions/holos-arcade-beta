📖 Rules - Never Have I Ever Party

🎯 Goal

Read each prompt out loud. Anyone who has done the thing takes a sip. That is the whole game. The stories that follow are the real prize.

🤫 How a turn works

  • Tap NEXT PROMPT and read it out loud, starting with the words on the card.
  • If you have done it, take a sip. If you have not, you are safe this round. No fibbing, the table will know.
  • Share the story if you want to. Nobody has to explain anything they would rather keep to themselves.
  • Tap Skip this one any time a prompt does not fit your crew. Skips are free and unlimited.
  • When the deck runs out, shuffle a new one or end the round and check your session best.

🎒 Categories

Silly, Travel, Food, School and Work, and Arcade Life. Pick one category to theme the whole deck, or play with all of them mixed together.

🥤 Play with water or soda

Every prompt works with water, soda, juice, or any drink you like. Nobody has to drink alcohol to play, and every sip in this game is optional. Swap in a snack or a silly confession instead if that suits the table better.

💧 Drink responsibly

Drink responsibly. Know your limits. Water counts too. Eat food, pace yourself, look out for your friends, and plan a safe ride home. This game is for adults 19 and older in Saskatchewan.
