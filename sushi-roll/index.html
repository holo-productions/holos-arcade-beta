    📜 Rules — Sushi Roll

    You're the sushi chef! Each shift is 5 customer orders. Every order goes through four steps: Fill → Roll → Slice → Serve.

    How an order works

    • Fill: Read the order ticket. Spread rice first (tap the Rice button), then tap fillings to lay strips on the nori until each count on the ticket matches. Wrong or extra fillings cost points. Tap a strip on the nori to remove it.
    • Roll: Press and hold the roll button. The tightness gauge fills up — release inside the green zone for a perfect roll. Too early is loose and floppy; holding to the very end squishes the roll.
    • Slice: The knife slides back and forth over the roll. Tap Slice! to cut where the knife is. Make all the cuts shown — pieces that are close to the same width score best.
    • Serve: Points are added up and the next customer steps up.

    Scoring (per order, max 110)

    Difficulty

    • Easy: 2 fillings per order, 4 cuts, slow knife, wide roll zone, 75 seconds per order.
    • Normal: 3 fillings per order, 5 cuts, medium knife, 60 seconds per order.
    • Hard: 4 fillings per order, 6 cuts, fast knife, narrow roll zone, 45 seconds per order.

    Modes & bests

    • Standard: Timed orders. Your final score can become your saved best for that difficulty.
    • Practice: No timer, no pressure — and practice scores are never saved to your bests.
    • If the timer runs out mid-order, the customer leaves and that order scores 0.
    • Your difficulty choice and best scores are saved on this device for this game.

    How to play Sushi Roll

    1
    Read the ticket

    The ticket shows which fillings your customer wants — and how many of each.

    2
    Fill the nori

    Tap Rice first, then tap fillings to lay strips. Counts turn green when right.

    3
    Fix mistakes

    Tapped too many? Tap the strip on the nori to remove it, or use Undo.

    4
    Roll it up

    Hold the roll button; let go in the green zone. Too soon floppy, too long squished.

    5
    Slice it even

    Tap Slice! as the knife glides. Even-width pieces earn the most points.

    6
    Serve & repeat

    Check your score, then take the next order. Five happy customers ends the shift!

    Tip: New to the bar? Start on Easy or flip on Practice mode — no timer, and nothing is saved to your bests while you learn.
