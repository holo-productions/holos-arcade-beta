SNOWBALL FIGHT

It's Christmas Eve at the North Pole. The elves are on break, the snowman doesn't mind getting pelted (he's used to it), and Santa's sleigh just flew over. Sixty seconds, one snowball at a time — how many Christmas targets can you hit?

Best score: 0  ·  Difficulty: Medium

HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap a target to pelt it with a snowball. Targets pop up around the yard for a short while, then duck back down.
  • You have 60 seconds. Most points wins.
  • Stars and elves are fast and rare — elves are on break, so they don't hang around. The star fell off the tree and is in a hurry to get back.
  • Combos: hit 3 in a row without a miss and your next hit is worth DOUBLE. Every 3rd hit in a row arms another double.
  • Clicking empty snow is a miss — it breaks your combo streak. A target ducking away on its own does not.
  • The snowman doesn't mind, he's used to it. The stockings are full of stuffers. The gifts are… well, gifts.
  • Practice mode: untimed, no score, no best saved — just cookies-and-milk calm.
