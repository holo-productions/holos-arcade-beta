📖 Math Mountain Rules

The goal: answer math questions to climb the mountain. Every right answer moves your climber up one step. Ten steps reach the flag at the top!

  • Pick a level first. Ages 3–5 is counting and tiny sums. Grades 1–3 adds bigger adding, taking away, and times as repeated addition (early multiplication — formal times tables are a Grade 4 outcome). Grades 4–6 uses bigger numbers, sharing (division), and shapes. Grades 7–9 and 10–12 add algebra (solve for x) and geometry.
  • Climb mode earns stars: reaching the top earns 3 stars, and every run of 5 right answers in a row earns 1 bonus star. Stars are kept on this device so they are still here next visit.
  • Practice Round is a short warm-up: 5 questions at your chosen level, hints shown straight away, no stars, no step pressure, and your climber stays at base camp. At the end you get a little round-up, then you can do another Practice Round or switch to Climb when you feel ready.
  • Wrong answers are okay. Your climber never slides down and there is no timer and no losing. Try again, tap Hint, or skip — the mountain waits for you.
  • Big buttons, gentle words. Every answer is a big tap target, and every message is encouragement.
  • Just math. There is no chat, no sign-up, no names or personal information, no ads, and no links away from this page.

Grown-ups: use the Parent Feedback button to rate the game and copy your comments — nothing is sent anywhere unless you paste it somewhere yourself.
