How to play Go

Rules of Go

The goal

Two players take turns placing stones on the intersections of the grid. Black plays first. When the game ends, whoever controls more of the board — empty territory they surround, plus stones they captured — wins. White gets 6.5 bonus points (komi) to make up for moving second, which is why scores end in .5 and never tie.

Liberties & capturing

Every stone or connected group of stones has liberties: the empty intersections directly next to it (up, down, left, right — not diagonal). Fill a group's last liberty and the whole group is captured and lifted off the board. A group with one liberty left is in atari — one move from capture.

Two moves you can't make

  • Suicide: you can't place a stone where its group would have no liberties — unless that move captures enemy stones and frees up space.
  • Ko: you can't make a move that recreates an earlier board position. This stops two players from capturing the same stone back and forth forever. Play somewhere else first, then you can take back.

Passing & ending the game

Don't see a useful move? Pass. When both players pass in a row, the game ends and scoring begins. You can also resign at any time.

Scoring

  • At the end, tap any group that is dead — surrounded with no way to live — to mark it. Dead stones are removed and count as captures for the other side. Tap again to un-mark.
  • Empty points completely surrounded by one colour are that colour's territory.
  • Score = territory + stones you captured. White adds the 6.5 komi. Biggest total wins.

Board sizes

9×9 is the classic learning board (about 10–15 minutes). 13×13 is a middle ground. 19×19 is the full tournament board.

Practice mode

Practice mode turns the AI off so you play both colours, and adds a Hint button, Undo, and orange atari highlights. Great for setting up a capture and watching it work.
