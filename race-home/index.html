    📖 Race Home — the rules

    🎯 The goal

    You and the rival each race 4 pawns around the track and up your home stretch. First racer to get all 4 pawns HOME wins the race.

    🃏 Your turn

    • Tap Draw a card, then pick one of the glowing move options.
    • If a card gives you no legal move, your turn passes — that’s racing!
    • Pawns in Start can only roll out with a 1, a 2, or a Turbo Start card.

    💥 Bumping

    • Land exactly on a rival pawn and it gets bumped all the way back to its Start.
    • You can’t land on your own pawn — pick a different move (except on ⭐ safe squares, where your pawns may share).

    ⭐ Safe squares

    Squares marked with a ⭐ (the corners and both Start squares) are safe: pawns sitting there can’t be bumped, and both racers’ pawns can share them.

    🛝 Slides

    Land on the head of a blue SLIDE square and you whoosh forward to its end. Any rival pawns caught on the slide get bumped back to Start. (Your own pawns on the slide just get out of the way — they stay put.)

    🂡 The cards

    • 1 · GO! — Roll out of Start, or step ahead 1.
    • 2 · QUICK START — Roll out of Start, or scoot ahead 2.
    • 3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 12 — Move ahead that many squares.
    • 4 · BACKTRACK — Oops! Move one pawn back 4.
    • 7 · SPLIT SEVEN — Move one pawn 7, or split the 7 between two pawns (like 3 + 4).
    • 10 · TEN / TAKE-BACK — Move ahead 10, or back just 1.
    • 11 · TRADE PLACES — Move ahead 11, or swap spots with a rival pawn on the track.
    • TURBO START — Blast a pawn out of Start straight to step 4, or boost any pawn ahead 6.

    🏠 The home stretch

    • After a full lap, your pawns turn into your coloured home stretch (teal for you, orange for the rival).
    • You need the exact count to land on HOME — overshooting doesn’t count, so endgame cards matter!
    • Pawns in the home stretch can’t be bumped or swapped. They can still slide back out with a Backtrack or Take-Back card, so careful!

    🧪 Practice mode & difficulty

    Practice turns on the Hint button, makes the rival play Easy, and keeps the race off your win/loss record. Difficulty (Easy / Medium / Hard) changes how cleverly the rival picks its moves — your choice is remembered for next time.
