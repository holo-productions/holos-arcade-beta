How to play

🐱

The (very short) rulebook

🐱 Play as Gizmo (the cat)

  • You're Gizmo, the calico cat. Steer with arrow keys / WASD, the on-screen buttons, or swipe on the board.
  • Catch the mouse by running into it. It respawns somewhere else in the house — cheeky thing.
  • 😌 Gentle: no clock, no losing. ⏱ Timed Chase: 60 seconds, catch it as many times as you can — it gets faster every catch!
  • Cheese = 25 points (the mouse snacks too!). Purple pom-poms = 5-second speed boost + 10 points. A catch = 100 points.

🐭 Play as the Mouse

  • You're the mouse, and the house is full of cats. Push the wooden crates around to block them — and to trap them!
  • A cat boxed in on all four sides is trapped… and turns into cheese (150 points). Science.
  • Grab every cheese or trap every cat, then scamper out the glowing door to finish the level.
  • If a cat catches you, you lose a life (you have 3). Run out and it's game over — but you can always try again.
  • Each level adds more cats. How far can you get?

🎚 Difficulty

  • Cozy: sleepier cats. Classic: the standard chase. Wild: cats on espresso.

🎓 Practice mode

  • Practice is just for fun — scores there never touch your bests, and the mouse gets unlimited lives.
