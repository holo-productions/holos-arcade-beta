📖 The Pirate's Code (Rules)

🎯 The Goal

Be the pirate with the most gold when the tile deck runs out. Simple as that. Richest pirate wins the day!

🔁 Your Turn — 2 Steps

  • 1. Place a tile. You draw one tile each turn. Tap 🔄 Rotate to spin it, then tap a glowing square to place it. A tile must sit next to tiles already on the board and at least one of its paths must join up with a path on a neighbouring tile. Don't like it? Tap 🗑️ Toss Tile to throw it back into the sea.
  • 2. Move your pirate. After placing, tap any glowing square your pirate can reach by following joined-up paths. You can move up to 3 squares per turn (4 for Captain AI on Hard — he's sneaky). Or tap ✔️ Done Moving to stay put.

💰 Treasure & Digging

Some tiles hide a big ✕ — that's buried treasure! Land on one and you dig it up automatically, pocketing 15–30 gold. Each treasure can only be dug once, so race Captain AI to the good spots.

⚡ Event Tiles — Watch Out!

  • 🌪️ Storm: A storm blows in! You lose 10 gold overboard and get blown all the way back to your home port.
  • 🏴 Rival Pirate: A rival jumps out! It's a coin-flip duel — win and you pinch 20 gold off Captain AI, lose and he pinches 10 off you.
  • 🗺️ Buried Map: Jackpot! The map leads straight to a hidden cache worth 20 gold.

🏁 Game Over

When the deck is empty, the game ends after that round. Most gold wins. Tied? Then it's an honourable pirate draw.

🎚️ Difficulty

  • Easy: Captain AI wanders a bit and only moves 2 squares a turn. Good for learning the ropes.
  • Normal: Captain AI hunts treasure properly and moves 3 squares.
  • Hard: Captain AI plans his tile placements, beelines for treasure, and moves 4 squares. Bring your best game!

🎓 Practice Mode

Turn Practice on for helpful hints — the best spot for your tile glows green and you'll get tips as you play. Practice games don't count toward your wins and losses, so experiment freely!

How to Play

✦

Step 1 of 5 — Place a tile

Every turn you draw one island tile. Spin it with the Rotate button, then tap a glowing square on the board to place it. Paths have to join up — that's how the island grows!

✦

Step 2 of 5 — Move your pirate

After placing, your pirate (the red token) can sail-walk up to 3 squares along joined-up paths. Tap a glowing square to move there, or tap Done Moving to stay where you are.

✦

Step 3 of 5 — Dig for treasure!

See a tile with a big ✕? That's buried treasure worth 15–30 gold. Land your pirate right on it and you'll dig it up automatically. Get there before Captain AI does!

✦

Step 4 of 5 — Mind the event tiles

Some tiles spring surprises: Storms blow you back to port and cost gold, Rivals duel you for gold, and Buried Maps hand you a bonus cache. Risky… but sometimes worth it.

✦

Step 5 of 5 — Most gold wins!

When the tiles run out, the game ends. Whoever dug up the most gold wins. Now go get plundering, Captain!
