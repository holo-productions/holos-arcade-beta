📜 Blob Arena Rules

Everything you need to know — no surprises mid-game.

🎯 The goal

  • Grow your blob as big as you can and reach #1 on the leaderboard.
  • There is no timer — a run ends only when a bigger blob eats you.
  • Your best size, most blobs eaten, and longest run are saved on this device.
  • Practice round: nothing is saved and you can't lose — get eaten and you pop back in at the edge. Hints pop up while you play, so it's the best way to learn.

🍬 Eating & growing

  • Small pellets are worth 1 size each. Golden pellets are worth 5.
  • You can eat a blob only if you are at least 10% bigger than it — its name tag turns into a warning when it can eat you.
  • Eating a blob adds most of its size to yours.
  • The bigger you get, the slower you glide — that's the trade-off.

🚀 Boosting

  • Hold boost for a burst of speed.
  • Boosting slowly sheds size, and it stops working when you're near your starting size.
  • Use it to escape a bigger blob or to run down a smaller one.

🤖 The other blobs

  • Every rival is an AI bot (live multiplayer is planned for later).
  • Bots hunt pellets, chase smaller blobs, and flee bigger ones — just like you should.
  • Eaten bots respawn at the arena edge after a few seconds, a little smaller.
  • Easy: bots are slower and less aggressive. Normal: a fair fight. Hard: bots are faster, start bigger, and chase harder.

🧱 The arena

  • The arena is bigger than your screen — the camera follows you and zooms out as you grow.
  • The glowing wall is the boundary. Nobody can leave, and nobody can hide outside it.

🎮 Controls

  • Mouse: your blob follows your cursor.
  • Touch: drag anywhere — a joystick appears under your finger.
  • Keyboard: W A S D / arrow keys to move, Space to boost, P to pause.

How to Play Blob Arena

Thirty seconds of know-how, then you're dangerous.

✦

1. Steer your blob
Point with your mouse, drag with a finger, or use W A S D / arrow keys. Your blob glides toward where you point.

✦

2. Snack to grow
Roll over pellets to eat them. Small ones add 1 size, golden ones add 5. Bigger blob = more eating power, but slower feet… er, blob.

✦

3. Eat smaller blobs
If you're at least 10% bigger than a rival, roll over it to absorb it. Watch the leaderboard on the right to see who's worth chasing.

✦

4. Run from bigger blobs
Anything notably bigger than you can eat you. Zigzag, hug the wall, and boost away when one locks onto you.

✦

5. Boost wisely
Hold Space or the BOOST button for speed. It burns size as fuel, so spend it on escapes and ambushes — not joyrides.

✦

New here? Try a Practice Round
From the menu, pick Practice Round: no score is kept, you can't lose, and hints appear while you play. Perfect for a first run.
