How to play

Simon plays a growing pattern of lights and tones. Your job is to repeat it back, one pad longer every round.

Pick a difficulty

  • Easy: slower playback with longer pauses between pads — the gentlest way to learn.
  • Normal: the classic Simon speed.
  • Hard: faster playback from the very first pad, and it tightens quicker.
  • Difficulty and mode can be changed between games, never mid-game.

Practice round

  • Tap Practice round (or pick Practice under Mode, then Start) for a warm-up with no pressure.
  • No score is saved, you have unlimited tries, and a wrong pad never ends the game — Simon just replays the same pattern so you can try again.
  • The pattern still grows by one pad each round and your chosen difficulty still sets the speed, so it's a true rehearsal.
  • Tap End practice whenever you're done, then start a real game when you're ready.

Quick tips

  • Say the colours out loud as Simon plays them — it genuinely helps.
  • Chunk long patterns into groups of three or four.
  • New to the game? Start with a Practice round on Easy, or play Relaxed mode on Easy: a wrong pad costs a life and Simon replays the pattern, instead of ending the game.

Rules

Objective

Repeat Simon's pattern of lights and tones in the exact order it was played. The pattern grows by one pad every round. There is no final round — the game is endless, and the goal is the longest pattern and the highest score you can reach.

How a round works

  • Simon plays the full pattern from the beginning, then adds one new random pad to the end.
  • Pads are locked while Simon is playing. They unlock when the pattern finishes — wait for your turn.
  • You must then press the pads in the same order, from the first pad to the last.
  • Completing the pattern starts the next round. Making a mistake is handled by the mode rules below.

Scoring

  • 10 points for every pad you press correctly.
  • 50-point bonus each time you complete a full round.
  • Your score and best score are shown on the scoreboard. Best score and longest pattern are saved on this device and kept between visits, and your longest pattern for the run is reported when a game ends.

Modes & special rules

  • Strict mode (classic): one wrong pad ends the game immediately.
  • Relaxed mode: you have 3 lives. A wrong pad costs one life, Simon replays the same pattern, and you try it again from the start of the pattern. Losing all 3 lives ends the game. Points already earned are kept.
  • Practice round: a warm-up with no game over. Wrong pads cost nothing — Simon replays the same pattern and you try again, as many times as you like. Lives show as ∞ and nothing counts: practice points are shown while you play but are never saved and never change your best score or longest pattern. The pattern still grows by one pad per round and difficulty still controls playback speed. End practice at any time with the End practice button.
  • Switching modes is only possible between games, never mid-game — except starting a Practice round, which (like Restart) starts a fresh run.
  • Keyboard play (keys 1–4) follows exactly the same rules as tapping the pads, including in Practice.

Difficulty

  • Difficulty controls playback speed only — how long each pad lights up and how long Simon pauses between pads. It works with either mode, and scoring is the same on every difficulty.
  • Easy: pads light for longer with generous pauses, and the speed-up per round is gentle. Best for learning the game or warming up.
  • Normal (default): the classic Simon speed. Playback speeds up noticeably at rounds 6, 11, and 16, and keeps tightening after that.
  • Hard: playback starts fast and tightens quicker and further than Normal — short flashes and short pauses, even in the early rounds.
  • On every difficulty the pattern still plays faster as rounds go up; difficulty sets where that speed starts and how quickly it tightens.
  • Switching difficulty is only possible between games, never mid-game. Your best score is shared across all difficulties.

Winning & game end

  • Simon has no final win — you "win" a game by beating your own best score or longest pattern.
  • A game ends when you press a wrong pad in Strict mode, or lose your last life in Relaxed mode. A Practice round never ends on a wrong pad — it ends only when you tap End practice.
  • Reaching round 20 or beyond earns the on-screen title of Simon Master for that run.
