How to Play

1
Pick Mode

Pick a difficulty and mode up top.

2
Pick Tools

Choose a colour, a tool and a brush size.

3
Draw

Draw with your finger or mouse.

4
Fill & Stamp

Fill floods an area; Stamp drops a shape where you tap.

5
Undo

Undo steps back one stroke.

6
Save & Download

Save to your gallery, or download a PNG file.

Rules

Paint Studio is a free-play art game. There is no score and no losing — just making things.

Your paintings, difficulty and mode are saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded or shared.

Practice mode never saves to the gallery. Create mode saves when you tap Save to gallery.
