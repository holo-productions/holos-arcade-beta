How to Play

1. Pick a difficulty and mode up top.

2. Choose a colour, a tool and a brush size.

3. Draw with your finger or mouse. Fill bucket floods an area. Stamp drops a shape where you tap.

4. Undo steps back one stroke. Save keeps your painting in the gallery on this device; Download gives you a PNG file.

Rules

Paint Studio is a free-play art game. There is no score and no losing — just making things.

Your paintings, difficulty and mode are saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded or shared.

Practice mode never saves to the gallery. Create mode saves when you tap Save to gallery.
