▶ How to Play

1. Pick your level — Ages 3–5, Grades 1–3, 4–6, 7–9, or 10–12.

2. Pick a topic like Animals, Space, or Chemistry — or tap Mix It All!

3. Read the question and tap an answer. Big buttons, no timer — take your time.

4. Every answer reveals a Did You Know? fact. You learn something either way!

5. Earn a ★ for every correct answer, plus a bonus ★★ for a perfect round. Stars are saved on this device.

📖 Rules

  • There is no timer and no losing. Wrong answers still teach you a fact.
  • One tap per question — once you answer, read the fact, then tap Next.
  • ★ 1 star for each correct answer. ★★ 2 bonus stars for getting every question right in a round.
  • Younger levels have 3 choices; older levels have 4 choices and harder facts.
  • Levels: Ages 3–5 is gentlest. Grades 7–9 adds physics, chemistry & biology basics. Grades 10–12 goes deeper.
  • Be kind to yourself — scientists get things wrong all the time. That's how discoveries happen!
  • No chat, no accounts, no personal information, no ads, and no links to other websites.
