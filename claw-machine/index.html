📖 Rules — Claw Machine

🎯 Goal

Use your play tokens to grab as many plushies as you can. Every plush you win earns tickets and (in a real round) a spot on your Collection Shelf. Collect all 12!

🕹️ How a turn works

  • Slide the claw left/right with ◀ ▶ (hold to move) or ← → keys.
  • Pick a row with ▲ Back / ▼ Front (or ↑ ↓ keys). The claw only grabs plushies in its row — the dashed outline shows your best target.
  • Press DROP THE CLAW (or Space). That costs 1 token. The claw drops, closes, and lifts.
  • If it grabs well, it carries the plush to the prize chute. If it's off-centre or the plush is heavy/rare, it may slip on the way up!
  • The round ends when your tokens run out. The pile restocks for the next round — your shelf and bests stay saved.

🎓 Practice mode

Unlimited tokens, stronger grip, no pressure. Practice wins do not save to your best scores or your Collection Shelf — it's purely for learning the claw. End any time with End round.

💰 Play money only

Tokens and tickets in this game are pretend. There's no real money, no prizes to buy, and nothing to win outside the game — just plushies on a shelf and bragging rights. 🎉

💾 Saving

Difficulty, best tickets, and your collection are saved in this browser on this device only (localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere. Use “Reset shelf & bests” on the shelf to start fresh.
