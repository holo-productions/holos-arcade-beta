Rules

  • Secret number must be a whole number from 1 to 100.
  • Guesser gets 10 guesses per half.
  • Hints: Higher means secret is higher than your guess. Lower means secret is lower. Correct ends the half.
  • If the guesser does not find it in 10 guesses, that half counts as 11 guesses.
  • After both halves, fewer guesses wins the round. Equal guesses is a draw.
  • Secret entry is masked and cleared before the phone is passed.
  • vs Computer: Half 1 the computer sets and you guess. Half 2 you set and the computer guesses with binary search (it halves the range each time).
  • Restart Round starts a fresh round. Reset Scores clears saved wins.
