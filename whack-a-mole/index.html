How to play

1
Pick a round

Choose Timed or Practice and a difficulty, then press Start game.

2
Practice freely

Practice has no clock and never counts toward your best — end it when you're done.

3
Whack the moles

Tap a mole — or press its number key, 1–9 — before it ducks back down.

4
Build a combo

Consecutive bonks raise your multiplier up to ×3; a miss or a bomb resets it.

5
Mind the specials

Golden moles are worth 50 and clock moles add +3 seconds — never whack a bomb.

6
Beat the target

Beat your difficulty's target before time runs out to win the round.

Rules

Objective

Score as many points as you can in 60 seconds by whacking moles as they pop out of the nine holes. The moles get faster the longer the round runs. Reach the target score for your difficulty before the clock runs out to win the round.

Practice round

Pick Practice above the board for a no-pressure warm-up. There is no clock and no target — moles stay up about a third longer and appear at a gentler pace, and the round only ends when you tap End practice. Scoring, combos and bomb penalties work exactly as in a timed round so you can learn them, clock moles score points only (there is no time to add), and your practice score is never saved as a best score and can't win or lose the round. Your summary at the end is just for you.

Scoring

TargetPointsNotes
Mole10 × multiplierThe standard garden pest.
Golden mole50 × multiplierRare, and ducks away about 40% faster than a normal mole.
Clock mole5 × multiplier, +3 secAdds 3 seconds to the clock. Time can never exceed 60 seconds.
Bomb−25Not a mole. Whacking one costs 25 points (never below 0) and resets your combo.
Empty hole / miss0No points lost, but your combo resets to zero.

Combo multiplier

  • Every successful bonk adds 1 to your combo.
  • Combo 0–4: ×1 · Combo 5–11: ×2 · Combo 12+: ×3.
  • The multiplier applies to mole, golden mole and clock mole points — never to the bomb penalty.
  • Your combo resets to zero when you whack an empty hole, whack a bomb, or tap the grass.

Special rules

  • A mole that ducks away on its own costs nothing — no penalty, and your combo survives.
  • Only one whack counts per pop-up: a bonked mole is gone, and whacking its hole again counts as a miss.
  • Speed ramps up during the round: moles stay up for less time and appear more often in the final seconds.
  • Pausing freezes the clock and every mole exactly where it is.

Difficulty

Pick Easy, Normal or Hard above the board before you start. Difficulty sets how long moles stay up, how fast new ones appear, how many can be up at once, and the target score. It locks while a round is running — change it between rounds. Your best score is saved separately for each difficulty.

Winning & ranks

The round ends when the clock reaches zero. You win if your score meets the target for your difficulty; either way your score stands, and your best score for each difficulty is saved on this device.
