How to Play

1. Pick how many slices your mandala has (difficulty).

2. Draw anywhere in the circle. Every stroke is copied into every slice, mirrored — so one squiggle becomes a whole pattern.

3. Try Dots for dotted rings, or draw slowly near the centre for tight patterns and near the edge for big petals.

4. Save keeps your mandala in the gallery; Download gives a PNG.

Rules

Mandala Maker is free-play: no score, no losing. For relaxation and fun.
