Rules

Basketball Shootout is a timed shooting contest. Race the clock, rack up points from five spots around the arc, and ride your hot streaks.

How to Play

  • Press Start Game. The clock starts right away.
  • A marker sweeps back and forth on the Shot Timing bar. A green zone marks the sweet spot for each shot.
  • Tap SHOOT (or press Space) to release the ball. Stop the marker inside the green to score — dead centre is a swish.
  • You shoot 5 balls from each spot, then move to the next spot: Left Corner → Left Wing → Top of the Arc → Right Wing → Right Corner, then back around until time runs out.

Scoring

  • Made shot: 2 points.
  • Money Ball (the gold 5th ball at each spot): 4 points.
  • On Fire: make 3 in a row and every make while your streak stays alive earns +1 bonus point. One miss ends the streak.
  • A swish (dead centre) doesn't add points — it just feels great and keeps the streak rolling.

Practice Round

Want to find your timing without pressure? Pick Practice mode (or tap Practice Round on the start screen).

  • Untimed — no clock, no buzzer. Shoot as long as you like and tap End Practice when you're done.
  • No score saved — practice points and streaks are just for learning; your Contest bests are never touched.
  • Hints on — a live coach line under the timing bar tells you when to wait, when you're close, and when to shoot (“NOW!”). After each miss it tells you if you were early or late and by how much.
  • Slower marker — in Practice the marker runs a touch slower and doesn't speed up, so you can lock in the rhythm before a Contest run.
  • Difficulty still applies in Practice — try Hard in Practice first to learn the drifting hoop risk-free.

Tips

  • The marker speeds up slightly with every shot in Contest — settle your timing early. (It stays steady in Practice.)
  • The sweet spot shifts a little from shot to shot. Watch the green, not the middle.
  • Protect a hot streak: a safe shot in the green beats a greedy swish attempt.
  • New to the game? Do one Practice round first, then switch to Contest.
