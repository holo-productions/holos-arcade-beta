How to play

Switch lanes

Tap Left or Right, swipe, or use the arrow keys.

2
Jump the junk-food zombies

Tap Jump or press Space to leap clean over one.

3
Grab the good snacks

Run through water, apples and carrots for points.

4
Survive the speed-up

It gets faster the longer you run.

5
Don't get tagged

A zombie tag ends the run — Practice keeps you going.

Rules

  • Goal: run as far as you can and collect as many healthy snacks as possible.
  • Scoring: +1 point for every moment you stay ahead of the horde, +25 points per healthy snack collected.
  • Getting caught: running into a junk-food zombie (same lane, feet on the ground) ends the run.
  • Jumping: while you're in the air, zombies pass right under you.
  • Difficulty: Easy, Normal and Hard change zombie speed and how often they appear. Your pick is saved.
  • Practice mode: zombies can't catch you and the score doesn't count toward your best — it's just for learning the ropes.
  • Best score: your best non-practice run is saved on this device.
