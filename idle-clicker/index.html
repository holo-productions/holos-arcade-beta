Idle Clicker — Rules

The Cozy Crumb Bakery is an idle game: you build it up by tapping and buying, then it keeps working on its own — even while the page is closed.

How to play

  • Tap the big muffin to earn Crumbs, the bakery's currency.
  • Spend crumbs in the Bakery Shop on Helpers & Gear. Each one bakes crumbs per second automatically.
  • Buy Upgrades to double your tap power, double a helper's output, or boost bonuses.
  • Use the ×1 / ×10 / ×100 buttons to buy helpers in bulk. Prices rise 15% with each one you own.

Practice Round

  • Tap Practice Round in the top bar (or the card under the muffin) any time you want a quick go with no pressure.
  • You get 60 seconds to tap the practice muffin as many times as you can. Each tap uses your current tap power, so upgrades still help.
  • Practice is separate: practice crumbs don't add to your real bakery, and they don't change your crumbs, helpers, upgrades or achievements.
  • Your helpers keep baking in the background while you practise. At the end you'll see your taps, practice crumbs and taps per second — and your best practice round is kept as a record.
  • Play it as often as you like. It's the best way to warm up or test a new tapping technique before playing for real.

Golden Crumbs

  • A Golden Crumb appears near the muffin every minute or two and only stays for 12 seconds.
  • Tap it before it vanishes to instantly earn 3 minutes' worth of your per-second baking (minimum 25 crumbs).

Offline earnings

  • When you come back, your helpers pay you for the time you were away — at a reduced rate, up to a cap.
  • Easy: 75% rate, up to 12 hours away. Normal: 50%, up to 8 hours. Hard: 30%, up to 4 hours.
  • The Early Riser upgrade adds +25% to your offline rate.

Difficulty

  • Easy — shop prices are 30% lower and everything bakes 50% faster. A relaxed bakery.
  • Normal — the standard prices and pace.
  • Hard — prices are 40% higher and baking runs 35% slower. For patient bakers.
  • You can switch difficulty any time from the top bar; your choice is saved and your crumbs and helpers are never lost by switching.

Saving, records & reset

  • Your game auto-saves to this browser (localStorage) every few seconds and when you leave the page.
  • Your best per-second rate and biggest crumb pile are kept as records, even if you reset.
  • Reset bakery wipes crumbs, helpers, upgrades and achievements and starts fresh. Records stay.
