Rules

Objective

Be the first racer to reach the checkered finish line. You race as the duck 🦆 against three AI racers: Splash 🐸, Bubbles 🐙 and Jet 🐢.

How racing works

  • Your racer moves only while you hold SPRAY (button, track press-and-hold with the button, or Space key).
  • Your stream's height is set by dragging up/down on the track or with the ↑ / ↓ arrow keys.
  • The bullseye in your lane bobs up and down. Stream on the bullseye = full speed. Stream near it = half speed. Stream far from it = quarter speed (a trickle).
  • Spraying drains your water tank. At 0% you can't spray until it refills. Releasing the button refills the tank.
  • AI racers move at their own pace with random surges and slowdowns. They don't use tanks.

Levels

There are 8 levels. Each level the track gets longer, the AI gets faster, and the bullseye bobs quicker. Finish 1st to unlock the next level. You can replay any unlocked level from the level selector after a race — your highest unlocked level is saved on this device.

Tickets (play-money only)

  • 1st place: 100 tickets · 2nd: 60 · 3rd: 30 · 4th: 10 — multiplied by the level number.
  • Tickets are play-money only. They have no cash value, can't be bought, sold, or exchanged for real prizes.
  • Your total ticket bank is saved on this device.

Difficulty

Easy: slower AI, bigger bullseye, slower tank drain. Normal: the standard race. Hard: faster AI, smaller bullseye, faster tank drain, and a +50% ticket bonus. Difficulty locks during a race and is saved on this device between visits. Best finish times are saved separately for each difficulty and level.

Practice mode

Practice is a no-pressure warm-up: the AI racers cruise slowly, there are no tickets, no level unlocks, and your finish time is never saved as a best. Tap End practice any time to stop early.

Winning & results

The race ends when you cross the finish line (AI finishing times are shown for places already decided). Your place, time, and tickets earned are shown on the results card. If an AI racer finishes before you, you can keep racing for the remaining places.
