How to play

Train your ears, one pair of notes at a time. Pick a mode first:

  1. Higher / Lower mode: Tap ▶ Play notes. You'll hear Note 1, then Note 2. Decide: is Note 2 Higher, Lower, or the Same?
  2. Intervals mode: Hear two notes and name the exact interval between them — Minor 2nd, Major 3rd, Perfect 5th, Octave, and more. Tap the correct interval name from the choices.
  3. Tap your answer. Correct answers build your streak and score bonus points.
  4. Need another listen? Tap ↻ Replay any time — it costs nothing.
  5. On Hard in Higher / Lower mode, you'll also get a bonus jump-size question.
  6. Just warming up? Switch Round type → Practice at the top. Practice is endless — no points, no best saved, no game over. You just see how many you got right, then tap End practice whenever you're ready for a scored game.

Tip: hum the first note in your head while the second one plays. Your voice knows the answer before your brain does!

Rules

Goal: Listen to two synthesized notes. 10 rounds per scored game.

🎧 Practice rounds: switch Round type to Practice for endless, unscored rounds in either mode and any difficulty. Practice tracks only how many you tried and got right (plus your accuracy) — it never adds points, never changes your streak bonus in scored games, and never overwrites your best. Tap “End practice & see summary” to finish.
