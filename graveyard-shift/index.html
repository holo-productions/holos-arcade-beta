How to Play

✦

1. Watch the graves

Ghosts pop up out of the graves in the graveyard. Keep your eyes peeled — they don't stay up for long!

📖 Rules

The goal

Bonk as many ghosts as you can before your shift ends. Friendly-spooky only — the ghosts love it, promise.

Scoring

  • Ghost 👻 — +10 points
  • Gold Ghost 🌟 — +30 points (rare & speedy!)
  • Pumpkin 🎃 — Don't bonk it! −15 points and your streak resets. Pumpkins are decorations, not ghosts.
  • Streak bonus — Every 5 bonks in a row without a miss earns +10 bonus points.
  • Tapping an empty grave doesn't cost points — it just resets your streak.

Modes

  • Timed Game — 45 seconds. Your score counts toward your saved Best.
  • Practice — No clock, no pressure, slower ghosts, and pumpkin bonks don't take points away. Practice scores don't replace your Best.

Difficulty

  • Easy 🌙 — Ghosts pop up slowly and stay up longer. Great for little ghouls.
  • Normal 👻 — A steady haunting pace.
  • Hard ⚡ — Fast ghosts, short pop-ups, more at once. Good luck, keeper!

Your difficulty choice and best score are saved on this device, so they're ready for your next shift.
