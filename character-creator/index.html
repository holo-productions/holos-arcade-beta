📖 The (tiny) rules

Honestly? There are barely any. Here they are anyway:

  • Free bits are free. All the basics — bodies, faces, hair, outfits, backgrounds — are free, no catch.
  • ★ PASS bits are the extra-fancy ones. They'll unlock with the one Arcade Pass later. While we're testing, you can preview them.
  • Your saves live here. Characters and your uploaded art are stored only in this browser on this device. Clear your browser data and they're gone — so download a Card for anyone you really love.
  • Your art is yours. Uploads never leave your device. Only pop in pictures you're happy to use.
  • Be kind if you share. Cards you download are yours to share — just don't claim other people's uploaded art as your own.
  • No scores, no timers. This is a chill one. Take as long as you like.
