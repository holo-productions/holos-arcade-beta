Picking a difficulty

Easy is light, slow traffic — learn the corners. Medium is a fair race. Hard is dense, fast traffic that leaves little room to pass.

Rules

The race

  • A top-down race around a looping track. 3 laps per race.
  • Your car is the yellow one, starting on the bottom straight. Drive clockwise around the loop.
  • The clock runs from the green light until you cross the line after lap 3.

Driving

  • Up / W accelerates, Left / Right or A / D steers, Down / S brakes (and reverses slowly).
  • Space boosts — much faster, but it drains the boost meter.
  • Going off the track onto the grass slows you right down. Stay on the asphalt.
  • On-screen buttons and dragging on the track work on touch screens. A controller works too: stick / D-pad steers, RB / RT boosts, Start pauses.

Traffic & coins

  • Traffic cars drive the racing line at their own pace. Hitting one bounces you back and costs speed — you get a short moment of protection after a crash.
  • Coins sit on the track. Each one refills +30 boost and adds points. New coins appear as you collect them.

Score

  • Score = laps × 1000 + coins × 50 − race seconds × 5 (never below 0).
  • Your best lap, best 3-lap time and best score are saved on this device.

Difficulty

  • Easy: 3 traffic cars, slow.
  • Medium: 5 traffic cars, steady.
  • Hard: 8 traffic cars, fast and dense.
  • You can change difficulty between races — it takes effect on the next race. Your choice is remembered for next time.

Practice round

  • Tap Practice round to switch to a practice session at any time.
  • No timer, no lap limit, no score is kept — laps just keep counting so you can learn the track.
  • Practice never saves anything. Tap the button again to return to a 3-lap race.
