📖 Rules

Wind Down is a slow sorting puzzle. There is no way to lose, and there is no timer.

  • Goal: Sort every shape into its matching bin.
  • Matching: Early rounds match by colour. Later rounds match by colour and shape — the bin shows exactly what belongs in it.
  • How to move: Tap a shape to pick it up, then tap a bin. You can also drag a shape onto a bin.
  • Wrong bin? Nothing bad happens. The shape stays put and you can try another bin.
  • Rounds: Each finished round gently adds a little more to sort. Rounds keep going — stop whenever you like.
  • Difficulty: Gentle uses fewer shapes and colours. Normal adds more. Challenge adds similar shades and more shapes to keep track of. Your choice is remembered on this device.
  • Practice mode: No score pressure, hints stay on, and there is no timer. A calm way to learn.
  • Hints: The Hint button softly glows the matching bin for the selected shape (or the next shape).
  • Evening dimming: While you play, the screen slowly warms and dims a little. You can adjust the warmth, turn it off, or tap Brighten to reset it.
  • Bests: Your best rounds and most shapes sorted are saved on this device only.

Welcome to Wind Down — a slow, gentle sorting puzzle.

2

4

Wrong bin? No problem — just try another.

5

Finish a round to drift into the next, slightly fuller one.

6

Tap Hint and the right bin will glow softly.
