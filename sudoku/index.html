How to play — Sudoku

How to play — under a minute

  1. 1
    The goalFill every row, column and 3×3 box with the numbers 1–9 — each number exactly once in each. The dark numbers are givens and can't be changed.
  2. 2
    Tap a cell, tap a numberSelect any empty cell, then use the number pad (or your keyboard, 1–9). Cells in the same row, column and box light up, and matching numbers glow so you can spot what's missing.
  3. 3
    Use Notes for maybesTurn Notes on to pencil small candidate numbers into a cell. Placing a number clears it from the notes around it automatically.
  4. 4
    Mistakes & hintsA wrong number counts as a mistake — three mistakes ends the game. Stuck? You get 3 hints per game; a hint fills the selected cell (or a random empty one) with the right number.
  5. 5
    Pick your difficultyEasy starts with 40 numbers given, Medium 32, Hard 28, Expert about 24. Every puzzle is generated fresh with exactly one solution. Your best time for each difficulty is saved below the board.
  6. 6
    Warm up with a Practice roundTap Practice round up top for a no-pressure go: no mistake limit, unlimited hints, and the timer is just for you — nothing is saved to your records. Perfect for learning or a chill solve. Switch back to Real game whenever you're ready for it to count.

Rules — Sudoku

The full rules, scoring and win conditions

Objective

Complete the 9×9 grid so that every row, every column and every 3×3 box contains the numbers 1–9 exactly once. No maths is involved — it's pure logic; numbers are just symbols.

The grid

  • Givens are the dark numbers printed at the start. They are fixed and can never be changed or erased.
  • Every puzzle in this game is generated fresh and has exactly one solution.
  • A number may never repeat inside its row, column or 3×3 box.

Placing numbers

  • Tap an empty cell, then tap a number on the pad (or press 1–9 on a keyboard; arrow keys move around the grid).
  • Notes mode ✏️ pencils small candidate numbers into a cell instead of committing. Notes are just reminders — they never count as mistakes, and placing a number automatically removes it from the notes of every cell in its row, column and box.
  • Erase ⌫ clears a cell you filled in. Undo ↩️ steps back one move at a time.

Mistakes & losing

  • Placing a number that isn't the cell's solution counts as a mistake and the number is shown in red.
  • Three mistakes ends the game — the puzzle is lost and a new one starts fresh. Mistakes are never carried between puzzles.

Hints

  • You get 3 hints per puzzle. A hint fills the selected cell with its correct number (shown in gold), or a random empty cell if nothing suitable is selected.
  • Hints don't count as mistakes, but they're limited — when they're gone, they're gone for that puzzle.

Difficulty

  • Easy — 40 givens · Medium — 32 givens · Hard — 28 givens · Expert — about 24 givens. Fewer givens means more logic is needed, not different rules.
  • Switching difficulty starts a new puzzle right away.

Practice rounds 🧘

  • Tap Practice round at the top to play the same puzzles with the pressure off: no mistake limit (wrong numbers still show in red so you can learn from them, but the game never ends), unlimited hints, and no pause on fun.
  • Practice rounds don't count — they aren't added to Played/Solved and can't set a best time. The timer still runs so you can see how long you took, it just isn't recorded.
  • Switching between Real game and Practice round starts a fresh puzzle, same as switching difficulty.

Winning & scoring

  • You win the moment every cell holds its correct number.
  • There are no points — you're racing the clock. Your best (fastest) solve time is kept separately for each difficulty, along with puzzles played and solved, in the records table under the board. Records are saved on this device only.
  • Pausing stops the timer (and hides the grid, so no studying while paused!).
