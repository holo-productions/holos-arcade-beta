How to Play

  • Jump by tapping anywhere on the snowy trail, clicking, or pressing Space. The reindeer runs on its own — you just time the leaps.
  • Leap the pine logs, snowdrifts and rocks on the trail. The run gets faster the further you dash.
  • Grab stars (+10), gift boxes (+25) and stockings hung on the trees (+15) — they float at jump height.
  • Cookies & milk are rare — grab a plate to win back one heart (or +25 if your hearts are already full).
  • You have 3 hearts. Hitting an obstacle costs one heart, and the reindeer flashes for about 1.5 seconds while it can't be hurt again. Lose all 3 and the run is over.
  • Score = distance (1 point per 10 metres) + everything you collect. Best score and best distance are saved on this device.
  • Practice mode: hearts still apply, but no score and no best are saved — scores don't count.
  • Wave to the elf as you pass, mind the North Pole signposts, and don't keep Santa waiting — the big night is coming.
  • Press P to pause.
