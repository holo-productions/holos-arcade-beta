❓ How to Play

Welcome to the busiest little kitchen in town! Here's the flow:

1
Read Tickets

Each order shows a dish, price, and patience bar — empty means walk-out!

2
Grab Ingredients

Tap shelf ingredients to build a dish — match the Recipe Book exactly.

3
Chop It

Salads, soups and stir-fries need chopping — smash Chop until done!

4
Cook It

Send it to the stove and plate when cooked — too long and it burns!

5
Plate & Serve

Hit Plate It, then Serve — fast service means bigger tips and combos!

6
Hit Goal

Hit the day goal before the shift timer runs out to unlock the next day.

Picked the wrong ingredient? Hit 🗑️ Trash and start the dish over. No shame — every chef does it.

📜 Kitchen Rush Rules

🎯 The Goal

Earn the day's goal amount before the shift timer hits zero. Clear the goal to advance to the next day — each new day raises the goal by $20 and customers get a little less patient.

💰 Scoring

  • Dish price — paid in full for every correct serve.
  • Tip — up to +50% of the dish price, based on how much patience is left when you serve. Faster = richer.
  • Combo bonus — serve dishes back-to-back with over 40% patience left to build a streak. Streak of 2 pays +$2, streak of 3 or more pays +$5 per serve.
  • Walk-outs — a customer who runs out of patience leaves, costs you $5, and breaks your combo streak.
  • Burnt food — cooked food left on the pass too long burns and must be trashed. No points, just tears.

🧪 Practice Mode

Practice gives customers 60% more patience and there's no failing — but scores in Practice are never saved to your bests. It's for learning recipes and trying wild strategies.

📏 House Rules

  • One dish on the counter at a time. Finish it or trash it.
  • Ingredients must match a recipe exactly — no freestyle fusion, sorry.
  • All money is imaginary in-game coins. No real-money anything, ever.
