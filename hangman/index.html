Hangman — Full Rules

The complete rulebook: how a round is won and lost, exactly how scoring works, and every special rule.

Win and loss conditions

  • You win the round the moment every letter of the secret word is revealed — by guessing, by a correct Solve, or by Reveal a letter completing the word.
  • You lose the round when your wrong guesses use up all your misses for the difficulty level (see below). The full word and its clue are then shown.
  • Give up ends the round immediately and counts as a loss.
  • New word (skip) during an unfinished round counts that round as played, ends your current win streak, and scores 0 for it.
  • There is no timer. Rounds are untimed, and your score carries from round to round until you change the word list or difficulty, or reset your stats.

Guessing rules

  • Each letter can be guessed only once. A correct guess reveals that letter in every position it appears — repeats are always free.
  • A wrong letter costs exactly 1 miss. Letters are never case-sensitive, and you can guess by tapping the on-screen keyboard or typing on a real keyboard.
  • Spaces in a short phrase (Pass & Play, or “Pizza Party” in Fun & Fantasy) are shown from the start and never need guessing. Only A–Z letters count.
  • Solve it: typing the exact whole word or phrase wins instantly. A wrong solve costs 1 miss — and if that was your last miss, the round is lost.

Special rules — help actions

  • Show clue is always free and can be used once per round. Every word-list word has its own clue; a Pass & Play word uses the clue its setter typed (or a default note if they left it blank).
  • Reveal a letter reveals one randomly chosen still-hidden letter and costs 1 miss. It is disabled when you have only 1 miss left — it can never spend your last miss — but if the revealed letter completes the word, you still win the round.
  • The figure on the gallows fills in proportionally: the same six body parts are spread across however many misses your difficulty allows, so on Chill (9 misses) some misses add no new part.

Scoring — exact formula

  • Points are scored only for a win. A loss, a give-up, and a skip all score 0.
  • Base = (10 × number of letters in the word) + (15 × misses you had left when you won).
  • Round points = round(Base × difficulty multiplier) + streak bonus.
  • Streak bonus = 25 × your win streak before this round (so your first win in a row adds 0, the second consecutive win adds 25, the third adds 50, and so on).
  • Worked example: winning “ELEPHANT” (8 letters) on Classic with 3 misses left, on your second win in a row: (80 + 45) × 1.5 = 188, plus 25 streak bonus = 213 points.
  • Winning by Reveal a letter scores the same as winning by guessing — the miss it cost is already reflected in your misses left.

Word lists

There are six lists — Animals, Food & Drink, Places & Travel, Nature & Weather, Sports & Hobbies, and Fun & Fantasy — plus Mixed bag, which draws from all of them. The computer avoids repeating any of your last six words. Every list word is a single word except “Pizza Party” in Fun & Fantasy.

Pass & Play rules

  • The setter types a word or short phrase of at least 3 letters (letters and spaces only, up to 24 characters) and an optional clue. The word is entered in a masked field and is never displayed while typing.
  • All guessing, miss, help-action and scoring rules above apply exactly the same. The round is labelled “Pass & Play” instead of a word list.
  • After a Pass & Play round, “Next word” returns to the setter form so a new word can be set.

Practice rounds

  • Turn Practice round on from the controls above the board. A “Practice” badge shows on the board and in the score bar while it's on.
  • Practice rounds play exactly like scored rounds — same word lists, difficulty, misses, clue, Reveal a letter and Solve rules — except no points are scored for a win.
  • A practice win, loss, give-up or skip does not count in Words played, does not change your win streak or best streak, and does not change your score or best score.
  • Turning Practice on or off starts a fresh word. Turning it on part-way through a scored round counts that unfinished round as a skip, exactly like tapping New word. Turning it off returns you to scored play with your previous score and streak intact.
  • Practice can be combined with Pass & Play: the practice setting applies to the friend-set word too.

Records

Words played, wins (shown as win rate), best win streak and best total score are saved on this device only. Reset stats clears those records plus your current score and streak; it does not change the round in progress.
