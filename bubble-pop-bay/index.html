How to play

1
Aim and shoot

Move to aim, tap or click to shoot a bubble.

2
Match 3+

Match 3 or more of the same colour to pop them.

3
Drop for bonus

Bubbles cut off from the top fall for bonus points.

4
Clear to win

Clear the whole board to win.

5
Watch the line

If bubbles reach the bottom line, it's over.

Rules

  • Pop groups of 3+ same-colour bubbles.
  • Every 5 shots, the ceiling drops one row (Hard: every 3).
  • Clear all bubbles to win the level.
  • Practice mode never saves to your best.
