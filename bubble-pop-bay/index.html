How to play

Move to aim, tap or click to shoot a bubble. Match 3 or more of the same colour to pop them. Bubbles that lose their connection to the top fall for bonus points. Clear the board to win — if bubbles reach the bottom line, it's over.

Rules

  • Pop groups of 3+ same-colour bubbles.
  • Every 5 shots, the ceiling drops one row (Hard: every 3).
  • Clear all bubbles to win the level.
  • Practice mode never saves to your best.
