Rules

Scoring

  • Distance: 10 points per 10 metres travelled.
  • Coins: 50 points each.
  • Close call: 25 points for passing a vehicle in an adjacent lane without contact (once per vehicle).
  • Your score only counts in Arcade mode. Practice mode never saves a best score.

Levels & speed

  • A new level starts every 400 m. Each level raises your speed and the traffic density.
  • Displayed speed is flavour — what matters is how fast the road moves. On Hard it moves very fast.

Difficulty

  • Easy: slower road, sparse traffic, gentler level-ups.
  • Normal: the intended ride.
  • Hard: faster from the start, tighter gaps, quicker level-ups in feel.
  • Your difficulty choice is remembered for this game on this device.

Crashing

  • Any contact with a car or truck ends the run immediately. There are no extra lives — one bike, one rider, one very dramatic slide.
  • Best scores are stored per difficulty on this device only.

Fair play

  • No real-money gambling, no prizes — just points, coins, and bragging rights.
  • All vehicles, art, and sounds are original and generated in your browser.
