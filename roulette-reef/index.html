How to play

Play money only — no real money, no prizes. Pick a chip size, choose Red, Black, Even, Odd or a single number, then tap Spin. If the wheel lands on your pick, you win virtual coins. Run low? Top up for free — it's all pretend!

Difficulty changes your starting coins: Easy 1500, Normal 1000, Hard 500.

Rules

  • Play money only — no real money, no prizes. Coins have no cash value.
  • Red/Black/Even/Odd pay 1:1 (bet back + equal winnings).
  • Single number pays 35:1.
  • Zero (green) loses for Red/Black/Even/Odd bets.
  • One bet per spin. Best = your highest coin balance.
  • Practice mode never saves to your best.
