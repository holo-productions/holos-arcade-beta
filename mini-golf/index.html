Rules

Mini Golf is a 9-hole putting game. Your goal is to get the ball into the cup in as few putts as possible.

How scoring works

  • Every release counts as 1 putt, even a tiny tap.
  • Each hole has a par — the expected number of putts. Finish under par and you're flying.
  • Your round total is all 9 holes added together. Course par is 29.
  • Your best total for each difficulty is saved on this device.

Course hazards

  • Walls & bumpers — the ball bounces off them. Use the bounce on purpose.
  • Sand (pale patches) — slows the ball right down. Avoid it or hit through it harder.
  • Water (blue) — splash! Ball returns to where you last putted from, plus +1 penalty putt.
  • Moving bumpers (holes 7 & 9) — they slide back and forth. Time your putt.
  • The cup — roll in gently. Too fast and the ball will lip out and roll past.

Practice round

  • Tap 🎯 Practice round at the top to start a practice round. Turning it on or off starts a fresh round.
  • No score: practice putts are shown so you can learn, but they are not saved, do not change your best total, and do not count as a finished round.
  • Hints on: a gold guide line on the course points toward the cup, and the hint box under the course gives a suggested direction and power for your next putt, plus a tip for that hole.
  • No pressure: there is no timer, water returns your ball with no +1 penalty, and you can use Retry last shot or Back to tee as often as you like.
  • Turn practice off any time — your next round counts and can set a new best.

Difficulty

  • Easy: bigger cup, grippier greens, slower moving bumpers.
  • Normal: standard cup and speed.
  • Hard: smaller cup, slicker greens, faster bumpers — for bragging rights.

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: press on or near the ball, drag away from your target to aim (like a slingshot), release to putt.
  • You can only putt when the ball has fully stopped.
  • Use Restart hole any time — it clears that hole's putts.
