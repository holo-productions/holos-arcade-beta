📐 Rules of the Lab

Simple, fair, and kind — for players and parents.

How scoring works

  • Each round is 10 questions.
  • 1 star for every correct answer in Scored mode.
  • +3 bonus stars for 9 or 10 correct in one round.
  • Stars are saved on this device only — no account, no cloud.
  • Practice Round: a shorter 5-question warm-up with no score pressure — scores & stars are not saved, hints are on (shown automatically), it is untimed, and you can keep trying each question until you get it right before moving on. Clearly marked 🌿 PRACTICE everywhere it appears.

The maths — quick reference

  • Right angle = 90° • Straight line = 180° • Full turn = 360°
  • Triangle angles add to 180° • Quadrilateral to 360°
  • Rectangle: Area = length × width • Perimeter = 2 × (length + width)
  • Triangle: Area = ½ × base × height
  • Circle: Circumference = 2πr • Area = πr² (use π ≈ 3.14)

Playing fair & safe

  • No timers — take as long as you need.
  • No chat, no usernames, no personal information is asked for.
  • No ads and no links to other websites.
  • Guessing is okay! Every answer explains why.
  • Grown-ups: use Parent Feedback to tell us what helped or what was confusing. Nothing is sent automatically — you copy it yourself.

For parents & teachers

  • Grades 4–6: shape properties, angle types, rectangle area/perimeter, symmetry lines.
  • Grades 7–9: angle rules, circles, composite area, transformations.
  • Grades 10–12: Pythagoras, trigonometry (sin, cos, tan — finding sides and angles), volume & surface area of prisms and cylinders, circle theorems, sectors, similarity, coordinates (midpoint, slope, quadrants).
